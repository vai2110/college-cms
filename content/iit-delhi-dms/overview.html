College Overview

About IIT Delhi

DMS IIT Delhi places management education inside IIT Delhi's technology, engineering and innovation ecosystem. Its full-time MBA is particularly relevant where business decisions intersect with analytics, technology, operations and industrial systems.

Why students shortlist it

Strong fit for students who want a low-tuition top-ranked MBA with an IIT ecosystem and interests in analytics, consulting, operations, technology or general management.

CAT cutoff: what the number really means

DMS does not present a simple CAT percentile cutoff as the whole decision. For 2026–28, candidates qualifying through CAT 2025 were shortlisted for interview, and the institute published a separate shortlisting criterion. Treat this as the eligibility/shortlisting threshold, not as a guaranteed call score.

Admission 2026

CAT 2025 + DMS shortlisting + PI. DMS IIT Delhi's 2026 admission page states that CAT 2025 applicants are shortlisted and invited for interview. It also states ₹12 lakh tuition for the entire full-time MBA for Indian citizens.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee of DMS IIT Delhi MBA in 2026?

The two-year full-time MBA tuition is ₹12 lakh for Indian citizens, payable in four semester instalments. The 2025–27 fee schedule also shows ₹3 lakh tuition per year plus ₹22,400 other charges and ₹8,600 institute residence fee for the applicable batch. So the main attraction is the relatively low tuition, but students should still budget for living, mess and personal expenses.

What CAT percentile is required for DMS IIT Delhi MBA?

DMS does not treat one CAT percentile as the entire admission decision. Candidates qualifying through CAT 2025 are shortlisted and then interviewed using the institute's published shortlisting criteria. A student should therefore distinguish the minimum eligibility/shortlisting rule from a 'safe percentile'; the latter is not officially guaranteed.

Is DMS IIT Delhi better than IIMs?

It can be better for a student who values IIT Delhi's technology ecosystem and a much lower published tuition. It is not automatically better for someone prioritising the broader alumni network and placement scale of older IIMs. Compare the role mix, median/average salary, fees and your target function before deciding.

What is the average package at DMS IIT Delhi?

Compare average salary, median salary and role mix rather than focusing only on the highest package. The 2023–25 report provides a useful benchmark; the key student question is whether the role mix matches your target in consulting, analytics, IT, operations or general management.

Does DMS IIT Delhi have good ROI?

On tuition alone, DMS has a strong ROI argument because ₹12 lakh covers the full two-year MBA tuition. ROI becomes less obvious if your target role is highly compensation-sensitive and you compare it with schools that have materially higher placement outcomes. Your decision should therefore use total cost, median salary and role quality together.

Is MBA Telecom at IIT Delhi worth choosing?

It makes sense mainly for candidates interested in telecom, digital infrastructure and technology management. If your goal is a generic consulting or finance career, the flagship MBA is the more natural comparison. DMS lists MBA Telecom as a separate programme with the same ₹12 lakh tuition reference.

What kind of student should choose DMS IIT Delhi?

Choose it if you want an IIT ecosystem, relatively low tuition and a technology/analytics/operations-friendly management environment. Think twice if your only goal is the strongest possible salary outcome or the largest traditional B-school alumni network.
