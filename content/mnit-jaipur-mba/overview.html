MNIT Jaipur MBA: Overview

The Department of Management Studies (DMS), Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur offers a two-year full-time MBA. The programme is designed around management courses, seminars, projects and practical exposure, with teaching that uses case studies, presentations, assignments, guest lectures, workshops, field visits and internships.

DMS was established in 1996 as a Centre of Management Studies and Industrial Collaboration and was upgraded to a full-fledged academic department in 2004. The department positions the MBA around techno-managerial capability, leadership and industry interaction.

What makes MNIT Jaipur MBA different?

Students get a management programme inside a centrally funded technical institute ecosystem. The department also supports exposure through case competitions, business quizzes, entrepreneurship sessions, panel discussions, workshops, live projects and industry-facing activities.

2025 NIRF Management rank: MNIT Jaipur was ranked #62 in India in the Management category in the NIRF 2025 ranking.

Eligibility for MNIT Jaipur MBA

The 2026-27 eligibility requires a valid three-year or four-year bachelor's degree from a recognized institute/university. The general minimum is CGPA 6.5/10 or 60% where CGPA is not awarded; for SC/ST candidates, the minimum is CGPA 6.0/10 or 55% where CGPA is not awarded. Final-year candidates can apply subject to the conditions in the admission brochure.

General eligibility

3/4-year bachelor's degree + minimum 6.5 CGPA or 60% equivalent where CGPA is not awarded.

SC/ST relaxation

Minimum 6.0 CGPA or 55% equivalent where CGPA is not awarded.

Management test requirement

A valid score in CAT, GMAT, XAT, CMAT or MAT is required for participation in the selection process, subject to the year's notified criteria.

For 2026-27, MNIT DMS uses a 100-point comprehensive score. Test performance and the Personal Interview each carry up to 40 points, while academic qualification carries 15 and work experience carries 5.

MNIT Jaipur Campus & Student Life

DMS is part of MNIT Jaipur's larger residential technical-institute campus. Students can participate in management events and also interact with a wider engineering, science and technology ecosystem.

  • Case study competitions, business quizzes and entrepreneurship activities.
  • Panel discussions, workshops, guest lectures and conferences.
  • Live projects, field visits and internships for practical exposure.
  • International collaborations are encouraged by the department.
  • Hostel accommodation is subject to availability and allotment.
Student-life reality check

MNIT Jaipur offers the wider NIT campus ecosystem rather than a standalone private B-school campus. Students comparing MBA colleges should consider whether they value a technical-institute environment, public-institute ecosystem and comparatively compact MBA cohort.

Is MNIT Jaipur MBA Worth Considering?

Good fit if you want

A public technical-institute environment, a small MBA cohort, multiple management-test routes, Business Analytics/Finance/Operations options and a selection process that rewards the overall profile.

Check before applying

Placement averages are below the headline highest package, hostel accommodation is subject to allotment, and the latest fee structure should be used for your cost comparison.

MNIT Jaipur vs a typical private B-school

The strongest differentiator is the institute ecosystem: MNIT is an NIT with a management department, while many private B-schools operate as dedicated management institutions. For students who value technical-campus exposure and a government-funded institute environment, this can be meaningful.

MNIT Jaipur MBA FAQs

What is the duration of the MBA at MNIT Jaipur?

The MBA is a two-year full-time programme.

Which entrance exams are accepted for MNIT Jaipur MBA?

For the 2026-27 selection process, MNIT lists CAT, GMAT, XAT, CMAT and MAT as acceptable management admission tests, subject to the notified criteria.

What is the minimum graduation eligibility for MNIT Jaipur MBA?

The general minimum is 6.5 CGPA on a 10-point scale or 60% where CGPA is not awarded; SC/ST candidates have a relaxation to 6.0 CGPA or 55% where CGPA is not awarded.

What is the CAT cutoff for MNIT Jaipur MBA?

MNIT does not publish one fixed final-admission CAT percentile as a guaranteed cutoff. Selection uses the comprehensive score.

What are the latest MNIT Jaipur MBA fees?

For 2026-27, the official four-semester institute fee works out to approximately ₹3.27 lakh for GEN/OBC and ₹1.27 lakh for SC/ST, including listed one-time charges, annual insurance and the additional ₹1,000 alumni membership fee. Hostel and mess charges are separate.

What specializations are offered at MNIT Jaipur?

The DMS lists Marketing, Finance, Operations Management, Human Resource Management, Business Analytics, and General Management & Strategy, subject to a minimum number of students opting for a specialization.

What is the latest MNIT Jaipur MBA placement package?

For the 2023-25 batch, the official 2026-27 brochure reports ₹16.4 LPA highest, ₹8 LPA average and ₹7.5 LPA median.

How much is hostel and mess cost at MNIT Jaipur?

The 2026-27 hostel fee notice lists ₹25,000 mess advance, ₹12,000 refundable caution money, ₹3,000 one-time hostel/mess admission fee and ₹12,000 room rent plus light and water charges for a new entrant, making the initial amount ₹52,000. Subsequent semester hostel and mess advance is ₹37,000 based on the listed recurring charges.
