MNIT Jaipur MBA: Overview

The Department of Management Studies (DMS), Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur offers a two-year full-time MBA. The programme is designed around management courses, seminars, projects and practical exposure, with teaching that uses case studies, presentations, assignments, guest lectures, workshops, field visits and internships.

DMS was established in 1996 as a Centre of Management Studies and Industrial Collaboration and was upgraded to a full-fledged academic department in 2004. The department positions the MBA around techno-managerial capability, leadership and industry interaction.

What makes MNIT Jaipur MBA different?

Students get a management programme inside a centrally funded technical institute ecosystem. The department also supports exposure through case competitions, business quizzes, entrepreneurship sessions, panel discussions, workshops, live projects and industry-facing activities.

Eligibility for MNIT Jaipur MBA

The 2026-27 eligibility requires a valid three-year or four-year bachelor's degree from a recognized institute/university. The general minimum is CGPA 6.5/10 or 60% where CGPA is not awarded; for SC/ST candidates, the minimum is CGPA 6.0/10 or 55% where CGPA is not awarded. Final-year candidates can apply if they meet the specified conditions for submitting proof later. citeturn1search0

MNIT Jaipur Campus & Student Life

DMS is part of MNIT Jaipur's larger residential technical-institute campus. Students can participate in management events and also interact with a wider engineering, science and technology ecosystem.

  • Case study competitions, business quizzes and entrepreneurship activities.
  • Panel discussions, workshops, guest lectures and conferences.
  • Live projects, field visits and internships for practical exposure.
  • International collaborations are encouraged by the department.
  • Limited hostel accommodation is available on a first-come-first-served basis, according to the DMS FAQ.
Student-life reality check

MNIT Jaipur offers the wider NIT campus ecosystem rather than a standalone private B-school campus. Students comparing MBA colleges should consider whether they value a technical-institute environment, public-institute ecosystem and comparatively compact MBA cohort.

Is MNIT Jaipur MBA Worth Considering?

Good fit if you want

A public technical-institute environment, a small MBA cohort, multiple management-test routes, Business Analytics/Finance/Operations options and a selection process that rewards the overall profile.

Check before applying

Placement averages are below the headline highest package, hostel accommodation is limited, and the official fee structure should be checked for the exact current payable amount before making a cost comparison.

MNIT Jaipur vs a typical private B-school

The strongest differentiator is the institute ecosystem: MNIT is an NIT with a management department, while many private B-schools operate as dedicated management institutions. For students who value technical-campus exposure and a government-funded institute environment, this can be meaningful.
