SIES College of Management Studies Overview

SIES College of Management Studies (SIESCOMS) is located at Plot 1E, Sector V, Nerul, Navi Mumbai. The institute was established in 1995 under the South Indian Education Society and offers MMS, MCA and PhD programmes. Its flagship two-year full-time MMS is affiliated to the University of Mumbai and approved by AICTE. SIESCOMS also states that its two-year autonomous flagship management programme is accredited by ACBSP, while the institute has NAAC and NBA accreditation/status on its official disclosures.

What is SIESCOMS best known for? The MMS programme combines University of Mumbai-aligned management education with specialisations in Finance, Marketing, Operations, Systems and HR, along with corporate interaction, concurrent projects and leadership/personality-development inputs.

MMS Programme

2-Year Full-Time MMS

The MMS is a two-year full-time management programme affiliated to the University of Mumbai and approved by AICTE. SIESCOMS supplements the prescribed syllabus with industry-oriented inputs, corporate interaction, concurrent projects and leadership development.

Specialisations

Students can pursue functional specialisations in Finance, Marketing, Operations, Systems and Human Resources. The programme also emphasises analytical decision-making, communication, teamwork and experiential learning.

Eligibility: Admission eligibility follows Maharashtra State CET Cell/DTE rules for the MMS route. Students should verify the applicable candidature type, qualifying-degree requirement and entrance-score conditions in the current CAP/institute admission notice before applying.

Track SIESCOMS institute-level and ACAP vacancies.
If seats remain after CAP, SIESCOMS publishes institute-level/against-CAP procedures and schedules. These are merit-based procedures subject to the applicable rules.
Complete institute reporting.

Campus & Student Life

SIESCOMS is located in Nerul, Navi Mumbai, making it relevant for students who want access to the Mumbai–Navi Mumbai corporate ecosystem without studying in a fully residential campus. The institute highlights corporate interaction, concurrent projects, personality development, leadership programmes, yoga and meditation, research activity and student conferences/events.

Who may prefer SIESCOMS? Students seeking a Mumbai-region MMS with a relatively moderate published academic fee, University of Mumbai affiliation, multiple functional specialisations and access to the wider Navi Mumbai/Mumbai employment market. Students looking specifically for a residential MBA experience should compare the campus model and living costs with residential B-schools.

FAQs

Is SIES College of Management Studies good for MMS?
SIESCOMS is an established Navi Mumbai management institute with a two-year full-time MMS, multiple functional specialisations and a long-standing placement ecosystem. The latest official 2023–25 MMS figures show ₹8.76 LPA average salary and ₹17 LPA highest salary. Whether it is a good choice depends on your CET percentile, fee budget, preferred specialisation and Mumbai/Navi Mumbai career preference.
What is the MMS fee at SIESCOMS?
The published MMS admission notice lists ₹2.15 lakh tuition plus ₹32,248 development fee, or ₹2.48 lakh in total, with University fees potentially additional. SIESCOMS has a separate 2026–27 fee page, so students joining the 2026–28 batch should confirm the current payable amount before payment.
What entrance exam is accepted for SIESCOMS MMS?
MMS admissions are governed by Maharashtra State CET Cell/DTE rules and the CAP process. Candidates should check the current year's CET Cell brochure for the complete accepted-exam and candidature rules rather than relying on older admission articles.
Does SIESCOMS have institute-level or ACAP admission?
Yes. SIESCOMS publishes institute-level and Against CAP/ACAP vacancy procedures when seats remain after CAP. These are not the same as guaranteed direct admission; candidates must follow the published merit, eligibility, counselling and reporting procedure.
What specialisations are offered in SIESCOMS MMS?
The official MMS programme page lists Finance, Marketing, Operations, Systems and Human Resources. The institute also supplements the curriculum with industry inputs, concurrent projects and leadership/personality-development activities.
What is the average package at SIESCOMS?
For the 2023–25 MMS placement season, SIESCOMS reports an average salary of ₹8.76 LPA and highest salary of ₹17 LPA. The institute also has older placement reports with different figures, so students should compare like-for-like batches rather than treating the highest package as a typical outcome.
Is SIESCOMS autonomous?
Yes. SIESCOMS currently announces that UGC has granted it autonomous status. Its MMS remains described by the institute as a two-year full-time management programme affiliated to the University of Mumbai and approved by AICTE; students should check the current academic regulations for the exact implementation of autonomy.
Is SIESCOMS Navi Mumbai suitable for MBA aspirants from outside Maharashtra?
It can be, but the admission route and candidature category matter. Out-of-Maharashtra candidates should carefully check the current Maharashtra CET Cell rules, CAP eligibility and the institute-level/ACAP notices applicable to their candidature before deciding. The Mumbai/Navi Mumbai location can be attractive for students targeting the regional corporate market.
