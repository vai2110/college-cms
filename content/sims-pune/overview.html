SIMS Pune Overview

Symbiosis Institute of Management Studies (SIMS) was established in 1993 and is a constituent of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). Its flagship full-time MBA has a distinctive defence-linked admission structure: SIMS describes the programme as primarily for Defence Personnel and their Dependents, while 10% of seats are available in the Open Category to civilian candidates on merit. The campus is at Range Hills Road, Khadki, Pune. citeturn1search3turn2search1

Important for applicants: SIMS is not a conventional open-category MBA where most seats are available to all applicants. The defence category and its eligibility documentation materially affect the applicant pool, while civilian candidates compete for the 10% Open Category. citeturn2search1

SIMS also runs a 24-month MBA (Executive) for working professionals. The current Executive programme offers Marketing, Finance, Supply Chain Management & Operations, Human Resource Management and IT & Analytics specialisations and uses Personal Interaction rather than an entrance examination. citeturn2search0turn2search2

SIMS Pune Admission Process

Register for SNAP.
For the full-time MBA, candidates desirous of admission to SIMS must appear for SNAP, the common Symbiosis postgraduate entrance test. citeturn2search1
Register separately for SIMS.
SNAP registration does not replace SIMS's own programme registration. SIMS publishes a separate registration deadline and requires the candidate to complete its application. citeturn2search1
Meet the applicable category eligibility.
The full-time MBA has a defence-focused admission structure. Defence applicants need the documents specified by SIMS for the applicable service/dependency category. Open Category candidates compete for 10% seats on merit. citeturn2search1
Attend GE-PI if shortlisted.
SIMS's 2027-29 process lists Group Exercise and Personal Interaction after SNAP-based shortlisting. citeturn2search1
Check merit list and complete fee payment.
Admission is merit-based under SIU's prescribed process. SIMS/SIU explicitly states that it does not charge capitation fee and that agents cannot guarantee admission. citeturn2search1turn1search7
Other admission modes: For the regular MBA, SIMS does not publish a management/direct-admission route outside the prescribed process. It separately identifies a few seats for International/NRI candidates routed through the Symbiosis Centre for International Education. Serving Defence officers on approved study leave can also apply through the specific study-leave process described by SIMS. citeturn2search1

Executive MBA admission

The Executive MBA is different: candidates need a bachelor's degree with 50% marks and at least one year of full-time work experience after graduation. The published selection procedure is Personal Interaction, with no entrance exam listed for the 2026-28 batch. citeturn2search2turn2search5

SIMS Pune Fees & Fee Bifurcation

For the full-time MBA, fee differences between Defence and Open Category candidates are substantial. Current 2026-28 public fee summaries report approximately ₹16.65 lakh for Defence Category and ₹23.20 lakh for Open Category for the two-year programme. Because SIMS's public admission page does not expose the full current fee table in the indexed text, students should confirm the exact installment-wise academic, hostel, mess and deposit amounts in the latest SIMS fee notice before payment. citeturn1search2

Campus & Student Life

SIMS is located on Range Hills Road in Khadki, Pune. The institute describes its campus as residential and states that it provides accommodation to more than 600 female and male students. Its location also places students within Pune's established education and business ecosystem. citeturn1search3turn1search7

SIMS Pune FAQs

Is SIMS Pune a good option for civilian students?
Yes, but civilian applicants should understand the seat structure before applying. SIMS states that 10% of seats in the flagship MBA are available in the Open Category to civilian candidates, and selection is on merit. This is a much smaller pool than the defence-focused category, so a civilian applicant should evaluate SIMS with the Open Category competition and fee level in mind. citeturn2search1
Is SNAP compulsory for SIMS Pune MBA admission?
Yes. SIMS's regular MBA admission procedure states that each student seeking admission must appear for SNAP. In addition, the candidate must separately register with SIMS; taking SNAP alone does not complete the institute application. citeturn2search1
What is the SIMS Pune MBA admission process for 2027-29?
For the 2027-29 batch, the sequence is SNAP registration and test, separate SIMS registration, SNAP result, SIMS shortlisting, GE-PI, merit list and fee payment. SIMS has scheduled GE-PI for January 29–31 and February 6–7, 2027, with the first merit-list window from February 22–25 and programme commencement on June 7, 2027. citeturn2search1
Can a non-defence student get admission to SIMS Pune?
Yes. SIMS explicitly provides an Open Category for civilian candidates, accounting for 10% of seats in the regular MBA. These candidates are selected on merit through the prescribed SIMS/SIU admission process; there is no published direct or guaranteed management-admission route. citeturn2search1
What is the SIMS Pune MBA fee for Defence and Open Category students?
Public 2026-28 placement/college summaries report approximately ₹16.65 lakh for Defence Category and ₹23.20 lakh for Open Category. The exact installment-wise academic, hostel, mess and deposit amounts should be checked against the latest SIMS fee notice before payment because fee schedules can be revised. citeturn1search2
What is the highest package at SIMS Pune?
For the 2025 MBA placement cycle, the reported highest package was ₹21 LPA. The same cycle reported average packages of ₹17.5 LPA for the top 10%, ₹15.5 LPA for the top 25% and ₹13.5 LPA for the top 50%. The 2024 highest package was ₹24 LPA, so the highest offer declined in 2025; students should therefore avoid treating the highest package alone as the expected salary. citeturn1search0turn1search2
Does SIMS Pune have an Executive MBA without an entrance exam?
Yes. SIMS's Executive MBA is a separate 24-month programme for working professionals. The published 2026-28 eligibility requires graduation with 50% marks and at least one year of work experience after graduation, while the selection procedure is Personal Interaction. SIMS's FAQ also states that there is no entrance exam for this programme. citeturn2search2turn2search5
Is SIMS Pune admission possible through management quota or donation?
SIMS/SIU does not publish a management-quota or donation-based admission route for its regular MBA. The institute states that SIU admissions are merit-based and that the university does not charge capitation fee. It also warns applicants against agents or organisations claiming to guarantee admission. citeturn2search1turn1search7
