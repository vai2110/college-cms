SIMS Pune Overview

SIMS Pune was established in 1993 and is a constituent of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). Its flagship full-time MBA is designed around a mix of Defence personnel/dependents and civilian students. The institute describes the campus as a residential management institute at Range Hills Road, Khadki, Pune. citeturn0search3

The biggest point students should understand: SIMS is not a conventional open-category B-school. The published admission structure reserves 80% of seats for Defence personnel and their dependents and 10% for civilian candidates in the Open Category. citeturn0search0

SIMS Pune Cutoff: What Students Should Know

SIMS does not publish a guaranteed future SNAP cutoff on its admission page. A single “official cutoff percentile” should therefore not be presented as a fixed admission threshold.

How to use cutoff information: treat SNAP percentile as the screening signal, then consider category, applicant pool, GE-PI performance and the final merit process. Coaching-site cutoff figures should be treated as historical or expected estimates unless SIMS officially publishes them. citeturn0search0

SIMS Pune Fees

SIMS publishes programme fees through its official admission information. Students should calculate the complete two-year cost rather than comparing tuition alone, because academic fees, hostel, mess, deposits and other charges can all affect the final budget.

Budgeting advice: verify the exact Indian-student fee notice for your batch before payment. For international/NRI/OCI applicants, SCIE publishes separate MBA fee schedules. citeturn0search0turn0search1

Campus, Student Life & Who Should Choose SIMS

SIMS is located in Khadki, Pune, and describes itself as a residential institute with accommodation for more than 600 students. Its Defence-civilian mix creates a different peer environment from a conventional private B-school. citeturn0search3

Is SIMS Pune good for an MBA?
SIMS can be a relevant MBA option for students who fit its category structure and want a Pune-based management programme with Marketing, HR, Finance, Operations & Supply Chain Management and Data Analytics for Business specialisations. Its official placement report shows roles across BFSI, consulting, IT/ITES, FMCG, manufacturing and other sectors. What is the SNAP cutoff for SIMS Pune?
SIMS does not publish a guaranteed future SNAP cutoff on its admission page. Third-party historical or expected numbers should therefore be treated as estimates. SNAP is mandatory, followed by the SIMS shortlist and GE-PI/merit process. citeturn0search0
Can a civilian get admission to SIMS Pune?
Yes. SIMS officially states that 10% of seats are in the Open Category for civilian candidates, while 80% are for Defence personnel and their dependents. Civilian applicants should evaluate their chances within the Open Category rather than comparing SIMS with an MBA college where almost all seats are open. citeturn0search0
Is SNAP compulsory for SIMS Pune MBA?
Yes. SIMS states that every student seeking MBA admission must appear for SNAP. You must also complete the separate SIMS registration within its deadline. SNAP participation alone does not complete the application. citeturn0search0
