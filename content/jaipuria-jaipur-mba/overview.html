Jaipuria Jaipur MBA: Overview

Jaipuria Institute of Management, Jaipur offers a two-year full-time PGDM programme that is AIU-recognised as equivalent to an MBA. The Jaipur campus is part of the Jaipuria group, which operates campuses in Jaipur, Noida, Lucknow and Indore.

The programme combines classroom learning with case analysis, projects, industry interaction, internships and professional-development activities. Students can apply through the common Jaipuria admission process and indicate their preferred campus and programme.

Why consider Jaipuria Jaipur?

The main attraction is the combination of a recognised PGDM, a relatively moderate fee compared with many private B-schools, multiple management specialisations and an active recruiter ecosystem. Students should still compare campus-specific outcomes, fees and their own career target before accepting an offer.

Jaipuria Jaipur MBA Eligibility

Applicants must hold a bachelor's degree from a recognised Indian university with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. Final-year graduation students can also apply subject to the institute's completion and document-submission conditions.

Academic requirement

Minimum 50% in a recognised bachelor's degree or equivalent CGPA.

Entrance test

Valid CAT 2026, XAT 2027, CMAT, MAT or GMAT score accepted for the selection process.

Jaipuria Jaipur MBA Scholarships

Jaipuria offers merit-based scholarships linked to national management aptitude-test performance. The published scholarship structure for the 2027-29 batch shows PGDM scholarships of ₹5 lakh for 90% and above, ₹3.5 lakh for 80% and above, ₹2.5 lakh for 70% and above and ₹1.5 lakh for 60% and above. The applicable scholarship for a student's batch should be checked in the current offer policy.

Jaipuria Jaipur MBA FAQs

Is Jaipuria Jaipur PGDM equivalent to an MBA?

Yes. Jaipuria states that its PGDM is AIU-recognised as equivalent to an MBA.

What is the Jaipuria Jaipur PGDM fee for 2026-28?

The published 2026-28 Jaipur campus fee is ₹14.75 lakh for two years, plus the refundable ₹15,000 security deposit and applicable taxes/other charges.

Which entrance exams are accepted?

Jaipuria's 2026-27 admission information lists CAT 2026, XAT 2027, CMAT, MAT and GMAT.

What is the Jaipuria Jaipur selection weightage?

The published framework gives 45% to the aptitude test, 10% to case analysis, 25% to PI, 15% to academics, 3% to work experience/sports/extracurriculars and 2% to diversity.

What is the latest highest package at Jaipuria?

The 2024-26 combined placement report lists ₹24.11 LPA as the highest CTC across Jaipuria's four campuses.

Is the ₹24.11 LPA placement figure only for Jaipur?

No. The institute explicitly says the published placement statistics are combined across Jaipur, Noida, Lucknow and Indore.

Does Jaipuria Jaipur provide hostel facilities?

Yes. The institute lists AC single and AC double hostel options for the 2026-27 session, with mess charges extra.
