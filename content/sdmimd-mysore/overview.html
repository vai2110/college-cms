SDMIMD Mysore Overview

SDM Institute for Management Development (SDMIMD), Mysuru, was established in 1993 and is promoted by the Shri Dharmasthala Manjunatheshwara Educational Trust. Its flagship offering is a two-year, full-time residential PGDM approved by AICTE, accredited by NBA, recognised as MBA-equivalent by AIU and internationally accredited by EFMD.

What stands out? SDMIMD combines a residential campus, single or dual specialisation choices, 70+ electives, international exchange opportunities and a strong emphasis on business ethics and wellness. It is best evaluated against your target role, placement expectations and total cost rather than only its highest package.

Important Dates: PGDM 2027–29

The current official programme page lists 28 February 2027 as the application deadline. SDMIMD does not present a common counselling-style calendar; shortlisted applicants receive interview and offer instructions.

PGDM Programme & Specialisations

2-Year Residential PGDM

The first year builds a broad management foundation. The second year allows single or dual specialisation and elective choices. A 6–8 week summer internship provides corporate exposure.

Six Specialisation Tracks

Finance, Marketing, Systems, Data Analytics, Human Resources and Operations. SDMIMD's current placement programme page lists 70+ electives across these tracks.

Eligibility: Graduates/final-year students from a recognised full-time degree in any discipline can apply. The current published requirement is at least 50% aggregate in Class 10, Class 12 and graduation. Accepted tests are CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA, MAT and the current admissions page also lists NMAT.

SDMIMD Admission Process

Complete the application.
Apply through the SDMIMD PGDM admissions portal.
Provide an accepted entrance score.
SDMIMD accepts CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA, MAT and NMAT. Applicants may apply while awaiting an exam result and provide the score later.
Academic and profile shortlisting.
Shortlisting considers consistent academics, competitive-exam performance, extracurricular achievements and work experience where applicable.
Personal Interview.
Shortlisted candidates attend the interview at designated Indian centres or online when arranged by the institute.
Offer and confirmation.
Selected students receive the offer and block/confirm the seat through the required fee instalments.
Does SDMIMD use only CAT? No. The institute accepts multiple national management tests, making it possible to apply without CAT if you have another accepted score.

SDMIMD Fees & Scholarships

The official institute page currently publishes ₹14.10 lakh for the 2026–28 two-year PGDM, including tuition and hostel stay. It is payable in six instalments. Food is charged separately at approximately ₹35,000 per year based on distributed mess expenditure. SDMIMD states that the fee is subject to revision for 2027–29.

Scholarships

Current scholarship schemes include an ₹75,000 Entry Merit Scholarship for selected students with at least 75% each in Class 10, Class 12/PUC 2 and degree, and an ₹1,00,000 Entrance Exam Merit Scholarship for candidates with 85 percentile or higher in CAT/XAT/GMAT. SDMIMD also offers a merit tuition-fee waiver for a limited number of economically weaker students and term-wise merit incentives.

Campus & Student Life

SDMIMD is a fully residential campus in Mysuru. The institute highlights modern classrooms, library resources, residential facilities, wellness activities, student clubs and international exchange opportunities. The residential format can be a strong advantage for students who prefer immersive peer learning.

International Exposure

Students can participate in exchange programmes with partner universities. Current eligibility, partner list and any additional costs should be checked for the relevant batch.

Learning Culture

The institute emphasises business ethics, socially relevant projects, case-based learning, internships and student-led activities alongside the academic curriculum.

Who Should Choose SDMIMD?

Good Fit If...

You want a residential PGDM, value a structured academic environment, want flexibility across finance/marketing/HR/operations/systems/data analytics and are comfortable studying in Mysuru.

Reconsider If...

Your priority is only elite consulting/investment-banking recruitment or a metro-city campus. Compare the specific roles offered, median salary, total cost and location against your alternatives.

SDMIMD Mysore FAQs

What is SDMIMD Mysore known for?
SDMIMD is known for its two-year residential PGDM, AICTE approval, NBA accreditation, AIU MBA equivalence, EFMD accreditation and emphasis on ethics, wellness and experiential learning.
What is the SDMIMD PGDM fee?
The published 2026–28 fee is ₹14.10 lakh for two years including tuition and hostel stay. Food is separate at approximately ₹35,000 per year. The institute says the 2027–29 fee may be revised.
Which exams does SDMIMD accept?
The current admission information accepts CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA, MAT and NMAT. Applicants can apply before receiving their score and submit the score later.
What is the eligibility for SDMIMD?
Applicants need a recognised full-time bachelor's degree and at least 50% aggregate in Class 10, Class 12 and graduation under the current published criteria. Final-year candidates can apply.
What is the latest SDMIMD average package?
For the 2024–26 batch, the reported average salary is ₹10.10 LPA and median is ₹9.70 LPA, with a highest package of ₹16.25 LPA.
Is SDMIMD good for finance?
It can be a relevant choice because Finance is one of the specialisation tracks and the latest placement mix is strongly weighted toward BFSI. Students should compare the actual finance roles offered with their career target.
Is SDMIMD worth it compared with other PGDM colleges?
It can be worth considering if you value a residential format, specialisation flexibility, academic credentials and around ₹10 LPA average placement outcomes. Compare alternatives on median salary, total cost, role quality and location before deciding.
