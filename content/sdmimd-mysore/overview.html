SDMIMD Mysore Overview

Shri Dharmasthala Manjunatheshwara Institute for Management Development (SDMIMD) is a management institute in Mysuru, Karnataka. Its flagship programme is a two-year, full-time, residential PGDM approved by AICTE and accredited by NBA. The PGDM has MBA equivalence from AIU and is also accredited by EFMD; the institute states that it was the first institute in India to receive EFMD EPAS accreditation.

What makes SDMIMD different? It is a residential B-school with a relatively focused PGDM model, a six-term structure, single or dual specialisation options and 70+ electives. It can be particularly relevant for students who want a campus-based management experience without restricting themselves to one functional area.

The institute is located at No. 1, Chamundi Hill Road, Siddarthanagar Post, Mysuru. SDMIMD says its student body draws learners from around India and highlights international student-exchange opportunities as part of the PGDM experience.

PGDM Programme & Specialisations

2-Year Full-Time Residential PGDM

The programme runs across six terms. The first year builds a common foundation across management functions; the second year offers specialisation and elective choices. The 2026–28 published fee includes tuition and hostel stay, while food is charged separately based on actual mess expenditure.

Single or Dual Specialisation

Students can choose single or dual specialisation from Finance, Marketing, Systems, Data Analytics, Human Resources and Operations. The placement curriculum page highlights more than 70 electives across these tracks.

Eligibility: Graduates and final-year students from a full-time graduation programme recognised by UGC/AIU are eligible. SDMIMD's current criteria require a consistent minimum of 50% aggregate in Class 10, Class 12 and the degree. Final-year students are considered using the percentage available up to the last completed semester/year.

SDMIMD Admission Process

Check the academic requirement.
Applicants need a recognised full-time bachelor's degree and at least 50% aggregate in Class 10, Class 12 and graduation under the current published criteria.
Take or register for an accepted entrance test.
SDMIMD accepts CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA and MAT scores. The institute also says applicants who have not yet registered for an exam can still apply and send their registration details later.
Submit the SDMIMD application.
The current official PGDM page lists an application fee of ₹1,500 for the current 2027–29 application page. Applicants should use the live application portal for the final payable amount because older admission pages show a different historical fee.
Shortlisting.
Shortlisting considers consistent academic performance, competitive-exam score, extracurricular achievements such as sports/debates/theatre/fine arts and work experience where applicable.
Personal Interview.
Shortlisted candidates attend an interview at designated centres across India; online interviews can also be arranged on request according to the institute.
Offer and fee payment.
Selected candidates receive the offer by email/phone. The seat is blocked after the first instalment by the offer-letter deadline and confirmed subject to the entrance score and second instalment.
Does SDMIMD have a separate direct-admission route? The official admission process is built around the listed management entrance exams plus SDMIMD's shortlist and interview. The institute does not present a separate guaranteed management-quota route on its current PGDM admission page.

SDMIMD Placements

2024–26 Placement Outcome

Latest reported figures show ₹16.25 LPA highest, ₹10.10 LPA average, ₹9.70 LPA median, ₹13.80 LPA average for the top 10% and ₹10.70 LPA for the top 50%.

Placement Mix

The latest reported sector distribution is concentrated in BFSI (44%) and IT & Services (36%), followed by consulting and FMCG/FMCD. This makes SDMIMD especially relevant for students targeting finance, banking, consulting and technology-enabled business roles.

Campus & Student Life

SDMIMD is a fully residential institute on Chamundi Hill Road in Mysuru. The institute describes its campus as a contemporary management-learning environment with a library combining conventional and digital resources, residential facilities, a student-managed mess, gym and wellness facilities.

Residential Learning

The residential model keeps academic, peer-learning and extracurricular activity within the campus environment, which can be useful for students who value a more immersive B-school experience.

International Exposure

SDMIMD highlights international student-exchange programmes with partner universities. Students interested in exchange should verify the current partner list, eligibility and additional costs for their batch.

Is SDMIMD Mysore Worth Considering?

Good Fit If...

You want a residential PGDM, value a structured academic environment, want finance/marketing/HR/operations/systems/data-analytics choices, and are comfortable targeting a mid-market B-school placement profile rather than paying only for a metro-city brand.

Think Twice If...

Your only goal is a very high consulting/IB placement or you strongly prefer a large-city campus. The latest median is ₹9.70 LPA, so the institute should be evaluated on role quality, sector mix and total cost—not the ₹16.25 LPA highest package alone.

SDMIMD Mysore FAQs

What is SDMIMD Mysore famous for?
SDMIMD is primarily known for its two-year full-time residential PGDM. The programme is AICTE approved, NBA accredited, has AIU MBA equivalence and offers single or dual specialisation options across Finance, Marketing, Systems, Data Analytics, HR and Operations.
What is the SDMIMD Mysore PGDM fee?
The published 2026–28 fee is ₹14.10 lakh for two years including tuition and hostel stay. Food is charged separately and is approximately ₹35,000 per year according to the official programme page. SDMIMD says the fee is subject to revision for the 2027–29 batch.
Which exams are accepted by SDMIMD?
The current PGDM page accepts CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA and MAT. Candidates can also apply before their entrance-exam result and submit their registration/score details later.
What is the eligibility for SDMIMD PGDM?
Applicants need a recognised full-time bachelor's degree and a consistent minimum of 50% aggregate in Class 10, Class 12 and graduation under the current criteria. Final-year candidates can apply using the marks available up to the last completed semester/year.
Does SDMIMD accept students without CAT?
Yes. SDMIMD accepts several tests besides CAT, including XAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA and MAT. The institute also permits applicants who are still awaiting or registering for an entrance exam to apply and submit the exam details later.
What is the average package at SDMIMD?
The latest 2024–26 placement figures report an average salary of ₹10.10 LPA and a median of ₹9.70 LPA. The highest package was ₹16.25 LPA. The median is generally more useful than the highest package for judging the typical placement outcome.
Is SDMIMD good for finance?
It can be a relevant option for finance-oriented students. Finance is one of the PGDM specialisation tracks, and the latest placement mix reported by the institute's placement data is heavily concentrated in BFSI. Students should still compare actual finance-role offers, not just the overall placement average.
Is SDMIMD worth it compared with other PGDM colleges?
It depends on your alternatives. SDMIMD is more compelling when you value its residential format, academic consistency, specialisation flexibility and a roughly ₹10 LPA average/₹9.70 LPA median outcome. Compare it against your other calls on total cost, median salary, role mix, location preference and the strength of the specific function you want.
