IBS Hyderabad Overview

ICFAI Business School Hyderabad is the flagship IBS campus under the ICFAI Foundation for Higher Education. The institute offers a two-year full-time MBA and is strongly associated with case-based teaching. IFHE's annual report notes that IBS Hyderabad maps course topics to business cases and uses hundreds of cases across the two-year programme.

Is IBS Hyderabad worth considering? It can suit students who want a large private residential MBA, extensive case-based learning and a broad corporate-facing curriculum. The main decision points are the relatively high fee, placement outcomes, entrance route and fit with the case-method learning style.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Programme

2-Year Full-Time MBA

The flagship MBA combines core management education with case analysis, classroom discussion, projects, internships and industry interaction.

Case-Based Learning

IBS describes its teaching approach as case based. Students analyse business situations and discuss decisions rather than relying only on textbook theory.

Industry Exposure

The Career Management Centre conducts industry interaction, professional-development sessions and placement preparation across finance, marketing, HR, operations and IT.

Summer Internship

The Summer Internship Programme is an integral part of the MBA and gives students practical exposure to organisations and business roles.

Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline with at least 50% marks and the prescribed English-medium and 15-year education requirements. Final-year students can apply subject to completing the degree requirements within the institute's prescribed timeline.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Admission Process

Register for IBSAT 2026.
The current IBSAT portal lists an application fee of ₹1,800. A single application can be used for all nine IBS campuses.
Use an accepted entrance route.
Applicants can appear for IBSAT or use valid CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC or GMAT scores, subject to the current admission rules.
Meet eligibility.
Applicants need at least 50% in graduation plus the prescribed education and English-medium conditions.
Shortlisting.
Applicants qualifying through IBSAT or an accepted external score are invited to the next selection stage.
Micro Presentation + Personal Interview.
The published process includes both. Final selection considers past academic performance along with performance in these stages.
Admission offer and payment.
Selected students complete admission formalities and pay the applicable fee according to the offer instructions.
Important: A CAT/XAT/NMAT/GMAT score does not remove the need to apply to IBS. The institute requires the candidate to complete its application and participate in the prescribed selection process.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Fees: 2027–29

The latest official fee page lists the IBS Hyderabad MBA at ₹8.505 lakh per year, or ₹17.01 lakh for two years. A ₹10,000 refundable caution deposit is payable with the first instalment. Living expenses are additional, and IBS Hyderabad also lists a ₹4,000 convocation fee with the fifth instalment.

Scholarships

For the 2027–29 intake, IBS has announced ₹10 crore in merit scholarships, comprising 500 scholarships of ₹2 lakh each. The scheme is open to eligible candidates taking IBSAT 2026 and is also extended to candidates applying through CAT/XAT/NMAT/GMAT routes, subject to the scholarship rules.

Campus & Student Life

IBS Hyderabad is located at Donthanapally on Shankarpalli Road, Hyderabad. The campus is designed as a residential management-school environment with academic facilities, student activities, case discussions, internships and industry interaction.

Location decision: The campus is away from central Hyderabad. Students should consider the residential nature of the programme and their preferred daily commute before choosing IBS Hyderabad.

Who Should Choose IBS Hyderabad?

Good fit if you...

Prefer case-based learning; want a large residential MBA campus; are comfortable with a ₹17 lakh-plus academic fee; want multiple entrance-test routes; and value broad corporate exposure.

Reconsider if you...

Need a low-cost MBA; are focused mainly on guaranteed high packages; prefer a small-batch institute; or want a programme where the highest package is representative of the typical outcome.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IBS Hyderabad good for an MBA?
IBS Hyderabad can be a strong option for students who value case-based learning, a residential campus and broad corporate exposure. The decision should be made after comparing its ₹17.01 lakh current fee with average placement outcomes and your alternatives.
What is the IBS Hyderabad MBA fee for 2027–29?
The current official fee listing is ₹8.505 lakh per year, or ₹17.01 lakh for two years. A ₹10,000 refundable caution deposit is payable with the first instalment, while living expenses are extra.
Which entrance exams are accepted by IBS Hyderabad?
IBS accepts IBSAT as well as valid CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC and GMAT scores for the applicable MBA admission route. Candidates still need to complete the IBS application and selection process.
What is the average package at IBS Hyderabad?
For the Class of 2026, the current official IBSAT placement page reports an average CTC of ₹10.01 LPA for IBS Hyderabad. It reports a ₹40 LPA highest CTC and ₹15.58 LPA average for the top 10%.
Does IBS Hyderabad offer scholarships?
Yes. For the 2027–29 intake, IBS has announced ₹10 crore of merit scholarships, comprising 500 scholarships of ₹2 lakh each. Eligibility and continuation conditions depend on the scholarship scheme.
Is IBS Hyderabad a residential campus?
Yes. IBS Hyderabad operates as a residential management campus. Students should budget separately for living expenses because the published MBA fee does not represent the full cost of living on campus.
What is the selection process at IBS Hyderabad?
Candidates qualify through IBSAT or an accepted CAT/XAT/NMAT/GMAT score and are then considered for the selection process. The published process includes a Micro Presentation and Personal Interview, with final selection also considering past academic performance.
