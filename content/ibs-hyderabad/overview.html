IBS Hyderabad Overview

ICFAI Business School (IBS) Hyderabad is the flagship IBS campus under the ICFAI Foundation for Higher Education. The campus offers a two-year full-time MBA and uses a case-based learning approach. IBS describes its teaching model as 100% case-based and highlights a large alumni network and industry engagement across finance, banking, consulting, IT/ITES, FMCG, manufacturing and e-commerce.

Is IBS Hyderabad worth considering? It can suit students who want a private, residential MBA with a strong case-method orientation and a broad corporate-facing curriculum. The key decision factors are its relatively high total fee, placement outcomes, entrance route and whether the student values IBS's case-based learning model.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Programme

2-Year Full-Time MBA

IBS Hyderabad offers a full-time MBA. The school emphasises case-based teaching, action learning, leadership development, extracurricular activities and industry exposure.

Case-Based Learning

IBS states that its teaching is 100% case based. Students work with business situations designed to develop analysis, discussion, decision-making and communication skills.

Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline with at least 50% marks, with English as the medium of instruction, and a minimum of 15 years of education (10+2+3 or 10+2+4). Final-year students can apply subject to completing degree requirements within the prescribed timeline.

Applicants whose graduation was not in English must submit a valid TOEFL, NELT or IELTS score as required by the institute.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Admission Process

Register for IBSAT 2026.
The current IBSAT page lists an application fee of ₹1,800. A single application is used for the nine IBS campuses.
Choose your test route.
Applicants can take IBSAT or apply using valid CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC or GMAT scores, subject to the current admission rules.
Meet the academic eligibility.
Applicants need at least 50% in graduation and the prescribed English-medium/education-duration requirements.
Receive a selection invitation.
Shortlisting is based on the qualifying entrance score or accepted test score and the institute's criteria.
Attend the selection process.
The current published process includes a Micro Presentation and Personal Interview. IBS says final admission considers past academic record along with performance in these stages.
Receive the final admission decision.
Selected students complete admission formalities and fee payment as per the offer and campus instructions.
Does IBS Hyderabad accept CAT/XAT/NMAT/GMAT? Yes. IBS's current admissions material says valid CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC and GMAT scores can be used instead of IBSAT. Candidates still need to complete the IBS application.

IBS Hyderabad Scholarships

IBS has announced ₹10 crore worth of scholarships for the 2027–29 programme, comprising 500 scholarships of ₹2 lakh each. The current announcement covers candidates taking IBSAT 2026 and also candidates applying through CAT/XAT/NMAT by GMAC/GMAT scores.

Important: Scholarship eligibility and adjustment rules are separate from admission eligibility. Students should check the current scholarship terms before assuming a particular test score guarantees an award.

Campus & Student Life

IBS Hyderabad's campus is in the Dontanapalli/Shankarpally Road area of Hyderabad. The institute presents the campus as a residential learning environment with case-based classrooms, student activities, leadership development, cultural clubs and industry interaction. IBS Hyderabad FAQs

Is IBS Hyderabad a good college for MBA?
IBS Hyderabad is a recognised private management school with a two-year MBA, case-based teaching and a large corporate/alumni ecosystem. Whether it is a good choice depends on your entrance score, career target and ability to justify the total cost of the programme against the placement outcomes.
What is the IBS Hyderabad MBA fee?
The latest clearly published official reference used by CollegeDecoded lists ₹16.21 lakh as the programme fee plus ₹80,000 admission fee, with a ₹10,000 refundable caution deposit and living expenses extra. Applicants for 2027–29 should verify the updated fee before paying.
Does IBS Hyderabad accept CAT, XAT or NMAT?
Yes. IBS's current admission material says valid CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC and GMAT scores can be used in place of IBSAT, subject to the current cycle's rules. The IBS application still needs to be completed.
What is the IBSAT exam pattern?
IBSAT is a two-hour computer-based aptitude test with 140 questions. The current pattern has Verbal Ability (50), Reading Comprehension (30), Quantitative Aptitude (30), and Data Adequacy & Data Interpretation (30).
What is the eligibility for IBS Hyderabad MBA?
Applicants need graduation in any discipline with at least 50% marks, English as the medium of instruction and a minimum of 15 years of education. Final-year students can apply subject to completing the degree requirements within the institute's stated timeline.
What is the IBS Hyderabad highest and average package?
For the Class of 2026, the current IBS placement page reports ₹40 LPA as the highest CTC and ₹10.01 LPA as the average CTC for IBS Hyderabad. The top 10% average is ₹15.58 LPA and top 25% average is ₹13.36 LPA.
Does IBS Hyderabad have an entrance exam?
Yes. IBSAT is the institute's own aptitude test and is the primary common route across the IBS campuses. Candidates can also use valid CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC or GMAT scores instead of IBSAT under the published admission rules.
Is IBS Hyderabad residential?
IBS Hyderabad is designed as a residential campus environment. Hostel and living costs are separate from the published academic/programme fee, so students should calculate the full two-year cost before accepting an offer.
