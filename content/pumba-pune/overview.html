PUMBA Pune Overview

The Department of Management Sciences (PUMBA) is a management department of Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU). It is located at Ganeshkhind, Pune and describes itself as one of the early management institutes in India, with management education dating back to 1971.

Why students consider PUMBA: PUMBA is particularly relevant for students looking for an SPPU MBA in Pune with a university-backed environment and a relatively affordable fee structure. Its main MBA admissions are governed through Maharashtra's official CET/CAP process rather than a private management-quota route.

PUMBA's current site also lists MBA Executive, MBA Pharma-Biotech and BBA programmes. For the flagship MBA, students should follow the latest Maharashtra State CET Cell notification and PUMBA's admission instructions rather than relying on older cutoff or date articles.

2026-27 status: PUMBA's official website currently carries MBA 2026-27 admission notices, including instructions for the Institute Level Round and vacancy information after CAP IV. Exact CAP dates are controlled by the Maharashtra CET Cell and can change during the admission cycle.

Programmes at PUMBA

MBA

The flagship full-time postgraduate management programme. Eligibility is generally a bachelor's degree with at least 50%; the official PUMBA page states 45% for backward-class/PwD categories. Admission follows the Maharashtra MBA CET/CAP framework.

MBA (Executive)

A two-year MBA for working executives and entrepreneurs. PUMBA requires graduation with at least 50% (45% for reserved candidates) plus a minimum of two years of managerial/executive work experience.

MBA (Pharma-Biotechnology)

A specialised MBA for eligible science, agriculture, medicine, engineering, pharmacy, technology and related graduates. For AY 2026-27, PUMBA specifies ATMA as the entrance test.

BBA

PUMBA currently lists BBA in Hospitality & Facilities Management and BBA in Facility & Services Management. The admission route differs by programme, so applicants should check the relevant 2026-27 notification.

MBA Admission Process

Take the required entrance exam.
For the regular MBA, admission is linked to the Maharashtra State CET Cell's MBA/MMS admission process.
Participate in the Maharashtra CAP process.
Eligible candidates register through the official CET Cell process and participate in Centralised Admission Process rounds.
Receive a PUMBA allotment.
Candidates allotted PUMBA must follow the institute's reporting and confirmation instructions for the relevant CAP round.
Verify original documents.
PUMBA's published instructions require scrutiny/verification of original documents before admission confirmation.
Pay the prescribed fees.
Fee payment is made through the prescribed SPPU One Campus process after approval/verification.
Does PUMBA offer management quota/direct guaranteed admission? No. PUMBA explicitly warns students that it does not offer management quota admission and that admissions are based on merit and the official selection procedure. Students should avoid anyone claiming to guarantee a PUMBA seat for payment.

International/supernumerary admissions have separate procedures and fee categories. They should not be mixed with the domestic Maharashtra CAP route.

PUMBA Fees

PUMBA's fee is one of the major reasons students compare it with private MBA institutes in Pune. The exact 2026-27 domestic fee should be taken from the admission-year fee notice because university fee heads can change. PUMBA's published 2024-25 MBA fee document shows multiple statutory and student-service heads rather than a single private-institute-style tuition figure.

Important fee point: Do not use foreign/supernumerary fee figures for domestic CAP candidates. For example, PUMBA's 2025-26 supernumerary instructions list ₹2,88,233 for CIWGC/SAARC categories and ₹4,25,733 for Foreign National/PIO/OCI/other specified categories for first-year MBA; these are separate from regular domestic admissions.

PUMBA Placements

PUMBA's official placement report for the 2022-24 batch reported ₹16.22 LPA highest package, ₹10.84 LPA average package, ₹15.42 LPA for the top 10%, ₹9.40 LPA for the top 50% and 80.7% placement among 171 participants. More than 140 companies visited, with BFSI/NBFC, IT, manufacturing, research/consulting and FMCG/retail among the major sectors.

Roles and recruiters

Officially reported roles include Financial Analyst, Credit Analyst, Growth & Strategy, Consultant, Business Analyst, Market Research Analyst, Product Manager, Project Manager, Supply Chain Manager, HR Recruiter and Talent Acquisition Specialist. Reported recruiters include Deloitte, Acuity Knowledge Partners, ICICI Bank, RBL Bank, Aditya Birla Capital, HDFC Bank, PwC, Siemens, Schneider Electric, Tata Consultancy Services, Tata Steel Downstream and others.

Campus & Student Life

PUMBA is situated within the Savitribai Phule Pune University campus at Ganeshkhind. Students benefit from the wider SPPU academic ecosystem, university facilities and Pune's large corporate and student environment.

Who should consider PUMBA? Students prioritising value for money, an SPPU university environment, Pune location and management roles across BFSI, IT, consulting, manufacturing and related sectors may find PUMBA particularly relevant. Students who prioritise a premium private B-school ecosystem should compare it separately on fees, campus infrastructure, placement consistency and selection process.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PUMBA Pune?
PUMBA is the Department of Management Sciences of Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), located at Ganeshkhind, Pune. It offers management programmes including the flagship MBA.
How can I get admission to PUMBA MBA?
For the regular MBA, candidates follow the Maharashtra State CET Cell admission process, including the applicable MBA entrance examination and CAP rounds. A PUMBA allotment is followed by document verification and fee payment as instructed by the institute.
Does PUMBA accept CAT or XAT?
PUMBA's regular domestic MBA admission is governed by the Maharashtra State CET Cell process. Students should not assume that CAT/XAT scores create a separate direct-admission route; follow the current CET Cell/PUMBA notification for the admission year.
Does PUMBA have a management quota?
No. PUMBA explicitly states that it does not offer management quota admission and warns students against third parties claiming to guarantee admission.
What is the PUMBA MBA fee?
The regular domestic fee is determined through SPPU's prescribed fee structure and candidate category. Students should use the current admission-year fee notice rather than relying on older websites. International/supernumerary fees are separate.
What is the average package at PUMBA?
PUMBA's official placement report for the 2022-24 batch reported an average package of ₹10.84 LPA and a highest package of ₹16.22 LPA. The report also recorded 80.7% placement among 171 participants.
Is PUMBA good for MBA?
PUMBA can be a strong value-oriented MBA option for students who want SPPU's university environment and Pune location. The decision should be based on the latest placement report, current fee, your CET percentile/category and the specific roles you want after MBA.
Is PUMBA better than private MBA colleges in Pune?
There is no universal answer. PUMBA's major advantage is its university setting and comparatively economical fee structure, while some private institutes may offer different infrastructure, corporate exposure or placement ecosystems. Compare current fees, median/average packages, placement rate and your admission probability rather than relying only on rankings.
