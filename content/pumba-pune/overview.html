PUMBA Pune Overview

The Department of Management Sciences (PUMBA), Savitribai Phule Pune University, is a university management department at Ganeshkhind, Pune. PUMBA traces its management-education legacy to 1971 and is known for its flagship full-time MBA, university ecosystem and comparatively low tuition.

Why students shortlist PUMBA: It combines a highly competitive Maharashtra CAP admission route with a low-cost public-university MBA and a recent official placement report showing ₹12.20 LPA average for the top 50% and ₹19 LPA as the highest package.

PUMBA currently also lists MBA Executive, MBA Pharma-Biotechnology and BBA programmes. This overview focuses primarily on the regular MBA because it is the programme most students mean when they search for PUMBA Pune.

PUMBA MBA Admission 2026

PUMBA's official website is publishing MBA 2026–27 admission notices, including institute-level-round instructions and vacancy information after CAP IV. Regular domestic MBA admission is governed by the Maharashtra State CET Cell/Centralised Admission Process. PUMBA also explicitly states that it does not offer management quota admission.

Courses & Specialisations

Full-Time MBA

Two years with a trimester-based curriculum. PUMBA's published structure uses 100 credits and combines core management learning with functional and sector-focused exposure.

Functional Specialisations

PUMBA has published functional areas including Marketing, Finance, Systems, Operations, Human Resources and International Business. Exact major/minor combinations can vary by batch.

MBA Pharma-Biotechnology

A specialised two-year programme for eligible science, agriculture, medicine, engineering, pharmacy, technology and related graduates. AY 2026–27 admissions use ATMA.

MBA Executive

A two-year programme for working executives and entrepreneurs. The current eligibility page requires graduation with 50% (45% for eligible reserved candidates) plus minimum managerial/executive experience.

PUMBA MBA Cutoff

PUMBA is one of the most competitive MBA options in Maharashtra CAP. For 2025 Round 1, the reported closing percentile was 99.85 for the All-India category and 98.97 for Maharashtra State Open (GOPENH). Category and round materially affect the cutoff.

PUMBA Placements: Latest Official Trend

The latest detailed official placement report located for PUMBA is for the 2022–24 batch. It reported a ₹19 LPA highest package, ₹12.20 LPA average for the top 50%, ₹15.40 LPA for the top 10% and ₹11.23 LPA overall average CTC. More than 140 companies participated.

Campus & Student Life

PUMBA is located inside the Savitribai Phule Pune University campus at Ganeshkhind. Students get access to the wider SPPU academic environment and Pune's large student and corporate ecosystem. PUMBA also runs corporate-relations activities, workshops, guest lectures, competitions and placement preparation.

University ecosystem

The SPPU campus provides a broader academic setting than a standalone private institute, with university resources available to students.

Corporate exposure

Corporate-relations and placement activities include guest sessions, workshops and recruiter interactions across BFSI, IT, consulting, manufacturing and other sectors.

Who Should Choose PUMBA?

Good fit if you want

A low-cost MBA, Pune location, SPPU's university environment, a merit-driven CET/CAP route and strong ROI relative to tuition.

Reconsider if you want

A premium private-B-school campus, a highly residential experience, extensive international exposure or a narrowly specialised modern curriculum.

Compare before deciding

For Pune/Maharashtra MBA decisions, compare PUMBA with JBIMS, SIMSREE, Welingkar, K J Somaiya and other CAP options on actual cutoff, total payable fee, median/average salary, placement rate, specialisation fit and admission probability.

PUMBA FAQs

Is PUMBA a good college for MBA?
PUMBA is a strong ROI-oriented option, especially for students who can secure a very high Maharashtra CAP percentile. Its key strengths are low tuition, SPPU's university ecosystem and a recent official placement report with ₹11.23 LPA overall average CTC and ₹19 LPA highest package. The experience is different from a private residential B-school, so campus style and infrastructure should also be considered.
What is the PUMBA MBA cutoff?
For 2025 Round 1, the closing percentile was 99.85 for All India and 98.97 for Maharashtra State Open. Cutoffs vary by category and CAP round. For an open/general candidate, 99+ is a sensible competitive target, but it does not guarantee admission.
Which exams are accepted for PUMBA MBA?
Regular domestic MBA admission is governed by the Maharashtra State CET Cell/CAP process. MAH MBA CET is the key state route, while eligible All-India scores can be considered under the applicable CAP rules. Students should use the current CET Cell information brochure rather than assuming that any national exam creates a separate direct-admission route.
What is the PUMBA MBA fee?
Current 2026 listings place MBA tuition at about ₹1.30–1.52 lakh for the programme, with other recent sources citing roughly ₹1.25 lakh per year. Because university fees can vary by category and revision, confirm the exact payable amount from the current PUMBA/SPPU fee notice.
What is the average package at PUMBA?
The latest detailed official placement report located for PUMBA, for the 2022–24 batch, reported ₹11.23 LPA overall average CTC and ₹19 LPA highest package. It also reported ₹12.20 LPA average for the top 50% and ₹15.40 LPA for the top 10%.
What specialisations are available at PUMBA?
PUMBA's published MBA curriculum has included Marketing, Finance, Systems, Operations, Human Resources and International Business, with sector-oriented exposure as well. Exact major/minor combinations can change by batch, so students should check the current curriculum before choosing PUMBA for a particular specialisation.
Does PUMBA have management quota?
No. PUMBA explicitly states that it does not offer management quota admission and warns students about third parties claiming to guarantee admission. Admission should be pursued only through the official CET Cell/PUMBA process.
