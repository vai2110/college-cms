IISWBM Kolkata MBA: Overview

Indian Institute of Social Welfare and Business Management (IISWBM) is a management institute in Kolkata with a long history of management education. Its two-year full-time MBA (Day) is awarded by the University of Calcutta and is approved by AICTE. The programme has an official intake of 120 seats.

The programme combines management fundamentals with case-based understanding, classroom interaction, practical business examples and industry exposure. IISWBM also offers MBA programmes in areas such as Human Resource Management, Public Systems and an Evening MBA, but this page focuses on the flagship two-year full-time MBA (Day).

What makes IISWBM worth evaluating?

The biggest student-facing advantage is the combination of a University of Calcutta MBA, relatively moderate programme fees and a centrally located Kolkata campus. The trade-off is that applicants should compare the institute's placement outcomes and career fit carefully against higher-fee private B-schools before making a final decision.

IISWBM MBA Eligibility

The current programme information requires a recognised bachelor's qualification of at least three years, a four-year honours degree, a professional degree or an equivalent programme recognised by the University of Calcutta. The minimum academic requirement is 50% aggregate marks, with 45% for SC/ST/NC-OBC/PWD candidates.

General eligibility

At least 3-year bachelor's degree, 4-year honours degree, professional degree or equivalent qualification recognised by the University of Calcutta, with 50% aggregate.

Relaxed threshold

The official programme page specifies 45% aggregate for SC/ST/NC-OBC/PWD candidates.

Final-year candidates

IISWBM's admission material has historically allowed candidates appearing for final examinations to apply, subject to fulfilling the required degree and documentation conditions. Applicants should verify the exact final-year rule in the admission brochure for their batch.

IISWBM MBA Selection Criteria

IISWBM's MBA (Day) selection is not based on CAT percentile alone. The institute obtains CAT scores of applicants and uses a cutoff to call candidates for Group Discussion and Personal Interview; the final selected list is prepared on merit.

IISWBM Kolkata MBA Cutoff

IISWBM does not publish a single fixed CAT percentile on its programme page as a guaranteed final-admission threshold. Instead, the institute states that candidates are called for GD and PI on the basis of a CAT cutoff, after which the final list is prepared on merit.

IISWBM Kolkata Campus & Student Life

IISWBM is located at Management House, College Square West, Kolkata. The institute's campus ecosystem includes academic facilities, library resources, student activities, clubs and industry-facing events. The location also puts students in central Kolkata with access to the wider city's corporate and cultural ecosystem.

Student activities

The institute provides student clubs, organisations, events and management activities alongside the academic programme.

Industry interaction

Case discussions, business interactions, guest sessions and placement activities help connect classroom learning with workplace expectations.

Summer project

The MBA programme provides an opportunity for a two-month summer project after the second semester, according to IISWBM's programme brochure.

Location advantage

College Square West is centrally located in Kolkata, making the campus accessible to the city's business and cultural districts.

Is IISWBM Kolkata MBA Worth It?

It can make sense if...

You want a University of Calcutta MBA at a substantially lower fee than many private B-schools, are comfortable with a CAT + GD/PI process, and value Kolkata's location and the institute's established management ecosystem.

Compare carefully if...

Your priority is a very high average package, a large national recruiter base or a premium private-B-school brand. In that case, compare the latest placement outcomes, batch size, total living cost and career domain before deciding.

Quick student checklist

  • Compare the latest MBA (Day) placement report rather than generic institute placement claims.
  • Calculate total cost including living expenses, not only the ₹8.30 lakh academic fee.
  • Check whether your preferred specialisation aligns with the companies and roles you want.
  • Prepare for GD and PI even if your CAT score is strong.

IISWBM Kolkata MBA FAQs

Is IISWBM Kolkata a good option for an MBA?

IISWBM can be a strong value-oriented option for students who want a University of Calcutta MBA at a comparatively moderate academic fee. Its two-year MBA (Day) has 120 seats, uses CAT followed by GD/PI, and offers specialisations across marketing, finance, HR, operations and business analytics. The decision should still be based on your target career, CAT profile, placement expectations and the latest programme-specific placement report rather than the institute name alone.

What is the eligibility for IISWBM MBA?

The official MBA (Day) programme page requires at least a three-year bachelor's degree, a four-year honours degree, a professional degree or an equivalent qualification recognised by the University of Calcutta. The general minimum is 50% aggregate marks, with the published relaxation to 45% for SC/ST/NC-OBC/PWD candidates. Final-year applicants should check the current admission brochure for the exact document and result-submission conditions applicable to their batch.

Which entrance exam is accepted for IISWBM MBA?

For the two-year full-time MBA (Day), IISWBM's current programme information uses CAT for the admission process. The institute receives the CAT scores of applicants from the IIM admission committee and uses a cutoff to shortlist candidates for GD and PI. This is different from some other IISWBM programmes, such as the Evening MBA or MBA-Public Systems, which have broader lists of accepted management tests.

What is the IISWBM MBA fee for 2026-28?

The official fee breakup applicable from 2026 onwards lists the two-year MBA (Day) total at ₹8.30 lakh. The first semester is ₹2.45 lakh and each of the next three semesters is ₹1.95 lakh. The first-semester amount includes ₹5,000 refundable library caution money and ₹5,000 refundable security deposit, while the fee document separately lists student activity/venue-visit charges.

What is the IISWBM MBA placement package?

Latest publicly reported 2025 MBA (Day) placement figures show a ₹16 lakh highest domestic salary, ₹8 lakh average salary and ₹9 lakh median salary. Reported placement data also shows 181 students placed out of 198 graduates for the relevant dataset. Because placement reports can differ by programme and graduating cohort, students should verify the exact MBA (Day) report before using a figure for ROI comparison.

Does IISWBM provide MBA specialisations?

Yes. The MBA (Day) curriculum lists five specialisation areas: Marketing Management, Financial Management, Human Resource Management, Production and Operations Management, and Business Analytics and Systems Management. Students choose one area and study specialised papers within it, while the programme also includes a common management foundation covering subjects such as accounting, economics, marketing, HR, operations, strategy, research methodology and data analytics.

Is IISWBM MBA better for ROI than high-fee private B-schools?

IISWBM's ₹8.30 lakh academic fee makes its cost base materially lower than many private B-schools, so it can offer attractive fee-to-salary economics if you secure a suitable role after graduation. However, ROI should not be judged from the highest package alone. Compare the latest average and median salary, placement coverage, your expected living cost in Kolkata, role quality and your alternative college offers before making the decision.

Does IISWBM have hostel facilities?

IISWBM lists hostel facilities through its official website, but hostel availability, allocation and charges can vary by programme and session. Students should therefore not add an assumed hostel amount to the MBA fee without checking the current hostel notice. For budgeting, treat accommodation, food, travel and other personal expenses as separate from the ₹8.30 lakh academic programme fee.
