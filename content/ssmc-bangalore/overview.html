SSMC Bangalore Overview

Symbiosis School of Media and Communication (SSMC) Bengaluru was a Symbiosis institution established in 2009 that offered a two-year full-time MBA in Communication Management. Historical programme material identifies an intake of 80 and an academic focus spanning communication-led business areas such as advertising, public relations and media management.

For students applying in 2026: do not treat older SSMC Bengaluru admission, fee or placement pages as proof that the Bengaluru programme is accepting applications today. SIU's current 2026–27 academic calendar lists MBA Communication Management under Symbiosis Institute of Media and Communication (SIMC), while the older 2022–23 calendar separately listed SSMC Bengaluru. This indicates a change in the current institutional listing that applicants should verify before making any payment or application decision.

Current Status: SSMC Bangalore vs SIMC Pune

The most important information for a prospective student is the change in SIU's programme listing. SIU's 2022–23 academic calendar explicitly listed Symbiosis School of Media and Communication, Bengaluru with MBA Communication Management. The current 2026–27 SIU academic calendar instead lists Symbiosis Institute of Media and Communication with MBA Communication Management and does not separately list SSMC Bengaluru.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SSMC Bangalore still offering MBA Communication Management in 2026?
The current SIU 2026–27 academic calendar does not separately list SSMC Bengaluru; it lists MBA Communication Management under Symbiosis Institute of Media and Communication (SIMC). SSMC Bengaluru was separately listed in older SIU calendars. Therefore, a student searching for SSMC Bangalore MBA admission in 2026 should verify the current SIMC programme rather than assume that the old Bengaluru intake is open.
What happened to SSMC Bangalore?
SIU's programme naming has changed in its current academic-calendar listings. Older calendars separately identified Symbiosis School of Media and Communication, Bengaluru, while current 2026–27 listings identify Symbiosis Institute of Media and Communication with MBA Communication Management. The page therefore treats SSMC Bengaluru as a historical campus/programme reference rather than claiming a current Bengaluru intake without official confirmation.
What course did SSMC Bangalore offer?
The historical SSMC Bengaluru programme was a two-year full-time MBA in Communication Management. Archived material described its focus around advertising, public relations, media management and communication-driven business functions, with an historical intake of 80.
Was SNAP compulsory for SSMC Bangalore MBA?
Yes, historical SSMC Bengaluru admission material states that candidates seeking the MBA in Communication Management had to register for and appear in SNAP, followed by the institute's own registration and selection process.
What was the SSMC Bangalore MBA admission process?
The historical route was SNAP registration and examination, separate SSMC Bengaluru programme registration, shortlisting, and GE-PI-WAT selection. Final admission was based on the prescribed merit process. These steps should not be assumed to be the current 2026 process for SIMC Pune.
What was the SSMC Bangalore MBA fee?
Archived 2020–22 programme information reported ₹4.85 lakh academic fee per year for Indian students plus a ₹35,000 institute deposit, making the reported first-year total ₹5.20 lakh. These figures are historical and should not be used as the 2026 fee for any current Symbiosis programme.
What were SSMC Bangalore placements like?
The clearest published historical figure reviewed for SSMC Bengaluru is for the 2018–20 batch: 64% of eligible students were reported as placed. Recruiters included RK Swamy BBDO, Adfactors PR, MSL Group, Genesis BCW, Social Panga, FCB Ulka, GroupM and others. A current SSMC Bengaluru package figure is not presented because the institute is not separately listed in the current SIU programme calendar.
Is SSMC Bangalore the same as SIMC Pune?
They are not the same campus name. SSMC Bengaluru was the historical Bengaluru institution, while SIMC is the current Symbiosis Institute of Media and Communication listed by SIU. Both names are associated with MBA Communication Management in different SIU academic-calendar periods, which is why students searching older SSMC information can encounter SIMC in current results.
