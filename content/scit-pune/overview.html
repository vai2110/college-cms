SCIT Pune Overview

SCIT is a constituent institute of Symbiosis International (Deemed University) at Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park, Hinjawadi, Pune. Its academic focus is the intersection of information technology and business management. The current postgraduate portfolio listed by SCIT includes the two-year residential MBA (Information Technology Business Management) and MBA (Data Science & Data Analytics).

What SCIT is specifically designed for: students who want management education tied closely to IT products, services, information security, systems, digital transformation or data/analytics. The MBA ITBM curriculum currently covers specialised streams including Information Security Management, Systems and Digital Transformation; SCIT also notes that Artificial Intelligence is planned for the 2027 batch in place of the earlier Data Science elective specialisation. Programmes at SCIT Pune

MBA (Information Technology Business Management)

Duration: 2 years, full-time residential. Intake: 180. The programme develops management capability for IT products and services and currently lists Information Security Management, Systems and Digital Transformation streams. SCIT states that Artificial Intelligence will be offered for the 2027 batch in place of the earlier Data Science elective specialisation.

MBA (Data Science & Data Analytics)

Duration: 2 years, full-time residential. Intake: 60. Eligibility is graduation with at least 50% (45% for SC/ST). The programme is focused on data science and analytics for management and uses SNAP plus the SCIT selection process.

Admission Process: Detailed but Crisp

Meet eligibility.
Applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade; the minimum is 45% for SC/ST. Final-year candidates can apply subject to meeting the required marks.
Take SNAP 2026.
SCIT states that appearing for SNAP and registering for SCIT are mandatory. The institute uses SNAP score for shortlisting candidates for its next selection stage.
Register for SCIT.
While registering for SNAP, applicants must also apply to SCIT and pay the institute registration fee. SCIT states that the programme registration is mandatory for consideration for GE-PI.
Get shortlisted for GE-PI.
SCIT shortlists applicants based on SNAP percentile. Shortlisted applicants then complete the institute's online Group Exercise and Personal Interaction process.
Final merit.
SCIT's published final-merit framework gives 50 marks to SNAP, 10 to Group Exercise and 40 to Personal Interview. The PI component also considers academic consistency, undergraduate degree, experience and extracurricular achievement.
Accept the offer and pay fees.
Candidates receiving an offer must pay the first instalment by the stated deadline. If the payment is not made on time, SCIT states that the seat can be offered to the next rank.
Are there direct/management-quota admissions? SCIT/SIU does not authorise agents or guarantee admission against payment. Admissions are through the prescribed eligibility and selection process. SCIT's official caution notice says SIU does not charge capitation fees and has not appointed anyone to guarantee admission outside the prescribed process.

International students are admitted through the Symbiosis Centre for International Education (SCIE). Reservation provisions are separately prescribed by SIU/SCIT, including SC, ST, PwD and defence-dependant categories.

Campus & Student Life

SCIT is located at Plot 15, Phase-I, Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park, Hinjawadi, Pune. The institute describes itself as an IT business school with computer labs, peer-learning opportunities and management learning linked to real-time IT business environments.

Residential status: Both MBA ITBM and MBA DSDA are listed by SCIT as full-time residential two-year programmes. Hostel fees are therefore a significant part of the published total cost and should be included when comparing SCIT with non-residential MBA options.

SCIT Pune FAQs

Is SNAP compulsory for SCIT Pune MBA admission?
Yes. SCIT states that candidates seeking admission to its MBA programmes must appear for SNAP and register for SCIT. SNAP performance is used for shortlisting candidates for the GE-PI stage, and the published final merit gives SNAP 50 marks out of 100.
What is the SCIT Pune MBA ITBM fee for 2026-28?
For Indian students, SCIT's 2026-28 MBA ITBM structure lists ₹3.90 lakh academic fee per instalment. The first instalment totals ₹6,02,300 after adding the ₹20,000 institute deposit, ₹20,000 hostel deposit, ₹1,12,500 three-sharing hostel fee and ₹59,800 mess fee. The second listed instalment is ₹4,49,800.
What is the SCIT Pune MBA placement package?
For MBA ITBM 2024-26, SCIT reports ₹30 LPA as the highest package, ₹11.65 LPA average and ₹11.64 LPA median, with 98% of eligible students placed. For MBA DSDA 2024-26, the corresponding figures are ₹20.38 LPA highest, ₹11.64 LPA average and ₹11.50 LPA median, with 87% placed.
What is the difference between SCIT MBA ITBM and MBA DSDA?
MBA ITBM has 180 seats and is built around IT business management with streams such as Information Security Management, Systems and Digital Transformation. MBA DSDA has 60 seats and is focused specifically on data science and data analytics for management. Their recent placement outcomes also differ: the 2024-26 highest package was ₹30 LPA for ITBM and ₹20.38 LPA for DSDA.
Does SCIT Pune have direct admission or management quota?
SCIT does not publish a management-quota or guaranteed direct-admission route for these MBA programmes. Its official caution notice states that SIU does not charge capitation fees and has not authorised agents or organisations to guarantee admission outside the prescribed process. Applicants should use only the official SNAP/SCIT process.
What is the SCIT Pune MBA eligibility criteria?
Candidates need a graduation degree from a recognised university or institution of national importance with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade. The minimum is 45% for SC/ST candidates. Final-year bachelor's candidates may apply, subject to obtaining the required marks.
What is the SCIT Pune MBA admission selection weightage?
SCIT's published selection framework awards 50 marks to SNAP, 10 to Group Exercise and 40 to Personal Interview, making the final merit total 100 marks. The institute states that the PI score also considers academic consistency at Class 10, Class 12 and graduation, undergraduate degree, experience and extracurricular achievement.
Is SCIT Pune a residential MBA college?
Yes. SCIT lists both MBA ITBM and MBA DSDA as full-time two-year residential programmes. Its 2026-28 fee structure separately lists a refundable hostel deposit, three-sharing hostel fee and annual mess fee, so accommodation is built into the practical cost calculation for most students.
