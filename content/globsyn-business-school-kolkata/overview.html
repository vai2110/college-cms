Globsyn Business School Kolkata PGDM: Overview

Globsyn Business School (GBS) is a management institute in Kolkata that positions itself as a corporate-focused B-school. Its two-year full-time PGDM is approved by AICTE and is designed around management fundamentals, industry exposure, research-driven case studies and practical learning.

For students comparing private B-schools in Kolkata and Eastern India, GBS is particularly relevant if you want a management programme with multiple entrance-exam routes, a structured aptitude test and a strong industry-connect orientation. The institute also offers a separate PGDM in Business Analytics and an MBA (Global), but this page focuses on the flagship PGDM.

At a glance

The official 2026-28 admission page confirms admissions are open, the PGDM is a two-year full-time programme, and the selection process combines GAT, a national management entrance score, academics, GD, PI and diversity/extracurricular factors.

Globsyn Business School PGDM Admission 2026-28

GBS has opened admissions for the 2026-28 batch. Unlike colleges that rely mainly on one national entrance exam, Globsyn uses a multi-stage process. Candidates can qualify through CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT or ATMA and must also appear for the Globsyn Aptitude Test (GAT), followed by GD and PI.

Globsyn Business School PGDM Eligibility

The official PGDM admission information requires a minimum three-year bachelor's degree in any discipline from a UGC-recognised university or equivalent, with at least 50% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA in accordance with AICTE norms. Final-year bachelor's students can also apply, subject to submitting proof of graduation and the required marks within the specified deadline.

Academic qualification

A minimum three-year bachelor's degree in any discipline from a UGC-recognised university or equivalent qualification.

Minimum marks

At least 50% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA as stated for the PGDM programme.

Final-year students

Students appearing in the final year can apply, subject to meeting the graduation and documentation requirements.

Entrance requirement

Applicants must appear for at least one accepted national-level management entrance examination: CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT or ATMA.

Globsyn Business School PGDM Selection Criteria

GBS publishes a 100-mark admission and selection framework. The national entrance score is important, but it contributes 10 marks; GAT, GD and PI together account for 70 marks, while academic performance and diversity/extracurricular factors account for the remaining 20 marks.

Student takeaway

A high CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT/ATMA score alone does not decide admission. GBS gives substantial weight to its own aptitude test, GD and PI, so applicants should prepare for the complete selection process rather than only the entrance exam.

Globsyn Business School Kolkata PGDM Cutoff

The institute states a minimum 50% cutoff percentile in CAT/MAT/CMAT/XAT/ATMA for applying to its PGDM. Treat this as the published qualifying threshold, not as a guaranteed final-admission percentile, because the final selection also depends on GAT, academics, GD, PI and other published factors.

How to interpret the cutoff

If your score is around the qualifying threshold, do not assume that admission is automatic. Since the institute's final selection uses a 100-mark framework, profile quality and performance in GAT, GD and PI can materially affect the outcome.

Globsyn Business School Courses

GBS offers multiple two-year full-time postgraduate management programmes. The main options relevant to MBA aspirants are the AICTE-approved PGDM, AICTE-approved PGDM in Business Analytics and the MBA (Global) offered in collaboration with Malaysia University of Science and Technology.

PGDM

The flagship AICTE-approved management programme with a broad business curriculum and corporate-oriented learning.

PGDM – Business Analytics

MBA (Global)

Experiential learning

Is Globsyn Business School Kolkata Worth It?

GBS can make sense for students who want a Kolkata-based private B-school with a moderate fee compared with many premium private institutes, several accepted entrance exams and a selection process that rewards performance beyond the national test score. Its corporate orientation and 2025 placement numbers make it worth considering for candidates targeting roles in BFSI, consulting, FMCG/FMCD, IT and services.

Globsyn Business School Kolkata PGDM FAQs

Is Globsyn Business School good for PGDM?

GBS is a reasonable option to evaluate for students seeking a Kolkata-based private B-school with an AICTE-approved PGDM, multiple entrance-exam routes and a strong corporate-learning orientation. Its 2025 placement figures show a ₹6.85 LPA average CTC and ₹16.42 LPA highest CTC, but students should compare these outcomes with competing colleges before making a final decision.

What is the eligibility for Globsyn PGDM?

Candidates need a minimum three-year bachelor's degree in any discipline from a UGC-recognised university or equivalent and at least 50% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA. Final-year students can apply subject to meeting the institute's documentation and graduation requirements.

Which entrance exams does Globsyn Business School accept?

For the PGDM, GBS accepts CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT and ATMA. The institute also requires applicants to participate in the Globsyn Aptitude Test, followed by GD and PI as part of the selection process.

What is the Globsyn Business School PGDM cutoff?

GBS publishes a minimum 50 percentile cutoff for CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT or ATMA as the qualifying threshold. This should not be interpreted as a guaranteed admission percentile because the final 100-mark selection also considers GAT, academics, GD, PI and diversity/extracurricular factors.

What is the Globsyn Business School PGDM fee?

The official GBS FAQ currently lists the total PGDM fee at ₹8.95 lakh. Hostel and other student-related expenses may be separate, so applicants should confirm the complete payable amount and semester schedule with the institute before admission.

What is the average package at Globsyn Business School?

GBS reports an average CTC of ₹6.85 LPA for its 2025 placement highlights. The same report lists ₹16.42 LPA as the highest CTC, but students should use the average and role-specific outcomes as more realistic planning benchmarks than the highest package alone.

Does Globsyn Business School provide hostel facilities?

Yes. GBS highlights its Globsyn Student Home/hostel facility as an on-campus lodging option. Because hostel fees and availability can change by batch, applicants should confirm the 2026-28 charges and room options directly with the admissions office.

Does Globsyn offer a Business Analytics programme?

Yes. GBS offers a separate AICTE-approved PGDM – Business Analytics programme. It is a two-year full-time programme focused on analytical concepts, techniques and their application to business decision-making, so it can be a better fit than the general PGDM for students specifically targeting analytics-oriented roles.
