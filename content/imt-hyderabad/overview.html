IMT Hyderabad PGDM: Overview

Institute of Management Technology Hyderabad (IMT Hyderabad) is a management institute established in 2011 and located at Shamshabad near Hyderabad. Its flagship PGDM is a two-year, full-time residential programme with a trimester-based structure and a strong emphasis on case-based, experiential and industry-oriented learning.

IMT Hyderabad is part of the IMT group and offers management education across general management and focused tracks in areas such as finance, marketing and logistics and supply chain management. The institute's PGDM programmes are AICTE approved, NBA accredited, SAQS accredited and have MBA equivalence from AIU.

Why aspirants shortlist IMT Hyderabad

The institute combines a residential 30-acre campus, multiple specialisation pathways, international exposure and a broad recruiter base. For the 2023-25 batch, the published placement snapshot reports ₹12.2 LPA average CTC, ₹31 LPA highest CTC and 93% placements.

IMT Hyderabad PGDM Admission 2026-28

For the 2026-28 intake, applicants use the IMT common application route and submit an accepted management entrance score. The current admission information lists CAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT as the accepted exams. Shortlisting considers more than the entrance score, followed by the institute's assessment and interview process.

IMT Hyderabad PGDM Eligibility

Applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree with the prescribed minimum academic performance and a valid score in an accepted management entrance examination. Final-year students can apply provisionally, subject to completing the degree requirements within the institute's prescribed timeline.

Academic qualification

A bachelor's degree of at least three years from a recognised university or institution.

Minimum marks

The published admission criteria use 50% aggregate marks as the minimum academic benchmark for the general PGDM route.

Entrance exam

CAT, XAT, CMAT or GMAT scores are accepted for the current admission cycle.

Final-year candidates

Students in the final year of graduation may apply, subject to completing their qualifying degree requirements.

IMT Hyderabad PGDM Selection Criteria

IMT Hyderabad follows a profile-based selection approach rather than treating the entrance percentile as the only deciding factor. Academic record, entrance performance, work experience and diversity are considered along with the institute's written assessment and interview.

Student takeaway

A strong CAT/XAT score helps, but IMT Hyderabad's process is not purely percentile-driven. Prepare for WAT and PI and be ready to explain your academic choices, career goals, work experience and business awareness.

IMT Hyderabad CAT, XAT, CMAT Cutoff

For the 2026 admission cycle, publicly available institute-linked admission information reports an overall CAT/XAT cutoff of 90 percentile for shortlisting, with no separate sectional cutoff. The institute's selection remains profile-based, so the cutoff should be treated as a screening benchmark rather than a guaranteed admission score.

IMT Hyderabad PGDM Courses & Specialisations

IMT Hyderabad's PGDM ecosystem allows students to align the programme with their intended career function. The flagship options include General Management, Finance, Marketing and Logistics & Supply Chain Management, with electives supporting broader career choices.

PGDM – General Management

A broad management route covering core business functions with foundation and elective courses aligned to career goals.

PGDM – Finance

Focused finance learning covering areas such as corporate finance, project finance, security analysis, portfolio management, investment banking and financial econometrics.

PGDM – Marketing

Marketing-focused learning across sales and distribution, digital marketing, brand management, integrated marketing communications, international marketing and marketing research.

Logistics & Supply Chain Management

A focused track for students targeting procurement, logistics, supply chain, operations and related roles, with industry-oriented learning.

IMT Hyderabad Campus & Student Life

IMT Hyderabad operates a 30-acre residential campus at Cherlaguda, Shamshabad in the Hyderabad region. The residential format creates a cohort-based environment with academic facilities, student activities, clubs, industry interaction and opportunities for international exposure.

Residential campus

The two-year PGDM is designed as a full-time residential management programme, making campus life a significant part of the learning experience.

International exposure

IMT Hyderabad promotes international student and faculty exchange through partnerships with overseas institutions.

Experiential learning

Case studies, simulations, live projects, internships and applied classroom work are integral to the learning model.

Student life

Clubs, committees, competitions, events and peer learning provide opportunities to build leadership and communication skills outside the classroom.

Is IMT Hyderabad Worth It?

IMT Hyderabad is worth considering for aspirants targeting a private B-school with a strong Hyderabad location, residential learning, recognised accreditations and a diverse recruiter base. Its 2025 placement numbers make the institute particularly relevant for candidates targeting finance, analytics, consulting, marketing and technology-linked management roles.

Consider IMT Hyderabad if...

You want a residential PGDM, are comfortable with a ₹15–16 lakh planning budget, and want access to finance, marketing, analytics, consulting and supply-chain career paths.

Think twice if...

You are primarily looking for a low-fee MBA or a college where the admission decision depends almost entirely on your entrance percentile.

ROI lens

Compare the total cost against the ₹12.2 LPA 2025 average CTC rather than judging the programme by the ₹31 LPA highest package alone.

Profile fit

Students who can combine a solid entrance score with good academics, communication skills and a clear career story can benefit from the institute's holistic selection model.
