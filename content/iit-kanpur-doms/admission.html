Eligibility

  • CAT 2025 is the qualifying examination for the 2026-28 MBA batch.
  • GEN/EWS/OBC applicants: overall CAT 80 percentile or above.
  • Class X and XII: 65% and above for GEN/EWS/OBC applicants.
  • Bachelor's degree/equivalent: 60% aggregate or CPI 6.0/10.
  • Mathematics must have been one of the subjects at 10+2.
  • Non-engineering graduates can apply if they satisfy the stated criteria.
Do not confuse eligibility with final selection

The department can decide actual category-wise shortlisting cutoffs based on completed applications and approved seats.

Selection Process

1Online application
2CAT-based shortlist
3GD + PI assessment
4Category-wise merit list

Stage 1 is application. Stage 2 uses CAT percentile for shortlisting. Stage 3 assesses shortlisted candidates through Group Discussion and Personal Interview. Stage 4 prepares final and wait lists for each category.

Gender diversity

DoMS states that it plans a gender diversity multiplier for shortlisted female applicants; the 2026 brochure proposes a 15% inflation of composite scores, capped at the maximum score.

Reservation

The published process states reservation norms of OBC(NCL) 27%, SC 15%, EWS 10%, ST 7.5% and DAP 3% horizontal reservation, subject to applicable Government of India norms.
