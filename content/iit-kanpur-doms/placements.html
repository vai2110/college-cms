Recruiter Examples

The official brochure highlights associations including ICICI Bank, AXTRIA, Wells Fargo, DS Group, Neenopal, Oxane Partners, Trident Group, Cognizant, Accenture, TVS, Sobha Realty, Infosys, IndiaMART, Deloitte, LUMIQ, EvoluteIQ, HDFC Bank and others.

Recruiter presence should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every company hires from every batch or for every role.

Placement Process

DoMS describes a structured campus placement process involving company invitations, job announcement forms, pre-placement talks, student registration, selection rounds and final offers.

  1. Company invitation and job-profile review.
  2. Job Announcement Form and pre-placement interaction.
  3. Student registration for relevant companies.
  4. Tests, interviews and/or group discussions as specified by the recruiter.
  5. Final selection and offer communication.
One-person-one-job policy

DoMS states that once a student is placed with a company, the student generally cannot participate further in the placement process, subject to its dream-company policy.

How students should read the placement numbers

  • Median is often more representative than the highest package.
  • Average CTC should be read alongside batch size and placement coverage.
  • CTC is not the same as monthly in-hand salary.
  • Top-25% data helps understand distribution better than one headline package.
  • Always compare role quality, location, function and recruiter mix.
