IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA: Overview

The Department of Management Sciences (DoMS), IIT Kanpur offers a full-time general-management MBA that combines management education with IIT Kanpur's analytical, technology and research ecosystem. The programme is spread over four semesters and a summer term and includes compulsory courses, electives, a summer project and a capstone project.

Why DoMS is different

DoMS is not a specialised MBA degree. The degree is general MBA, while students build their interest areas through electives across management and other IIT Kanpur departments. The department highlights areas such as operations, analytics, finance, marketing, economics, IT and systems, sustainability and energy policy.

For applicants, the biggest eligibility filter is Mathematics at 10+2. Non-engineers can apply, but the published criteria require Mathematics as one of the subjects and specify academic and CAT benchmarks.

GEN/EWS/OBC eligibility

  • Overall CAT 2025 percentile of 80 or above.
  • 65% or above in Class X and Class XII.
  • 60% graduation aggregate or CPI 6.0/10.
  • At least three years of education after 10+2/diploma and Mathematics as a 10+2 subject.

SC/ST eligibility

The published 2026-28 criteria specify 55 CAT percentile and 50% graduation aggregate or CPI 5.0/10, along with the Mathematics requirement.

IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA Fees: Bifurcation

Placement trend: what the numbers actually say

The latest batch shows a major jump in the highest CTC from ₹24 LPA to ₹36 LPA, while average CTC fell from ₹18.2 LPA to ₹17.2 LPA. That means the latest headline package should not be read as a broad salary jump for the entire batch. The median of ₹16.5 LPA is the better indicator of the middle of the distribution.

The 2023-25 final report says recruiter participation rose from 21 to 43 and roles covered analytics, consulting, BFSI, manufacturing, FMCG, product, finance, supply chain and operations.

Campus & Student Life

DoMS students are part of IIT Kanpur's residential campus and broader academic ecosystem. The institute provides access to a major technical and research environment rather than a standalone business-school campus.

What matters for MBA students

  • Residential IIT Kanpur campus and hostel availability.
  • P.K. Kelkar Library and wider institute infrastructure.
  • Interaction with engineering, science, technology and research communities.
  • Student activities, competitions and institute events.
  • Analytical and technology-oriented peer environment.

Pros, Cons & Decision Guide

Pros

  • IIT Kanpur brand combined with a management curriculum.
  • Strong analytical, quantitative and technology ecosystem.
  • General MBA with electives across DoMS and other IIT departments.
  • Strong recent placement distribution with a ₹16.5 LPA median in 2023-25.
  • Access to operations, analytics, finance, consulting and technology-linked roles.

Cons / limitations

  • Mathematics at 10+2 is a mandatory eligibility filter.
  • CAT is the qualifying test for the current admission route.
  • The programme is a general MBA rather than a named specialisation degree.
  • Highest CTC can overstate typical outcomes; median is lower.
  • Exact semester fees change and must be checked against the current notice.

Who should choose IIT Kanpur DoMS?

Students targeting analytics, consulting, operations, finance, product, technology-linked management or quantitatively oriented business roles are likely to benefit most. It is particularly attractive if you value the IIT ecosystem as much as the MBA curriculum.

Who should reconsider?

Applicants without Mathematics at 10+2, applicants seeking multiple entrance-test routes, or students who want a highly specialised MBA degree may find the programme less suitable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA only for engineers?
No. Non-engineers can apply. However, Mathematics must have been one of the subjects at 10+2 and the applicable academic and CAT criteria must be met.
What CAT percentile is required for IIT Kanpur DoMS?
For the 2026-28 criteria, the published overall benchmark was 80 percentile for GEN/EWS/OBC applicants and 55 percentile for SC/ST applicants. The actual shortlist cutoff can be higher because DoMS decides category-wise shortlisting based on applications and seats.
Does IIT Kanpur DoMS have sectional CAT cutoffs?
The official FAQ says no sectional cutoff is used and that overall CAT percentile is considered for shortlisting and subsequent selection.
What is the latest IIT Kanpur DoMS placement package?
For the 2023-25 MBA batch, the official report gives ₹36 LPA highest CTC, ₹17.2 LPA average CTC, ₹16.5 LPA median CTC and ₹22.1 LPA top-25% CTC.
What is the IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA fee?
DoMS releases MBA fees semester-wise. The 2026-28 application fee was ₹2,000 for General/OBC(NCL)/EWS and ₹1,000 for SC/ST/DAP. For tuition and other programme charges, applicants should use the latest official semester fee notice.
Is work experience compulsory for IIT Kanpur MBA?
No. Work experience is not mandatory, but it contributes 10% to the final composite score in the published selection criteria.
What is the MBA course structure at IIT Kanpur?
The programme has four semesters and a summer term, with core courses, electives, a compulsory summer project and a capstone project. The published curriculum structure totals 216 credits.
