IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA: Overview

The Department of Management Sciences (DoMS), IIT Kanpur offers a full-time MBA designed around management education with the analytical and technical ecosystem of IIT Kanpur. The programme runs for four semesters plus a summer term and includes compulsory courses, electives and a capstone project.

Important for applicants

For the 2026-28 batch, DoMS required CAT 2025. The official admission process states an overall CAT percentile of 80 or above for GEN/EWS/OBC applicants, along with Class X and XII and graduation requirements. This is an eligibility/shortlisting benchmark, not a guaranteed final-call cutoff.

The curriculum covers Marketing, Finance, HRM, Economics, Operations, IT, Systems, Service Management, Public Policy, Sustainability and Energy Policy. Non-engineers can apply, but Mathematics at 10+2 is a stated requirement.

CAT Cutoff: What the official data means

DoMS officially states 80 percentile or above in CAT 2025 for GEN/EWS/OBC applicants as the benchmark used for eligibility and shortlisting. The department also says the actual shortlist cut-off can be decided based on the number of eligible applications and approved seats.

Therefore, students should not treat 80 percentile as a safe-call score. For planning, compare your CAT score with the profile strength of recent applicants and keep multiple IIT/IIM options.

Campus & Student Life

IIT Kanpur's campus provides a strong technology, research and entrepreneurship environment. DoMS students also benefit from the wider IIT ecosystem, including the P.K. Kelkar Library and institute infrastructure.

For MBA applicants, the key differentiator is the combination of management training with access to a leading technical institute rather than a conventional standalone B-school campus.

Who should choose DoMS IIT Kanpur?

Good fit if

  • You want an IIT-brand MBA with strong analytical orientation.
  • You have a strong CAT profile and quantitative comfort.
  • You want access to technology, engineering and research ecosystems.
  • You are interested in analytics, operations, finance or technology-linked management.

Reconsider if

  • You do not have Mathematics in 10+2.
  • You are targeting a programme where admission is based on several entrance tests.
  • You want a very large conventional MBA specialization portfolio.
  • Your decision depends only on the highest advertised package.

FAQs

Can a non-engineer apply for IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA?
Yes. DoMS states that non-engineers can apply. However, Mathematics as one of the subjects in 10+2 is required.
Which entrance exam is accepted for the MBA?
For the 2026-28 batch, DoMS accepted CAT 2025 only.
Is 80 CAT percentile enough for IIT Kanpur MBA?
80 percentile is the official benchmark stated for GEN/EWS/OBC eligibility and shortlisting. It is not a guaranteed final-call score because the department can set category cutoffs based on applications and seats.
What is the latest IIT Kanpur DoMS placement package?
The official MBA 2026 brochure reports ₹36 lakh highest CTC, ₹17.2 lakh average CTC and ₹16.5 lakh median CTC for the 2023-25 batch.
What is the duration of the MBA?
The programme is spread over four semesters and a summer term.
Does IIT Kanpur DoMS have a summer term?
Yes. The official admission notice describes the programme as four semesters plus a summer term, with compulsory and elective coursework and a capstone project.
Where are the MBA interviews conducted?
For the 2026-28 cycle, the official schedule listed interview dates of 13-16 March 2026 in Noida and Kanpur.
