Official CAT Cutoff Benchmark

For GEN, EWS and OBC applicants in the 2026-28 admission process, DoMS states an overall CAT 2025 percentile of 80 or above. The same published criteria include 65% or above in Class X and XII and 60% graduation aggregate or CPI 6.0/10.

Important

DoMS explicitly says the department will decide category-wise cut-off percentiles for shortlisting based on the total number of eligible and completed applications and approved seats. Therefore, 80 is not a guaranteed interview-call score.

Other Profile Requirements

  • Class X: 65%+ for GEN/EWS/OBC in the published criteria.
  • Class XII: 65%+ for GEN/EWS/OBC.
  • Graduation: 60%+ or CPI 6.0/10.
  • Mathematics must be one of the 10+2 subjects.
  • Category-specific criteria are considered separately under the admission process.

How to use the cutoff while planning

Strong CAT profile

If your percentile is comfortably above the published benchmark, focus on GD/PI preparation and academic-profile documentation.

Near the benchmark

Do not rely on IIT Kanpur alone. Keep a broader B-school list because the actual shortlist threshold can move.

Also remember that a CAT percentile by itself does not complete the DoMS selection process. The official process moves from CAT-based shortlisting to GD/PI and then category-wise merit preparation.
