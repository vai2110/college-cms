MBA Course Structure

DoMS describes the MBA as a full-time programme spread across four semesters and a summer term. The published curriculum structure has 216 total credits and combines core courses, electives, a compulsory summer project, capstone work and management seminars.

Electives & Academic Areas

Students can take elective courses from DoMS and other IIT Kanpur departments. This makes the MBA broader than a fixed specialisation programme.

Finance

Finance, accounting, investment and analytical decision-making subjects can support finance-oriented careers.

Marketing & Product

Marketing, consumer, product and business-development interests can be developed through relevant electives.

Analytics & Technology

Quantitative methods, analytics, IT and systems subjects suit technology-linked management roles.

Operations & Supply Chain

Operations, systems, supply chain and decision sciences are strong fits with the DoMS analytical orientation.

HR & Organisation

Organisation design, HR and people-management subjects support people and organisation roles.

Policy & Sustainability

Public policy, sustainability and energy-policy areas broaden the management application space.

Summer Project & Capstone

Summer project

The summer project is compulsory during the summer break after the second semester and carries 10 credits. It provides an opportunity to apply classroom learning to a practical problem.

Capstone project

DoMS says the capstone was implemented after feedback from industry, faculty and alumni. Its objectives include integrating functional knowledge, addressing real-life problems and creating useful solutions for companies while incorporating sustainability and innovation.

Learning Model & Academic Experience

The official FAQ says the curriculum uses a mix of conceptual learning and practical exposure to real-time industry problems rather than being entirely case-study based. DoMS also states that its management education leverages IIT Kanpur's technical and analytical strengths.

Who will enjoy this curriculum?

Students who like quantitative decision-making, technology, operations, analytics, research and structured problem-solving are likely to find the academic environment particularly relevant.

Course FAQs

Is IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA a specialised MBA?
No. DoMS states that it is a general management MBA. Students build their preferred functional profile through electives from DoMS and other IIT Kanpur departments.
How many credits are there in the MBA?
The published course structure totals 216 credits, including core courses, electives, the summer project, capstone project and management seminars.
Is the summer project compulsory?
Yes. DoMS lists a compulsory summer project after the second semester carrying 10 credits.
Can MBA students take courses outside DoMS?
Yes. The published curriculum says students can take elective courses from DoMS and other departments of IIT Kanpur.
Is the curriculum completely case-study based?
No. DoMS says the curriculum combines conceptual learning with practical exposure to real-time industry problems.
What careers does the curriculum support?
The curriculum can support analytics, consulting, finance, operations, supply chain, marketing, product and technology-linked management careers, depending on electives, internships and placement outcomes.
