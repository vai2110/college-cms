Programme Structure

The official DoMS admission notice describes the MBA as a full-time programme spread over four semesters and a summer term. The curriculum combines compulsory courses, elective courses and a capstone project in the participant's area of interest.

Business functions

Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management and Operations.

Analytical & technology

IT, Systems, Economics and technology-linked management topics.

Public Policy, Sustainability and Energy Policy.

Applied learning

Electives and a capstone project allow students to build an interest-area profile.

Why the IIT Kanpur ecosystem matters

DoMS explicitly positions its management education as complementary to IIT Kanpur's core technical strengths. For students interested in analytics, operations, technology, finance or research-led decision-making, this ecosystem can be a meaningful differentiator.

Student perspective

The value proposition is not simply the IIT name. Applicants should evaluate curriculum fit, role outcomes, batch profile, total cost and their own CAT profile together.

Career Fit

  • Analytics and technology-oriented management roles
  • Consulting and business problem-solving
  • Finance and corporate roles
  • Operations and supply-chain management
  • Marketing and product-linked roles
  • Policy, sustainability and energy-related management work
