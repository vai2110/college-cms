Programme Fee: How to verify the exact payable amount

DoMS provides an MBA Fee resource alongside its admission notice. Because institute fees can be revised by academic year and category, this page deliberately does not freeze an unverified tuition total.

Hostel, Mess & Living Costs

Applicants should budget beyond tuition. The total cost of attendance can include hostel accommodation, mess/food, refundable deposits, medical/insurance or institute charges, books, travel and personal expenses.

Cost headWhat to check
HostelRoom allocation, hostel fee and any one-time deposit.
MessMess charges and payment schedule.
AcademicTuition, institute charges and semester-wise installments.
PersonalLaptop, travel, stationery, activities and daily expenses.

Budget Checklist

  • Application fee
  • Admission/seat confirmation amount, if applicable
  • Semester tuition and institute charges
  • Hostel and mess
  • Refundable deposits
  • Books, laptop and academic supplies
  • Travel between home and campus

Fees vs Placement ROI

The official 2026 brochure reports a 2023-25 average CTC of ₹17.2 lakh, median CTC of ₹16.5 lakh and highest CTC of ₹36 lakh. These are CTC figures, not take-home salaries, so students should compare total MBA cost against realistic post-MBA compensation rather than the highest package alone.
