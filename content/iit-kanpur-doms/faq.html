Frequently Asked Questions

Which CAT score is accepted for IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA?
For the 2026-28 batch, DoMS required CAT 2025 and stated an overall CAT percentile of 80 or above for GEN/EWS/OBC applicants. The actual shortlist cutoff can be decided by the department based on applications and seats.
Is Mathematics compulsory for IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA?
Yes. The official FAQ states that the bachelor's qualification must follow at least three years of education after 10+2 and that Mathematics must have been one of the subjects in 10+2. Candidates without Mathematics at 10+2 are not eligible for the MBA programme.
Can a non-engineer apply to DoMS IIT Kanpur?
Yes. DoMS explicitly says the MBA is not restricted to engineering graduates. Non-engineers can apply if they satisfy the stated eligibility criteria, including the Mathematics requirement.
Is 80 percentile a safe CAT score for IIT Kanpur?
No. It is the published benchmark in the 2026-28 criteria, not a guaranteed interview-call score. The department can set actual category-wise shortlisting cutoffs based on the application pool and approved seats.
What is the IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA fee?
The application fee for the 2026-28 process was ₹2,000 for General/OBC(NCL) and ₹1,000 for EWS/SC/ST/DAP candidates. For the programme fee, use the latest official DoMS fee notice because academic-year charges can change.
What is the latest IIT Kanpur DoMS placement package?
The official MBA 2026 brochure reports ₹36 lakh highest CTC, ₹17.2 lakh average CTC, ₹16.5 lakh median CTC and ₹22.1 lakh top-25% CTC for the 2023-25 batch.
When and where were the 2026-28 MBA interviews?
The published 2026-28 schedule listed GD/PI dates of 13-16 March 2026 at Noida and Kanpur.
