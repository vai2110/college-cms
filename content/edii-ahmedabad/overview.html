EDII Ahmedabad PGDM: Overview

The Entrepreneurship Development Institute of India (EDII) is a specialised institute focused on entrepreneurship education, research, training and ecosystem development. Its flagship PGDM in Entrepreneurship (PGDM-E) is a two-year, full-time programme designed for aspiring entrepreneurs, family-business successors, social innovators and entrepreneurial managers.

For MBA aspirants, EDII is a different proposition from a conventional placement-led B-school. The programme is built around venture creation, family-business growth, social entrepreneurship and practical entrepreneurial learning, while still covering core management subjects such as finance, marketing, HR, strategy, operations and analytics.

What makes EDII different?

The 2026-28 PGDM-E has four pathways: New Enterprise Creation, Family Business Management, Social Entrepreneurship and the new Tech Startup pathway. The programme uses milestone-based learning, mentoring, venture projects and a summer internship rather than treating entrepreneurship as a single elective.

EDII Ahmedabad PGDM Admission 2026-28

EDII's official PGDM-E page lists the 2026-28 batch and describes admission through a recognised management entrance score followed by the Entrepreneurial Aptitude Test (EAT) and Personal Interview. The institute accepts several national-level management entrance examinations, so CAT is not mandatory.

EDII Ahmedabad PGDM Eligibility

For Indian applicants, EDII requires a recognised bachelor's degree with the prescribed minimum marks and a valid management entrance examination score. Final-year students are also eligible, subject to completing the degree requirements before the programme begins.

Academic qualification

3-year bachelor's degree under 10+2+3 or a 4-year degree under 10+2+4 in the specified recognised format.

Minimum marks

50% marks for general candidates and 45% for candidates from reserved categories, as published by EDII.

Entrance exam

CAT, MAT, XAT, ATMA, CMAT, GMAT or CUET is accepted. CAT is not compulsory.

Final-year students

Final-year candidates can apply, provided degree requirements are completed before commencement of the programme.

EDII PGDM Selection Criteria

EDII does not publish the selection as a simple CAT-percentile cutoff model. The process combines a recognised management entrance score with the institute's Entrepreneurial Aptitude Test and a Personal Interview.

Student takeaway

If you are applying to EDII, preparation should go beyond the management entrance exam. Be ready to explain the business problem you want to solve, why entrepreneurship interests you and how you think about customers, competition, execution and risk.

EDII Ahmedabad Courses

EDII's on-campus management portfolio includes PGDM-E and PGDM-IEV. PGDM-E is broader and is designed for students who may be exploring entrepreneurship, family business or social enterprise; PGDM-IEV is more focused on innovation-led ventures and students with a startup idea or early-stage venture.

PGDM – Entrepreneurship

Two-year full-time programme with New Enterprise Creation, Family Business Management, Social Entrepreneurship and Tech Startup pathways.

PGDM – IEV

Two-year full-time programme focused on innovation, entrepreneurship and venture development, with incubation-oriented learning.

Functional electives

Students can study areas including Marketing, Finance, International Business, HRM, Start-up and Business Analytics & Information Systems.

Practical learning

The PGDM-E curriculum includes Startup Immersion, industry verticals, summer internship, project work and venture-oriented deliverables.

EDII Ahmedabad Placements & Career Outcomes

EDII's PGDM-E is not positioned as a conventional placement-first MBA. The institute explicitly states that the programme is intended for entrepreneurship, family-business succession, social ventures and entrepreneurial career paths. Traditional placement is available as a career option, but EDII does not present a guaranteed placement or a single headline package as the core outcome.

What career paths are relevant?

Graduates can pursue new venture creation, family-business leadership, social entrepreneurship, innovation-led roles and employment in organisations that value entrepreneurial problem-solving. EDII also provides internship facilitation, alumni networking, corporate linkages, venture support and career guidance.

ROI reality check

For EDII, ROI should not be evaluated only through an average-placement-package calculation. A better comparison is the total programme cost against the entrepreneurial ecosystem, mentoring, incubation access and the career path you actually want.

EDII Ahmedabad Campus & Student Life

EDII's campus is at Bhat in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. The institute combines academic learning with venture development, mentoring and an entrepreneurship ecosystem. Students have access to residential facilities, student activities and the wider EDII incubation and entrepreneurship network.

Residential campus

On-campus hostel facilities are available. EDII publishes a hostel fee of ₹80,000 per year for the programme.

CrAdLE incubation ecosystem

CrAdLE, EDII's technology business incubator, supports early-stage ventures with mentoring, advisory and ecosystem connections.

Mentoring

Students interact with faculty, entrepreneurs, industry practitioners, business leaders, venture capitalists and bankers.

Student activities

EDII encourages student-driven activities across entrepreneurship, marketing, finance, social and cultural interests.

Is EDII Ahmedabad PGDM Worth It?

EDII is worth considering if your objective is to build a venture, take over or transform a family business, develop a social enterprise or work in an innovation-led environment. Its strongest differentiator is that entrepreneurship is the central learning model rather than a small part of a general MBA curriculum.

Consider EDII if...

You want entrepreneurship, family business, startup or innovation exposure and are comfortable with a career model that is not built only around campus placements.

Think twice if...

Your only goal is a conventional corporate MBA with a predictable placement pipeline and a package-led ROI comparison.

Strong fit for...

First-generation entrepreneurs, family-business successors, startup aspirants and candidates interested in social or technology entrepreneurship.

Key differentiator

Milestone-based venture learning, specialised entrepreneurial pathways, mentoring and access to EDII's wider ecosystem.

EDII Ahmedabad PGDM FAQs

Is EDII Ahmedabad's PGDM equivalent to an MBA?

Yes. EDII states that its PGDM-E is recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA, in addition to being AICTE approved and NBA accredited.

Is CAT compulsory for EDII PGDM-E?

No. EDII accepts CAT, MAT, XAT, ATMA, CMAT, GMAT and CUET. A candidate needs a valid score in an accepted management entrance examination.

What is the EDII PGDM-E fee for 2026-28?

The published non-residential total is ₹13.65 lakh. The residential double-sharing total is ₹15.55 lakh, with hostel fee and specified deposits incorporated in the residential schedule.

What is the EDII PGDM-E selection process?

Selection combines a recognised management entrance score with the Entrepreneurial Aptitude Test and Personal Interview. EDII describes the final decision as a holistic assessment.

What is the EDII PGDM-E cutoff?

EDII does not publish a single fixed 2026-28 percentile cutoff on its PGDM-E programme page. It specifies accepted entrance exams and eligibility requirements instead.

Does EDII provide placements?

Yes, EDII provides internship facilitation, alumni networking, corporate linkages, venture support and career guidance. However, the programme is not presented as a placement-guaranteed MBA; entrepreneurship is a central career outcome.

What specialisations are available in PGDM-E?

The 2026-27 onward programme has four pathways: New Enterprise Creation, Family Business Management, Social Entrepreneurship and Tech Startup.

Can students without a business background apply to EDII?

Yes. EDII states that a business background is not mandatory. Students from different academic disciplines can apply if they meet the eligibility and entrance-exam requirements.
