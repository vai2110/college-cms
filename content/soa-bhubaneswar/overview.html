SOA University Bhubaneswar: Courses, Fees, Admission 2026 & Placements

SOA University, officially Siksha 'O' Anusandhan, is a deemed-to-be university in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. It offers programmes across engineering, management, pharmacy, nursing, law, medicine, dentistry, agriculture, veterinary sciences, hospitality, computer applications and sciences. The official 2025-26 programme list confirms this multidisciplinary structure.

SOA Admission Process

SAAT is not the admission test for every SOA programme. SAAT covers several programmes. B.Tech can also use JEE Main, while MBBS and BDS use NEET and the prescribed counselling route.

SOA Placements

SOA's placement outcomes differ by discipline. In NIRF 2025 data, Engineering reported 1,802 placements among 1,903 UG graduates in 2023-24 with a median salary of ₹6 lakh. Management reported 175 placements among 180 graduates with a ₹5.20 lakh median. Medical UG data reported 148 placements among 148 graduates in 2022-23 with a ₹9 lakh median. These are median figures, not highest packages.

FAQs

What courses are offered at SOA University Bhubaneswar?

SOA offers programmes in engineering and technology, management, pharmacy, nursing, law, medical and dental sciences, agriculture, veterinary sciences, hospitality, computer applications, sciences and research.

What entrance exam is required for SOA?

It depends on the programme. SAAT is used for several programmes, JEE Main is accepted for applicable B.Tech admissions, and NEET is required for MBBS and BDS through the prescribed counselling route.

Is SOA a university or college?

SOA is a deemed-to-be university in Bhubaneswar, Odisha.

What is the SOA University placement package?

There is no single package that represents every SOA course. NIRF reports discipline-specific median salaries; the latest figures used here are ₹6 lakh for Engineering (2023-24), ₹5.20 lakh for Management (2023-24) and ₹9 lakh for UG Medical (2022-23).

Does SOA accept JEE Main?

Yes, SOA provides a JEE Main route for applicable B.Tech admissions.

Is NEET required for MBBS and BDS at SOA?

Yes. SOA states that MBBS and BDS admissions are through NEET and the prescribed counselling route.

What should I compare before choosing SOA?

Compare the specific course's fee, eligibility, admission route, intake, curriculum, internship/career route and discipline-specific placement data rather than using the university-wide reputation alone.
