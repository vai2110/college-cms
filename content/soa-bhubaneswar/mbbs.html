SOA MBBS: Fees, Admission 2026, Eligibility & Seats

SOA offers MBBS through the Institute of Medical Sciences and SUM Hospital, Bhubaneswar.

SOA MBBS Admission 2026

Entrance exam: NEET. SOA states that MBBS admission is through NEET and the prescribed DGHS counselling route. SAAT is not the MBBS admission route.

SOA MBBS Eligibility

Candidates must meet the applicable 10+2 PCB eligibility and qualify NEET under the prevailing medical-admission rules.

SOA MBBS Seats

SOA reported an approved UG intake of 250 seats for 2023-24 in its NIRF 2025 Medical submission. This is an older reported intake and should not be presented as the 2026 sanctioned intake without current confirmation.

SOA MBBS Fees 2026-27

The official Institute of Medical Sciences fee structure lists ₹22.95 lakh per annum for MBBS for 2026-27, with additional applicable charges.

SOA MBBS Placement / Career Outcome

NIRF 2025 reports 148 of 148 UG medical graduates placed in 2022-23, with a median salary of ₹9 lakh. Medical outcomes should be read separately from engineering and management placement data.
