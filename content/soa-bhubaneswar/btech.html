SOA B.Tech: Fees, Admission 2026, Eligibility & Placements

SOA B.Tech is offered by the Institute of Technical Education and Research (ITER). The 2025-26 programme list includes Civil, CSE, CSE-AI & ML, CSE-Cyber Security, CSE-Data Science, CSE-IoT, CSIT, Electrical, EEE, ECE and Mechanical Engineering.

SOA B.Tech Eligibility 2026

Eligibility is branch-specific. CSE and its listed specialisations require 10+2 with the prescribed Physics/Mathematics requirements and minimum aggregate; other branches have their own subject combinations. Use the current 2026 eligibility page before applying.

How to Get Admission to SOA B.Tech

SOA accepts applications through its admission process and also publishes B.Tech admission rounds for JEE Main candidates. SAAT is another route used for applicable programmes. Check the specific 2026 round before submitting documents.

SOA B.Tech Fees 2026

The published SOA fee structure is branch-specific. The latest 2026 admission portal should be treated as the controlling source for the payable academic fee, hostel, mess and other charges.

SOA B.Tech Placements

NIRF 2025 reports 1,802 placements among 1,903 engineering graduates in 2023-24, with a median salary of ₹6 lakh. This is a discipline-level median and should not be presented as the highest package for every B.Tech branch.

FAQs

Does SOA accept JEE Main for B.Tech?

Yes. SOA publishes B.Tech admission rounds for JEE Main candidates.

Which B.Tech branch is available at SOA?

SOA offers CSE and multiple CSE specialisations along with ECE, EEE, Electrical, Mechanical and Civil Engineering.

What is the SOA B.Tech placement salary?

The latest NIRF figure available in the 2025 Engineering submission is a ₹6 lakh median salary for the 2023-24 graduating cohort.

Is SAAT required for SOA B.Tech?

SOA uses its own admission process for applicable programmes and also accepts JEE Main for B.Tech. The applicable route should be checked for the admission round.
