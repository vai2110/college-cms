SOA B.Sc Nursing: Admission 2026, Eligibility, Fees & Career

SOA offers B.Sc Nursing (Basic) through its nursing faculty and also lists Post Basic B.Sc Nursing and M.Sc Nursing programmes.

SOA B.Sc Nursing Eligibility 2026

SOA's 2026 criteria require 10+2 with PCB and English and the prescribed aggregate. The published criteria also specify age conditions for the 2026 batch.

SOA B.Sc Nursing Admission

B.Sc Nursing is among the programmes offered through SAAT. SOA's 2026 admission updates include B.Sc Nursing results through later SAAT rounds.

SOA B.Sc Nursing Fees

Use SOA's current 2026 fee-structure page for the programme fee and additional charges. No unsupported fee is stated here.

Career After B.Sc Nursing

Graduates can pursue professional nursing roles in hospitals and healthcare settings or continue with postgraduate nursing study.

FAQs

What percentage is required for B.Sc Nursing at SOA?

SOA's 2026 criteria specify the required PCB and English aggregate, with category-wise thresholds. Check the official 2026 eligibility page before applying.

Is B.Sc Nursing at SOA through SAAT?

Yes. B.Sc Nursing is included in SOA's SAAT programme list.

Is NEET the admission route for SOA B.Sc Nursing?

SOA places B.Sc Nursing under its SAAT admission system rather than the MBBS/BDS NEET route.
