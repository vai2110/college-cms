SOA BDS: Fees, Admission 2026, Eligibility, Seats & NEET

SOA's Institute of Dental Sciences offers the Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) in Bhubaneswar.

SOA BDS Admission 2026

Entrance exam: NEET. BDS admission follows the prescribed NEET counselling route; SAAT is not the BDS admission test.

SOA BDS Eligibility

Candidates must meet the applicable 10+2 PCB requirements and qualify NEET under current dental-admission rules.

SOA BDS Seats

NIRF 2025 reported an approved intake of 100 UG seats for 2023-24. Treat this as the reported NIRF year-specific intake, not automatically the 2026 sanctioned intake.

SOA BDS Fees 2026-27

The official SOA fee structure lists a ₹6 lakh annual course fee for 2026-27, plus applicable caution and examination charges.

SOA BDS Internship

The published 2026-27 fee information lists an internship stipend of ₹13,000 per month for one year.

FAQs

Is NEET required for BDS at SOA?

Yes. BDS admission is through NEET and the prescribed counselling route.

What is SOA BDS fees?

The 2026-27 course fee is ₹6 lakh per annum, excluding applicable additional charges.

How many BDS seats are there at SOA?

NIRF 2025 reported 100 approved UG seats for 2023-24. Verify the current sanctioned intake for 2026.
