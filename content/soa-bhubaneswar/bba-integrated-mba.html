SOA BBA & Integrated MBA: Admission 2026, Fees & Eligibility

SOA offers BBA and a five-year Integrated MBA through the Institute of Business and Computer Studies.

Eligibility

SOA's management admission criteria require 10+2 in any stream with the prescribed aggregate, with category-wise relaxation where applicable.

Admission 2026

BBA and Integrated MBA are among the programmes covered by SOA's SAAT admission system. The 2026 admission calendar records programme-specific results and later rounds.

Fees

SOA's published fee information lists BBA at ₹1.90 lakh per year and Integrated MBA at ₹2.50 lakh per year. Verify the 2026 fee schedule and additional charges before payment.

FAQs

What is SOA BBA eligibility?

10+2 in any stream with the prescribed aggregate under SOA's 2026 management criteria.

What is SOA BBA fees?

The published annual fee is ₹1.90 lakh, subject to the current 2026 fee schedule and additional charges.

What is SOA Integrated MBA fees?

Which is better, BBA or Integrated MBA?

BBA suits students wanting an undergraduate management degree with flexibility for later postgraduate study; Integrated MBA suits students seeking a continuous five-year management route.
