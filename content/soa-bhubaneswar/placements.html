SOA Medical Placement Trends

UG medical median salary was ₹8.40 lakh in 2021-22 and 2022-23, then ₹9 lakh in the latest reported UG year. PG medical data for 2023-24 shows 106 placements among 106 graduates and a ₹12 lakh median.

SOA Management Placement Trends

Management median salary increased from ₹4.20 lakh in 2021-22 to ₹4.80 lakh in 2022-23 and ₹5.20 lakh in 2023-24. The latest reported cohort had 175 placements among 180 graduates.

Highest Package vs Median Salary

Do not treat the highest package as the typical outcome. The NIRF figures above are median salaries. Median salary indicates the middle reported outcome and is more useful for comparing typical placement performance than an exceptional highest offer.

SOA Placement Process

SOA's placement function conducts industry-facing recruitment and engagement activities. The exact process, eligible students and participating employers can vary by institute and graduating batch.

FAQs

What is the SOA University placement package?

There is no single package for every SOA programme. The latest NIRF medians used here are ₹6 lakh for Engineering, ₹5.20 lakh for Management and ₹9 lakh for UG Medical, with different reporting years.

What is the SOA B.Tech placement salary?

The latest NIRF Engineering median is ₹6 lakh for the 2023-24 graduating cohort.

What is the SOA MBA placement salary?

The NIRF 2025 Management submission reports a ₹5.20 lakh median salary for 2023-24.

What is the SOA MBBS placement salary?

NIRF reports a ₹9 lakh median for UG medical graduates in 2022-23.

How many engineering students were placed at SOA?

1,802 of 1,903 engineering graduates were reported placed in 2023-24.

Does SOA placement data include all courses?

No single NIRF category covers every SOA programme. Placement outcomes should be read using the relevant discipline or programme data.
