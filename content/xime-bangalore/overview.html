XIME Bangalore Overview

Xavier Institute of Management & Entrepreneurship (XIME) is a management institute at Electronics City, Phase II, Bengaluru. Its flagship PGDM is a two-year, full-time residential programme, while the Bangalore campus also offers a dedicated PGDM in Business Analytics. XIME describes the PGDM as AICTE-approved and the Bangalore PGDM is NBA accredited; the PGDM programme also has AIU recognition.

Why students shortlist XIME Bangalore: It combines a residential management programme, Bengaluru's business ecosystem, experiential learning, internships, multiple entrance-test routes and a relatively transparent published selection formula.

XIME's current programme page lists 240 PGDM seats at Bangalore and 60 PGDM-BA seats. The programme includes case studies, simulations, live projects, internships, mentoring, student clubs and industry interaction. citeturn1search2turn1search3

XIME Bangalore Admission Process 2026

Check eligibility.
A bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks is required; 45% applies for SC/ST candidates. Final-year students can apply subject to XIME's completion and proof requirements. Applicants with backlogs are not eligible.
Take an accepted test.
XIME accepts CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, MAT or ATMA for Bangalore.
Apply online.
The current application fee is ₹2,000. An additional ₹250 applies if a second preferred XIME campus is selected.
Attend GD & PI.
Shortlisted candidates are called for the Group Discussion and Personal Interview stage in the process specified by XIME.
Final selection.
The published weightage is 35% admission-test score, 40% GD/Interview, 13% academic performance and 12% sports, extracurriculars, academic diversity, gender diversity and work experience.
Accept the offer.
Selected students confirm their seat by paying the applicable fee within the deadline communicated by XIME.
Eligibility in simple terms: A 50% bachelor's degree is the standard benchmark, but your entrance score is only 35% of the published selection formula. GD/PI carries the largest 40% share, so interview preparation matters significantly. citeturn1search0turn1search5

Campus & Student Life

XIME Bangalore is located in Electronics City, Phase II, Bengaluru. The institute describes the programme as fully residential and highlights smart classrooms, Wi-Fi, a large library, Bloomberg terminals, analytics labs, gym, medical facilities and sports infrastructure.

Experiential learning

Case studies, simulations, live projects, market-based projects, internships, mentoring and industry interactions form a major part of the learning model.

Student ecosystem

Should You Choose XIME Bangalore?

Good fit if...

You want a residential PGDM in Bengaluru, value experiential learning, are comfortable with a ₹12.90 lakh programme fee, and want a broad management route or a specialised analytics route.

Reconsider if...

Your priority is a very low-cost MBA, you need a non-residential programme, or your target role requires placement outcomes materially above the ₹10–11 LPA average range published for XIME Bangalore.

Decision tip: Compare XIME with alternatives using four numbers together — total cost, average/median CTC, role quality and percentage of eligible students placed — rather than comparing highest packages alone.

XIME Bangalore FAQs

Is XIME Bangalore good for PGDM?
XIME Bangalore is a credible option for students seeking a two-year residential PGDM in Bengaluru. It is AICTE-approved, the Bangalore PGDM is NBA accredited, and XIME reports a Class of 2025 Bangalore PGDM average CTC of ₹10.6 LPA. Whether it is “good” for you depends on your budget, target role and the alternatives you can convert.
What is the XIME Bangalore fee for 2026–28?
The current official programme fee is ₹12.90 lakh for both PGDM and PGDM-BA at Bangalore, split as ₹6.70 lakh in Year 1 and ₹6.20 lakh in Year 2. A ₹25,000 refundable general caution deposit and ₹10,000 refundable hostel deposit are separate.
Which entrance exams are accepted by XIME Bangalore?
XIME accepts CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, MAT and ATMA for the Bangalore campus. KMAT is listed only for Kochi and TANCET only for Chennai.
What is the XIME Bangalore cutoff?
XIME does not publish a single fixed official percentile cutoff for Bangalore. Its selection formula gives 35% weight to the entrance-test score, 40% to GD and interview, 13% to academics and 12% to extracurriculars, diversity and work experience. Therefore, third-party cutoff numbers should not be treated as official guaranteed thresholds.
What is the average package at XIME Bangalore?
For the Class of 2025, XIME reports ₹10.6 LPA average CTC for Bangalore PGDM and ₹11.2 LPA for Bangalore PGDM-BA. The combined XIME-campus average was ₹10.25 LPA.
Is XIME Bangalore residential?
Yes. XIME describes its PGDM as a fully residential programme, with separate hostels for men and women. Hostel charges are published separately from the programme fee.
Does XIME Bangalore offer Business Analytics?
Yes. XIME Bangalore offers a two-year PGDM in Business Analytics with a published intake of 60. The programme combines management with analytics, AI and technology and has reported stronger top-10% CTC outcomes than the regular PGDM in the latest published data.
