XIME Bangalore Overview

Xavier Institute of Management & Entrepreneurship (XIME) is a management institute in Electronics City, Bengaluru. Its flagship PGDM is a two-year, full-time residential programme with a broad management foundation and specialisations in Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and IT & Analytics. XIME also offers a dedicated PGDM in Business Analytics at Bangalore.

Who should consider XIME Bangalore? Students looking for a residential PGDM with a relatively broad management curriculum, experiential learning, internships and access to Bengaluru's business ecosystem. XIME describes its PGDM as AICTE-approved and highlights NBA accreditation for the Bangalore PGDM.

Important Admission Information

XIME currently lists admissions open for the 2026–28 PGDM and PGDM-BA batch. The institute says applications open from November every year. Unlike institutes that depend on one entrance examination, XIME accepts multiple national management-test scores.

Programmes at XIME Bangalore

PGDM

Two-year, full-time, residential and AICTE-approved. Bangalore intake is 240. Core management education covers areas such as Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and Analytics, with specialisation beginning in Year 2. The programme includes an 8-week Summer Internship and a 3-week SUPA social-impact internship.

PGDM in Business Analytics

Two-year, full-time, residential programme offered exclusively at XIME Bangalore with an annual intake of 60. It blends management with analytics, AI and technology. The curriculum includes Python, data handling, data science, visualisation, business analytics and related technology electives.

Admission Process

Check eligibility.
A bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks is required; the minimum is 45% for SC/ST candidates. Final-year students can apply subject to XIME's stated completion and proof requirements. Applicants with backlogs are not eligible.
Submit an accepted entrance-test score.
XIME accepts CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, MAT or ATMA for Bangalore. Applicants can use any one valid score.
Apply online to XIME.
Applications are submitted online. The current application fee is ₹2,000, with ₹250 extra for selecting another preferred campus.
Shortlisting.
XIME shortlists applicants for the next stage using its admission-test and profile evaluation process.
Group Discussion and Personal Interview.
Shortlisted candidates attend GD and PI in the locations/process specified by XIME.
Final selection.
XIME's published weightage is 35% for the admission-test score, 40% for GD and interview, 13% for academic performance and 12% for extracurriculars, diversity and work experience.
Offer and fee payment.
Selected candidates confirm their seat by paying the applicable fee within the deadline communicated in the offer.
Is there a separate management-quota route? XIME's official admission information describes a prescribed online application, test-score based shortlisting, GD/PI and final selection process. Students should not treat third-party claims of guaranteed/direct admission as an official XIME route.

Campus & Student Life

XIME Bangalore is located in Electronics City, Phase II, Hosur Road. The PGDM is fully residential, with separate hostels for men and women. XIME highlights smart classrooms, Wi-Fi, a library with 70,000+ titles, Bloomberg Terminals, analytics labs, gym, medical facilities and sports facilities.

Students also participate in 15+ clubs and committees covering areas such as HR, marketing, finance, operations and entrepreneurship. The programme includes live projects, market-based projects, internships, international learner-exchange opportunities and industry interactions.

Analytics-focused student? XIME's PGDM-BA is the more direct fit because the programme explicitly combines management with Python, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, data science, AI/ML and other analytics/technology components.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is XIME Bangalore a good college for PGDM?
XIME Bangalore can be a relevant option for students seeking a residential PGDM with a broad management curriculum, experiential learning and access to Bengaluru's corporate ecosystem. The decision should be based on your entrance score, preferred specialisation, total cost and the latest placement outcomes rather than the institute name alone.
What is the XIME Bangalore PGDM fee?
For the 2026–28 batch, XIME lists ₹12.90 lakh as the Bangalore programme fee, payable in six instalments: ₹6.70 lakh in Year 1 and ₹6.20 lakh in Year 2. Refundable general and hostel caution deposits are additional.
Which entrance exams are accepted by XIME Bangalore?
XIME accepts CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, MAT and ATMA for Bangalore. KMAT is listed specifically for Kochi and TANCET specifically for Chennai.
What is the eligibility for XIME Bangalore PGDM?
Candidates need a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks; the minimum is 45% for SC/ST candidates. Final-year candidates can apply subject to XIME's completion and documentation conditions. Applicants with backlogs are not eligible.
What is the average package at XIME Bangalore?
For the Class of 2025, XIME reports an average CTC of ₹10.6 LPA for Bangalore PGDM and ₹11.2 LPA for Bangalore PGDM-BA. XIME's combined-campus average was ₹10.25 LPA.
What is the highest package at XIME Bangalore?
XIME's latest published Class of 2025 placement highlights report a highest CTC of ₹23.7 LPA across XIME campuses. The placement page separately gives Bangalore programme-level averages and top-percentile figures but does not attribute the ₹23.7 LPA figure specifically to Bangalore.
Is XIME Bangalore residential?
Yes. XIME describes the PGDM and PGDM-BA as fully residential programmes, with separate hostels for men and women. Hostel charges are separate from the programme fee.
Is XIME PGDM equivalent to an MBA?
XIME's PGDM is an AICTE-approved postgraduate management diploma. XIME states that an AICTE-approved PGDM from a reputed autonomous institution is comparable to an MBA for employment and higher-study purposes, but students should check the specific eligibility rules of any university or examination where a degree qualification is required.
