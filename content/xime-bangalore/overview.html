XIME Bangalore Overview

Xavier Institute of Management & Entrepreneurship (XIME) is a management institute at Electronics City, Phase II, Bengaluru. Its flagship PGDM is a two-year, full-time residential programme, while the Bangalore campus also offers a dedicated PGDM in Business Analytics. XIME's PGDM is AICTE-approved; the Bangalore PGDM is NBA accredited and the qualification has AIU recognition.

Why students shortlist XIME Bangalore: It combines a residential PGDM, Bengaluru's business ecosystem, experiential learning, internships, multiple entrance-test routes and a clearly published selection formula.

XIME lists 240 PGDM seats and 60 PGDM-BA seats at Bangalore. Its learning model includes case studies, simulations, live projects, internships, mentoring, student clubs and industry interaction.

XIME Bangalore Admission Process 2026

Check eligibility.
Bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate; 45% for SC/ST. Final-year students can apply subject to XIME's completion requirements. Backlog candidates are not eligible.
Take an accepted test.
CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, MAT or ATMA.
Apply online.
Application fee ₹2,000; ₹250 extra for a second preferred XIME campus.
Attend GD & PI.
Shortlisted candidates go through Group Discussion and Personal Interview.
Final selection.
Admission-test score 35%, GD/Interview 40%, academics 13%, and extracurriculars/diversity/work experience 12%.
Accept the offer.
Confirm the seat by paying the applicable fee within the stated deadline.
Important: GD/PI carries the largest share of the published selection formula at 40%, while the entrance test contributes 35%.

Who Should Choose XIME Bangalore?

Good fit if...

You want a residential PGDM in Bengaluru, value experiential learning, can manage the ₹12.90 lakh programme fee and want either broad management exposure or an analytics-focused route.

Reconsider if...

Your priority is a very low-cost MBA, you need a non-residential programme, or your target requires placement outcomes materially above the ₹10–11 LPA average range published for Bangalore.

Decision tip: Compare total cost, average/median CTC, role quality and placement coverage together instead of comparing highest packages alone.

XIME Bangalore FAQs

Is XIME Bangalore good for PGDM?
XIME Bangalore is a credible option for students seeking a two-year residential PGDM in Bengaluru. It is AICTE-approved, the Bangalore PGDM is NBA accredited, and the Class of 2025 Bangalore PGDM average CTC is reported at ₹10.6 LPA. The right choice depends on your budget, target role and alternatives you can convert.
What is the XIME Bangalore fee for 2026–28?
The official programme fee is ₹12.90 lakh for both PGDM and PGDM-BA at Bangalore, split as ₹6.70 lakh in Year 1 and ₹6.20 lakh in Year 2. A ₹25,000 refundable general caution deposit and ₹10,000 refundable hostel deposit are separate.
Which entrance exams are accepted by XIME Bangalore?
XIME accepts CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, MAT and ATMA for Bangalore. KMAT is listed for Kochi and TANCET for Chennai.
What is the XIME Bangalore cutoff?
XIME does not publish a single fixed official percentile cutoff for Bangalore. Its published selection formula gives 35% to the entrance-test score, 40% to GD and interview, 13% to academics and 12% to extracurriculars, diversity and work experience.
What is the average package at XIME Bangalore?
For the Class of 2025, XIME reports ₹10.6 LPA average CTC for Bangalore PGDM and ₹11.2 LPA for Bangalore PGDM-BA. The combined XIME-campus average was ₹10.25 LPA.
Is XIME Bangalore residential?
Yes. XIME describes its PGDM as fully residential, with separate hostels for men and women. Hostel charges are separate from the programme fee.
Does XIME Bangalore offer Business Analytics?
Yes. XIME Bangalore offers a two-year PGDM in Business Analytics with a published intake of 60. It combines management with analytics, AI and technology and has reported stronger top-10% outcomes than regular PGDM in the latest published data.
