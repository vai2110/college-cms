VJIM Hyderabad Overview

Vignana Jyothi Institute of Management (VJIM), Hyderabad is a standalone management institute established in 1993 at Vignana Jyothi Nagar, Bachupally. Its flagship two-year full-time PGDM has a sanctioned intake of 270 and is approved by AICTE. The institute's current programme ecosystem covers functional areas such as Finance, Marketing, Human Resources, Business Analytics, Agri-Business Management and Operations, Logistics & Supply Chain.

Why students consider VJIM: The 2026–28 PGDM fee is ₹9 lakh before the refundable caution deposit and other specified charges, the institute accepts six major management tests, and the latest official placement overview reports ₹6.32 LPA average and ₹12.16 LPA highest CTC for Batch 31 (2024–25).

VJIM is better viewed as a mid-fee private PGDM option where admission is driven by a composite profile rather than entrance score alone. Its current brochure gives the national-level test 35% weightage and the PI & Communication Round 30%.

VJIM Hyderabad PGDM Admission 2026

VJIM is currently admitting students to Batch 34 (2026–28). The official 2026 brochure states that applicants need a bachelor's degree from a UGC-recognised university with at least 50% marks and a minimum 50 percentile in CAT, XAT, GMAT, MAT, CMAT or ATMA. The institute application fee is ₹1,200.

Important: VJIM does not use a single entrance-score-only formula. Final selection is based on a 100-point composite score covering academics, national-level test performance, PI & Communication and other diversity/extracurricular factors.

VJIM Scholarships 2026–28

VJIM's current scholarship policy is unusually detailed and can materially reduce the effective fee for strong applicants. Awards are available across composite score, national-level test performance, talent, location, disability, need and sister-institute categories.

Composite Score

Above 75%: ₹50,000; above 80%: ₹75,000; above 85%: ₹1,00,000; above 90%: ₹1,50,000. Two top students with 90%+ composite score can receive a 100% tuition-fee waiver subject to conditions.

National-Level Test

For CAT/XAT/GMAT, the published scholarship is ₹4 lakh at 80+ percentile, ₹3 lakh at 70–79.99 and ₹2 lakh at 60–69.99. MAT/CMAT and ATMA have separate lower slabs.

Talent / Outstation

Sports and arts talent scholarships can cover 10–25% of fee depending on achievement. Eligible students from states outside AP/TS can also receive up to ₹2.25 lakh under the location-specific policy.

Need / Defence / Sister Institutes

Need-based aid can go up to ₹4.50 lakh for qualifying low-income students; children of defence personnel can receive ₹1.80 lakh; eligible sister-institute/alumni-linked applicants can receive ₹2 lakh.

Only one scholarship criterion is applicable to a student unless VJIM's current terms specify otherwise. Contact admissions before assuming awards can be combined.

Who Should Choose VJIM Hyderabad?

Good fit if…

You want a two-year PGDM with a manageable ₹9 lakh academic fee, multiple entrance-test routes, a strong PI component and the option to specialise in analytics, finance, marketing, HR, operations or agribusiness.

Reconsider if…

Your priority is a consistently high average package or a very high placement percentage. VJIM's latest official average is ₹6.32 LPA and placement rate is 81% for Batch 31, so ROI should be assessed against your expected role and scholarship.

Decision checklist

Before applying, compare VJIM on effective fee after scholarship, expected role, average/median CTC, entrance-score strength, specialisation, location, hostel requirement and your alternative calls. Do not make the decision using the highest package alone.

VJIM Hyderabad FAQs

Is VJIM Hyderabad good for an MBA?
VJIM is a PGDM institute rather than a conventional university MBA college. Its two-year PGDM is AICTE approved, and VJIM states that its PGDM is recognised by AIU as equivalent to a two-year full-time master's degree. For students comparing private PGDM options in Hyderabad, the key attraction is the ₹9 lakh 2026–28 fee combined with multiple specialisations and a composite selection process.
What is the VJIM Hyderabad fee for PGDM 2026–28?
The official Batch 34 fee is ₹9,00,000 for two years. A ₹10,000 refundable caution deposit is payable in the first year and ₹5,000 is payable in the second year towards yearbook and alumni registration. Hostel is listed separately at ₹1.40 lakh per year plus a ₹10,000 refundable hostel caution deposit in the first year.
What percentile is required for VJIM Hyderabad?
For Batch 34, the official brochure requires at least 50 percentile in CAT, XAT, GMAT, MAT, CMAT or ATMA. This is an eligibility floor, not a guaranteed admission score. Because the national-level test carries 35% of the final composite score, stronger performance can improve your selection position and scholarship prospects.
Which exams are accepted by VJIM?
VJIM's 2026 brochure accepts CAT, XAT, GMAT, MAT, CMAT and ATMA for the PGDM admission route. Applicants should use the current batch brochure as the final source if the accepted-test list changes in a later admission cycle.
What is the average package at VJIM Hyderabad?
The detailed official placement overview reports ₹6.32 LPA average CTC for Batch 31 (2024–25), with ₹6.50 LPA median and ₹12.16 LPA highest CTC. The institute's 2026 brochure rounds the average to ₹6.37 LPA, so CollegeDecoded uses ₹6.32 LPA for the detailed year-wise trend because it appears on the placement overview.
Does VJIM provide scholarships?
Yes. The 2026–28 policy includes composite-score scholarships, national-level-test scholarships, talent scholarships, outstation scholarships, differently-abled student aid, need-based aid, defence-personnel scholarships and sister-institute benefits. The national-level-test slabs can reach ₹4 lakh for 80+ CAT/XAT/GMAT percentile, subject to the published terms.
Is VJIM Hyderabad worth joining?
It can be a sensible option if your priority is a relatively moderate-fee PGDM with multiple entrance routes, strong scholarship possibilities and a broad specialisation menu. The latest official average CTC is ₹6.32 LPA, so compare the effective fee after scholarship and the likely role you can target rather than using the ₹12.16 LPA highest package as the expected outcome.
