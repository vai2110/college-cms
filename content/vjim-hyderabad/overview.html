VJIM Hyderabad Overview

Vignana Jyothi Institute of Management was established in 1993 and offers a two-year full-time PGDM. The institute is approved by AICTE, its PGDM is NBA accredited, and VJIM states that the programme is recognised by AIU as equivalent to a two-year full-time MBA for higher-study purposes. The campus is at Vignana Jyothi Nagar, Bachupally, Hyderabad.

Who should consider VJIM? VJIM is most relevant for students looking for a private PGDM in Hyderabad with a relatively moderate published tuition fee, multiple national management-test routes, a Personal Interview-based selection process, summer internship exposure and functional electives in areas such as Finance, Marketing, HR, Operations and Business Analytics.

VJIM's current mandatory disclosure lists 270 approved PGDM seats. The institute also states that it has students from multiple states and follows an industry-linked pedagogy including Business News Analysis, outcome-based education and classroom applications.

Important Admission Dates

VJIM's 2026 admission material identifies Batch 34 as the 2026–28 intake. Unlike institutes with one fixed entrance-exam window, VJIM accepts applicants using recognised management-test scores and processes applications through its own admission procedure. Students should therefore verify the application status and PI schedule with VJIM before applying.

PGDM & Specialisations

PGDM

Duration: 2 years, full-time. The curriculum combines core management subjects with functional electives and industry exposure. VJIM's current materials identify Finance, Marketing, HR, Operations and Business Analytics among the specialisation areas/elective choices.

Career-oriented learning

VJIM highlights Summer Internship Programme (SIP), Business News Analysis, mentor-mentee interaction, pre-placement training, business conclaves, clubs and committees. The institute describes the curriculum as industry aligned and application oriented.

Important naming point: VJIM's current official materials primarily present the offering as a two-year PGDM rather than a collection of separate two-year PGDM degrees. Students choose functional areas/electives subject to the programme's academic structure.

VJIM Admission Process 2026

Check eligibility.
A bachelor's degree from a UGC-recognised university with at least 50% marks is required under VJIM's 2026 admissions material.
Have a management entrance score.
VJIM's 2026 brochure specifies a minimum 50 percentile in CAT, XAT, GMAT, MAT, CMAT or ATMA.
Apply online.
Complete the VJIM application and pay the ₹1,200 application fee.
Attend Personal Interview.
VJIM's admission procedure calls shortlisted applicants for PI. Its FAQ also states that candidates are interviewed as part of admission.
Composite selection.
For 2026, VJIM's brochure assigns 35% to the national-level test, 30% to PI & communication round, 15% to graduation, 5% to Class 10, 5% to Class 12 and 10% to other components. The latter includes academic diversity, sports/extracurricular activities and gender diversity.
Offer and fee payment.
Selected candidates receive an offer by email and must submit the prescribed first/registration amount by the deadline in the offer letter.
Does VJIM have management quota or reserved seats? VJIM's official FAQ says it follows a merit channel without quotas or reservations. It also states that entrance-test scores cannot be waived under exceptional circumstances.

Campus & Student Life

VJIM is located at Vignana Jyothi Nagar, Bachupally, Hyderabad. The institute highlights classroom learning, library and infrastructure, industry interactions, business conclaves, student clubs and committees, mentor-mentee support and career-development activities. The PGDM includes a Summer Internship Programme designed to provide hands-on industry exposure.

Career support: VJIM highlights pre-placement training and a Career Vision Schooling approach. Students should still evaluate placement outcomes by average/median salary, role quality and the number of eligible students placed rather than relying only on the highest package.

VJIM Hyderabad FAQs

Is VJIM Hyderabad a good college for PGDM?
VJIM can be a relevant option for students seeking a two-year PGDM in Hyderabad with AICTE approval, NBA accreditation, AIU equivalence and a published ₹9 lakh academic fee for the 2026–28 batch. The latest completed placement data reports ₹6.32 LPA average and ₹6.50 LPA median, so students should compare these with their target role and alternative B-schools before deciding.
What is the VJIM Hyderabad PGDM fee for 2026–28?
VJIM publishes ₹9 lakh total academic fee for Batch 34 (2026–28). There is also a ₹10,000 refundable caution deposit in the first year and ₹5,000 toward yearbook/alumni registration in the second year. Hostel is published separately at ₹1.40 lakh per year plus a ₹10,000 refundable first-year caution deposit.
Which entrance exams are accepted by VJIM?
For the 2026 admissions cycle, VJIM lists CAT, XAT, GMAT, MAT, CMAT and ATMA. Its brochure specifies a minimum 50 percentile in a recognised management entrance test.
What is the VJIM admission process?
Candidates apply online, submit a recognised entrance-test score, and appear for a Personal Interview. VJIM's 2026 brochure uses a composite score with 35% national-level test, 30% PI and communication, 15% graduation, 5% Class 10, 5% Class 12 and 10% other components.
Does VJIM have a management quota or direct admission?
VJIM's official FAQ says it has a merit channel without quotas or reservations. It also states that entrance-test requirements cannot be waived under exceptional circumstances. Students should therefore avoid claims of guaranteed/direct admission outside the official process.
What is the average package at VJIM Hyderabad?
For the 2024–25 batch, VJIM reports an average salary of ₹6.32 LPA and a median of ₹6.50 LPA. The same batch had a highest reported package of ₹12.16 LPA and 156 students placed out of 193 eligible students.
Does VJIM guarantee 100% placement?
No. VJIM's current FAQ explicitly says it does not guarantee placements. Its latest placement overview reports an 81% placement rate for 2024–25, based on 156 placed students out of 193 eligible students.
Is VJIM PGDM equivalent to an MBA?
VJIM states that its two-year PGDM is recognised by the Association of Indian Universities as equivalent to a two-year full-time Master's degree in Business Administration, which supports higher-study eligibility such as PhD applications subject to the receiving institution's rules.
