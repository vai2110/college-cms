SICSR Pune: Overview

Symbiosis Institute of Computer Studies and Research (SICSR), Pune is a constituent institute of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). It focuses on computer applications, information technology and technology-led management education.

Its current portfolio has BCA, BBA (IT), M.Sc. Computer Applications, MBA (IT) and MBA (Digital Transformation). The campus is at Atur Centre, Model Colony, Pune.

Student view: SICSR suits students who want IT or computer studies with a management component. The admission route is different for each programme, so the exact course matters when checking exams, cutoff and fees.

Courses, Fees & Eligibility

BCA

4-year Honours/Honours with Research programme. Intake: 180. Class 12 or approved equivalent diploma with 50%; 45% for SC/ST.

BBA (IT)

4-year Honours/Honours with Research programme. Intake: 120. Class 12 with 50%; 45% for SC/ST.

M.Sc. (CA)

2-year programme. Areas include Software Development, Data Science and Cyber Security. 50% graduation requirement; 45% for SC/ST.

MBA (IT)

2-year full-time programme. Current academic fee is ₹2.85 lakh per annum, before deposit and living costs.

MBA (Digital Transformation)

2-year full-time programme. Current academic fee is ₹3.184 lakh per annum, before deposit and living costs.

Admission process

  1. Choose the programme and check eligibility.
  2. Register for SET, SNAP or the programme-specific non-entrance route.
  3. Complete the SICSR institute application before its deadline.
  4. Attend PI or GE-PI if shortlisted.
  5. Check the merit list and pay the required fee.
Important: A SET or SNAP score alone does not complete admission. The institute application and later selection stages also matter. SIU states that admissions follow prescribed merit-based processes and that it does not charge capitation fees.

Is SICSR Pune Worth Considering?

Good fit if

  • You want an IT-focused programme.
  • You are comfortable with SET, SNAP or the relevant institute route.
  • You want to study in Pune.
  • You are targeting analytics, software, IT infrastructure, digital or techno-management roles.

Check carefully if

  • You are choosing only because of the highest package.
  • You need a low total cost.
  • You want a general MBA without an IT or digital focus.
  • You are relying on unofficial cutoff numbers.

Before applying

  • Compare the exact programme fee.
  • Check the latest admission notice.
  • Compare average salary with total cost.
  • Include hostel and mess in your budget.

SICSR Pune FAQs

Which entrance exam is required for SICSR Pune?

It depends on the programme. BCA and BBA (IT) used SET in the main 2026 UG route. BBA (IT) also had a separate Non-SET route. MBA (IT) and MBA (Digital Transformation) use SNAP. M.Sc. (Computer Applications) had a Non-SNAP admission process in 2026.

What is the SICSR Pune MBA cutoff?

A secondary source reported an 82 percentile Round 1 closing cutoff for both MBA (IT) and MBA (Digital Transformation) in 2025. SICSR does not publish a guaranteed future cutoff on its current programme pages. Treat 82 as a historical reported reference, not a guaranteed shortlist score.

What is the latest SICSR Pune placement package?

For the 2023–25 batch, the official placement report lists ₹8.40 LPA as the highest annual compensation for MBA (IT), ₹7.87 LPA for MBA (Digital Transformation) and ₹7.00 LPA for M.Sc. (Computer Applications). Their averages were ₹5.98 LPA, ₹5.85 LPA and ₹4.66 LPA respectively.

Is SICSR Pune good for BCA?

SICSR's BCA is a four-year Honours/Honours with Research programme with an intake of 180. Its programme structure includes technology-focused areas such as Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Cloud Computing and Data Security. The decision should be based on curriculum, total cost, SET performance and course-wise placement data.

What is the SICSR Pune MBA fee?

For Indian students in 2026-28, MBA (IT) has an academic fee of ₹2.85 lakh per annum and MBA (Digital Transformation) has ₹3.184 lakh per annum. A ₹20,000 refundable institute deposit is listed separately. Hostel and mess are extra.

Can I get SICSR admission without SET or SNAP?

For some programmes, yes, through an institute-published alternative process. BBA (IT) had a Non-SET route in 2026, while M.Sc. (CA) used a Non-SNAP process. These should not be confused with guaranteed direct admission. SIU states that admission follows its prescribed merit-based process.

Does SICSR provide internships?

Yes. SICSR states that UG students start internships after Semester 5 and PG students after Semester 3. The published PG internship duration is generally 4–6 months. This is a useful factor when comparing programmes with optional internships.
