SICSR Pune Overview

SICSR is a constituent institute of Symbiosis International (Deemed University) focused on computer applications, information technology and technology-led management education. Its current programme portfolio spans undergraduate BCA and BBA (IT) and postgraduate M.Sc. Computer Applications, MBA (IT) and MBA (Digital Transformation). The institute is located at Atur Centre, Gokhale Cross Road, Model Colony, Pune.

What stands out for a student: SICSR combines computing subjects such as programming, databases, software development and data science with IT-management subjects. The MBA (IT) curriculum, for example, includes Python, Python for Data Science, database management, ERP, digital marketing, AI/ML for business and an industry internship. citeturn3search2

Admission Process: Detailed but Crisp

Choose the programme and check eligibility.
UG applicants need the prescribed Class XII qualification; BCA currently requires 50% (45% SC/ST). PG applicants need a recognised graduation qualification; M.Sc. CA and MBA IT currently specify 50% (45% SC/ST). citeturn3search3turn3search0turn3search9
Complete the applicable entrance/admission registration.
SET is the standard published UG route for SICSR's 2026 cycle; MBA IT and MBA DT use SNAP. M.Sc. CA's 2026 schedule is explicitly Non-SNAP. SICSR also published a separate Non-SET BBA (IT) route for 2026. citeturn0search2turn3search4turn3search5
Complete SICSR's institute application.
For programmes requiring institute registration, submit the SICSR application and payment within the programme-specific deadline. An entrance-test score alone does not complete the institute application.
Attend the institute's selection stage.
Depending on the programme, this includes online PI and, for the PG programmes using the published SNAP route, GE-PI. M.Sc. CA's 2026 Non-SNAP schedule lists online GE-PI. citeturn3search4turn3search5
Check the merit list and pay the first fee installment.
Selection is merit-based under SIU's prescribed process. SICSR/SIU explicitly states that it does not charge capitation fee and has not authorised agents to guarantee admission. citeturn0search2turn0search8
Alternative admission routes: SICSR does have programme-specific routes beyond a single common entrance exam: BBA (IT) has an officially published Non-SET UG route, while M.Sc. Computer Applications has a Non-SNAP process. These are not management-quota/direct guaranteed admissions; SIU states admissions are strictly through prescribed merit-based processes and capitation is not charged. citeturn0search2turn3search4

FAQs Students Actually Ask About SICSR Pune

Is SICSR Pune good for BCA?
SICSR is specifically focused on computer studies and currently offers BCA (Honours/Honours with Research) with an intake of 180. The programme is housed within an institute whose portfolio also includes M.Sc. Computer Applications, MBA IT and MBA Digital Transformation, so students seeking a dedicated IT/computer-education environment can evaluate the full SICSR ecosystem rather than a general management college. SICSR currently reports strong UG placement headlines, including a ₹23.50 LPA institute-level highest package; however, students should compare course-wise placement data rather than treating the highest package as the expected BCA salary. citeturn3search3turn2search0
What entrance exam is required for SICSR Pune?
For UG admission, SICSR's 2026 schedule used SET for its main UG route. BBA (IT) also had a separately published Non-SET UG admission schedule. For MBA IT and MBA Digital Transformation, the published admission process uses SNAP followed by SICSR's selection process. M.Sc. Computer Applications had a 2026 Non-SNAP process with shortlist and online GE-PI. Therefore, the answer depends on the programme rather than one exam applying to every SICSR course. citeturn0search2turn3search4turn3search5
Can I get admission to SICSR without SET or SNAP?
For some programmes, yes, through an officially published alternative process. SICSR's 2026 BBA (IT) schedule included a Non-SET UG route, and its M.Sc. Computer Applications schedule was explicitly Non-SNAP. This should not be confused with direct or management-quota admission: SIU states that admission is merit-based through prescribed processes and that it does not charge capitation fee or authorise agents to guarantee admission. citeturn0search2turn3search4turn0search8
What is the SICSR Pune MBA IT fee?
For Indian students in the 2026-28 MBA IT batch, SICSR lists academic fees of ₹2,85,000 per annum, plus a ₹20,000 refundable institute deposit in the first installment. The published Model Colony three-sharing hostel fee is ₹1,23,200 per annum and mess is ₹1,19,600 per annum. Hostel and mess charges can rise by up to 15% each year and taxes may apply. citeturn4search0
What is the highest package at SICSR Pune?
SICSR's current homepage highlights a ₹23.50 LPA highest package at the institute level. This should not be read as the highest package for every course. Recent course-wise reported figures show different outcomes: for example, 2025 MBA IT was reported at ₹8.40 LPA highest, MBA DT at ₹7.87 LPA and M.Sc. CA at ₹7 LPA, while UG was reported at ₹23.05 LPA. The institute also states that 50+ companies participate in placements on average every year. citeturn2search0turn2search4
What is the average package at SICSR Pune?
There is no single meaningful average for all SICSR students because placements are reported by programme. For 2025, published secondary-source summaries report averages of about ₹5.97 LPA for MBA IT, ₹5.84 LPA for MBA DT, ₹4.65 LPA for M.Sc. CA and ₹4.48 LPA for UG programmes. SICSR's own website currently highlights the ₹23.50 LPA highest package and 100% overall internship/placement rate for eligible students rather than publishing one institute-wide average on its homepage. citeturn2search4turn2search0
Is SICSR Pune expensive compared with its placement outcomes?
The answer depends on the programme. MBA IT academic fees are ₹2.85 lakh per annum, MBA DT ₹3.184 lakh, M.Sc. CA ₹2.373 lakh, BCA ₹1.225 lakh and BBA IT ₹1.25 lakh for the currently published Indian-student fee schedules. Hostel and mess add a substantial separate cost. Because recent course-wise averages are materially below the institute's highest-package headline, students should evaluate the programme-specific average, course fee and career target together rather than using the highest package alone. citeturn4search0turn4search1turn4search2turn0search4turn0search5turn2search4
Does SICSR provide internships?
Yes. SICSR states that UG students begin internships after completion of the fifth semester, while PG students begin after completion of the third semester. The institute describes the PG internship as 4–6 months and the UG internship as 4 months. This is particularly relevant for students comparing SICSR with programmes where internships are optional rather than embedded in the academic progression. citeturn1search0
