IMS Ghaziabad Overview

IMS Ghaziabad is a management institute at Lal Quan, Ghaziabad, in Delhi-NCR. Its flagship PGDM is a two-year, full-time programme approved by AICTE, accredited by NBA and recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA. The institute currently describes a 36-year legacy and a broad industry-oriented management ecosystem.

Why students shortlist IMS Ghaziabad: It offers a Delhi-NCR location, dual specialisation, multiple accepted entrance exams, a ₹9.50 lakh academic fee and an established recruiter network.

IMS Ghaziabad PGDM

2-Year Full-Time PGDM

Intake: 450
Structure: trimester system
Internship: compulsory 6–8 week summer internship after the first three terms
Second year: dual specialisation courses + dissertation project.

Specialisation Areas

Current areas listed by IMS include Applied AI, Finance, Human Resource, Marketing and Operations. The dual-specialisation model lets students combine two functional areas.

Eligibility: A minimum three-year bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks or equivalent. Final-year students can apply subject to completion and proof requirements.

IMS Ghaziabad Admission Process 2026

Check eligibility.
Graduates need at least 50% in a recognised bachelor's degree. Fresh graduates and relevant work-experience candidates can apply.
Submit the application.
The current online application fee is ₹1,100. Submit academic documents and the accepted entrance-test score.
Use an accepted test score.
IMS lists CAT, XAT, CMAT and MAT for the PGDM admission process.
Attend WAT, GD and PI.
Shortlisted applicants participate in Written Aptitude Test, Group Discussion and Personal Interview stages.
Final merit.
Published weightage: 20% entrance score, 20% 10th/12th/graduation academics, 10% WAT, 25% PI and 25% GD.
Accept the offer.
Selected candidates receive a deadline to submit the first fee instalment and confirm the seat.
Most important selection insight: GD + PI together account for 50% of the published final selection weightage, so a good entrance score alone does not guarantee admission.

IMS Ghaziabad Cutoff

IMS does not publish one fixed official percentile cutoff for CAT, XAT, CMAT or MAT on its current admission procedure page. The institute evaluates applicants through entrance score, academics, WAT, GD and PI.

Campus & Student Life

IMS Ghaziabad is located at G.T. Road, Lal Quan, Ghaziabad, giving students access to the wider Delhi-NCR corporate ecosystem. The institute highlights lecture rooms, computer labs, cafeteria and residential accommodation, along with industry interactions, live projects and corporate events.

Learning environment

Trimester-based academics, case-based learning, live projects, summer internships, corporate talks and industry-oriented activities are built into the PGDM experience.

Career ecosystem

The placement ecosystem covers consulting, banking/NBFC, FMCG/FMCD, IT/services, logistics, manufacturing, retail and analytics-related roles.

Who Should Choose IMS Ghaziabad?

Good fit if...

You want a Delhi-NCR PGDM, need a relatively moderate academic fee, prefer dual specialisation and are comfortable with a selection process where GD/PI carry 50% combined weight.

Reconsider if...

Your only decision criterion is the highest package, you want a highly selective national B-school brand, or you need a programme with a substantially lower total cost including living expenses.

Decision tip: Compare IMS using median salary, total two-year cost, placement coverage, role mix and the entrance/GD-PI profile rather than the ₹43 LPA headline alone.

IMS Ghaziabad FAQs

Is IMS Ghaziabad good for PGDM?
IMS Ghaziabad is a reasonable Delhi-NCR PGDM option for students who value location, dual specialisation and an established placement ecosystem. The programme is AICTE-approved, NBA-accredited and AIU-recognised as equivalent to an MBA. Your decision should still depend on the alternatives you can convert and your target role.
What is the IMS Ghaziabad PGDM fee for 2026–28?
The official academic fee is ₹9.50 lakh for the full two-year PGDM. The listed hostel fee is ₹1.60 lakh per year including mess for a two-seater room, plus ₹5,000 refundable hostel security.
Which entrance exams are accepted by IMS Ghaziabad?
The current admission procedure lists CAT, XAT, CMAT and MAT for the PGDM. The entrance-score component carries 20% of the published final selection weightage.
What is the IMS Ghaziabad cutoff?
IMS does not publish one fixed official percentile cutoff on its current admission page. It uses the entrance score along with academics, WAT, GD and PI, so unofficial cutoff figures should be treated only as indicative.
What is the average or median package at IMS Ghaziabad?
The current placement page reports a ₹9.5 LPA median salary and ₹43 LPA highest salary. An earlier published placement set reported ₹9.25 LPA median and ₹35 LPA highest. Median is the more useful number for typical-outcome comparison.
Does IMS Ghaziabad offer scholarships?
Yes. The 2026–27 policy includes scholarships linked to CAT/XAT, CMAT, MAT, academic background, defence background and sister institutions. The maximum combined scholarship is ₹75,000 and the listed awards apply for the first year only.
What specialisations are offered at IMS Ghaziabad?
The current PGDM programme lists Applied AI, Finance, Human Resource, Marketing and Operations. The programme uses a dual-specialisation structure in the second year.
