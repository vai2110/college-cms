IMS Ghaziabad Overview

IMS Ghaziabad is a management institute in Lal Quan, Ghaziabad, in Delhi-NCR. The institute says it has completed 36 years and its flagship PGDM is AICTE approved, NBA accredited and recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA. The programme follows a trimester structure and includes a compulsory 6–8 week summer internship after the first three terms.

Who should consider IMS Ghaziabad? Students looking for a relatively accessible Delhi-NCR PGDM with dual specialisation, a broad management curriculum and an established campus-placement ecosystem. The institute currently offers specialisation areas including Applied AI, Finance, HR, Marketing and Operations.

PGDM Programme

2-Year Full-Time PGDM

Intake: 450. The programme is run on a trimester system and is equivalent to MBA status by AIU. Students complete a compulsory summer internship and choose dual functional specialisations in the second year.

Specialisation Areas

Current areas listed by IMS include Applied AI, Finance, Human Resource, Marketing and Operations. The dual-specialisation structure allows students to build a broader functional profile rather than limiting themselves to one area.

Eligibility: A minimum three-year bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks or equivalent is required. Final-year students may apply subject to completing the qualifying degree requirements before joining.

IMS Ghaziabad Admission Process

Check eligibility.
Graduate applicants need at least 50% in a recognised bachelor's degree. Fresh graduates and candidates with relevant work experience can apply.
Submit the application.
The institute lists an online application fee of ₹1,100. Applicants submit academic documents and their entrance-exam score.
Use an accepted management entrance score.
IMS lists CAT, XAT, CMAT and MAT scores in its admission procedure. The institute's final selection table assigns 20% to the entrance-score component.
Appear for WAT, GD and PI.
Shortlisted applicants participate in the Written Aptitude Test, Group Discussion and Personal Interview stages as prescribed by IMS.
Final merit.
IMS publishes the following weightage: 20% entrance score, 20% 10th/12th/graduation academic record, 10% WAT, 25% PI and 25% GD.
Accept the offer and pay the fee.
Selected candidates are given a period to submit the first fee instalment after the final selection list.
Is management quota/direct guaranteed admission available? Students should not treat an agent's promise of guaranteed admission as an official route. Admission is subject to IMS's published eligibility, application, entrance-score and selection process.

Placement Data: Use the Latest Official Figure Carefully

IMS's current placement page provides the latest headline figures above, but does not present a clean multi-year average/median table in the same format. CollegeDecoded therefore does not fabricate missing annual figures. Students comparing IMS across years should use the institute's annual reports and placement reports for like-for-like comparisons.

The ₹43 LPA headline is a highest-salary figure, not the typical salary. For ROI decisions, median salary (₹9.5 LPA on the current placement page), total fee and the student's target role should be considered together.

Campus & Student Life

The IMS Ghaziabad campus is at G.T. Road, Lal Quan, Ghaziabad, within Delhi-NCR. The institute highlights lecture rooms, computer labs, cafeteria and residential accommodation. The PGDM structure also includes industry internships, workshops, certifications and management-oriented activities.

Location advantage: Ghaziabad provides access to the wider Delhi-NCR employment market while keeping the campus outside central Delhi. Students targeting NCR consulting, BFSI, FMCG, IT/services, analytics or sales/marketing roles can benefit from the regional corporate ecosystem.

IMS Ghaziabad FAQs

What is the IMS Ghaziabad PGDM fee for 2026–28?
The official academic fee is ₹9.50 lakh for the two-year PGDM. The 2026–28 schedule splits this into ₹5 lakh in Year 1 and ₹4.50 lakh in Year 2. Hostel and refundable security are separate.
What entrance exams does IMS Ghaziabad accept?
IMS's published admission procedure lists CAT, XAT, CMAT and MAT scores. The entrance-score component carries 20% weight in the published final-selection framework.
What is the IMS Ghaziabad PGDM eligibility?
Candidates need a recognised minimum three-year bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent. Final-year students can apply subject to completing the qualifying requirements before joining.
What is the IMS Ghaziabad highest package?
The current official placement page reports a highest salary of ₹43 LPA. This is the highest figure, not the average salary. The same page reports a ₹9.5 LPA median salary.
What is the average or median salary at IMS Ghaziabad?
The current official placement page reports a median salary of ₹9.5 LPA. IMS's homepage separately highlights the ₹43 LPA highest salary and 185+ companies; students should use median and role-level outcomes for ROI evaluation rather than the highest package alone.
Does IMS Ghaziabad have dual specialisation?
Yes. The flagship PGDM is structured around dual functional specialisation, with current areas including Applied AI, Finance, Human Resource, Marketing and Operations.
Does IMS Ghaziabad offer scholarships?
Yes. For 2026–27, IMS lists scholarships based on CAT/XAT/CMAT/MAT performance, B.Tech academic background, sister-institution status and defence background. The maximum combined scholarship is ₹75,000 and is available only for the first year.
Is IMS Ghaziabad good for MBA/PGDM in Delhi NCR?
It can be considered by students seeking a PGDM in the NCR region, particularly those who value a ₹9.50 lakh academic fee, dual specialisation, residential facilities and an established placement ecosystem. The right choice depends on your entrance score, target function, expected salary and alternatives available at the same fee level.
