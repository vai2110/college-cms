IPE Hyderabad Overview

The Institute of Public Enterprise (IPE) is an autonomous non-profit management institute established in 1964. Its Shamirpet campus is in Medchal-Malkajgiri district, Hyderabad. IPE launched its first two-year full-time PGDM in 1995 and currently offers multiple PGDM options with a management-focused curriculum, industry exposure and career support.

Who should consider IPE? Students looking for a Hyderabad-based PGDM with multiple entrance-test routes, comparatively moderate academic fees, residential campus facilities and a broad set of management specialisations. The current official site says 2026–28 spot admissions are open for select vacant seats.

Programmes at IPE Hyderabad

PGDM – Flagship

Two-year full-time AICTE-approved PGDM covering core management disciplines and designed for broad management careers.

PGDM – Marketing Management

Specialised PGDM route for students targeting marketing, sales, brand, customer and digital-market roles.

PGDM – International Business

Management programme with an international-business orientation for students interested in global markets and cross-border business.

PGDM – Banking & Financial Services

Finance-oriented route listed in IPE's current postgraduate portfolio for careers in banking and financial services.

PGDM – Human Resource Management

HR-focused route covering people-management and organisational roles.

PGDM – Business Analytics

AICTE-approved two-year programme launched in 2024–25, focused on analytics skills required by domestic and global businesses.

IPE Admission Process

Meet eligibility.
A recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks/equivalent CGPA is required; IPE states 45% for SC/ST/PC candidates.
Submit an accepted entrance score.
IPE accepts CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, CMAT and ATMA. The current FAQ also identifies the national-level test route for 2026–28.
Apply to IPE.
Complete the institute application and submit academic and entrance-test details. The application fee listed by IPE is ₹500.
Shortlisting.
IPE considers academic consistency, entrance performance and other application factors. Its FAQ notes 60% and above across 10th, Intermediate/12th and graduation and 50 percentile or above in management tests as part of the selection framework.
Personal Interview.
Shortlisted candidates participate in the institute's PI process. Earlier cycles also used GDPI; verify the exact process for any spot-admission offer.
Offer and fee payment.
Selected candidates are informed and must complete fee payment according to the provisional admission communication.
Direct/management admission: Do not treat a vacant-seat/spot-admission announcement as a separate management-quota route. Students should apply through IPE's prescribed process and obtain written confirmation of the programme, fee and admission conditions before paying.

Campus & Student Life

IPE's Shamirpet campus is located in Medchal-Malkajgiri district, away from central Hyderabad. The residential setup includes hostel and food facilities, and the institute highlights academic activities, seminars, special lectures, student activities, sports and industry interaction as part of the campus experience.

Location decision: IPE can suit students who prefer a residential management-campus environment around Hyderabad rather than a city-centre institute. Check commute/transport requirements carefully if you plan to live off campus.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IPE Hyderabad good for an MBA?
IPE offers PGDM rather than an MBA degree. It is an AICTE-approved management institute with multiple PGDM routes, a residential campus and established management education since 1995. Whether it is a good choice depends on your target specialisation, entrance score, budget and placement expectations.
What is the IPE Hyderabad fee for 2026–28?
The current published academic fee is ₹9.15 lakh for two years: ₹5.15 lakh in Year 1 and ₹4 lakh in Year 2. Hostel, food and transportation are additional.
Which entrance exams are accepted by IPE?
IPE accepts CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, CMAT and ATMA for its PGDM admissions. IPE's homepage also says TG-ICET candidates can apply for the currently advertised spot-admission seats.
What is the average package at IPE Hyderabad?
IPE's official placement page reports an average salary of ₹7.02 lakh for 2025 and ₹7.41 lakh for the 2026 cycle while placements are still in progress. Compare the completed 2025 figure rather than treating the 2026 number as final.
Does IPE Hyderabad offer scholarships?
Yes. IPE states that scholarships range from ₹60,000 to ₹4 lakh, including merit-based awards linked to entrance-test performance and scholarships for eligible students from specific categories, states and sports/arts backgrounds.
Does IPE Hyderabad have hostel facilities?
Yes. IPE lists residential hostel accommodation at its Shamirpet campus. For 2026–27, double occupancy with food and accommodation is listed at ₹1.20 lakh per academic year, subject to revision.
Is IPE Hyderabad's PGDM Business Analytics available?
Yes. IPE launched its two-year AICTE-approved PGDM Business Analytics programme in 2024–25. It is designed to develop analytics skills for business applications.
Are IPE Hyderabad placements 100%?
No blanket 100% claim should be made from the current official data. IPE reports 81.4% placed for 2025, while the 2026 cycle is still in progress. Placement percentages should therefore be read batch-wise and alongside the number of eligible students.
