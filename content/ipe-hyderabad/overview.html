IPE Hyderabad Overview

The Institute of Public Enterprise (IPE) is an autonomous non-profit management institute established in 1964. It launched its first two-year full-time PGDM in 1995 and operates from its Shamirpet campus in the Medchal-Malkajgiri district of Hyderabad.

Student takeaway: IPE is worth considering if you want a Hyderabad PGDM with several entrance-test routes, a residential campus, multiple functional/sector-focused programmes and a fee that is below many private B-schools in the same broad market.

IPE's current programme portfolio includes PGDM, PGDM Marketing Management, PGDM Banking & Financial Services, PGDM International Business, PGDM Human Resource Management and PGDM Business Analytics.

IPE Hyderabad Admission Process

Meet eligibility.
A bachelor's degree from a recognised university with at least 50% marks/equivalent CGPA is required. IPE states 45% for SC/ST/PC candidates.
Take an accepted entrance test.
IPE accepts CAT, XAT, MAT, ATMA, CMAT and GMAT. Its current homepage also says TG-ICET candidates can apply.
Submit the IPE application.
Complete the online form and provide academic records, entrance score and relevant profile information. IPE's FAQ lists the application fee as ₹500.
Shortlisting.
IPE considers academic record, management aptitude test performance and profile factors. Its FAQ states that 50 percentile and above in management tests is part of the selection framework.
Personal Interview.
Shortlisted candidates attend the PI process at IPE Hyderabad or designated cities/online mode as communicated by the institute.
Final selection.
The current admission procedure publishes 40% management aptitude test, 20% academic record, 35% PI and 5% sports/extracurricular/academic-diversity/gender-diversity weightage.
Important: IPE's current 2026–28 homepage says TG-ICET candidates can also apply, while the core national-test list is CAT/XAT/MAT/ATMA/CMAT/GMAT. Confirm the exact test eligibility for the specific programme before applying.

IPE Hyderabad Cutoff

IPE does not present a single fixed CAT/XAT/CMAT cutoff table as its current admission requirement. Its FAQ says selection includes an adequate entrance score and mentions 50 percentile and above in management tests as part of the selection criteria.

How to interpret this: 50 percentile should not be treated as a guaranteed admission cutoff. The final selection is composite, with 40% test score, 20% academic record, 35% PI and 5% profile/diversity factors.

For a student comparing IPE with colleges that publish a hard cutoff, IPE's composite-selection model makes the PI and academic record particularly important.

Hostel & Other Costs

For 2026–27, double-occupancy hostel accommodation including food is listed at ₹1,20,000 per academic year, subject to revision. Transportation to/from the Shamirpet campus is listed at ₹45,000 per academic year. IPE also states that a laptop will be issued to all students.

Scholarships

IPE states that scholarships for 2026–28 range from ₹60,000 to ₹4 lakh. General-category merit scholarships can reach ₹2 lakh for qualifying entrance-test scores, while international/national-level sports and arts representation can attract scholarships of up to ₹4 lakh.

Campus & Student Life

IPE's Shamirpet campus is located in Medchal-Malkajgiri district, away from central Hyderabad. The residential setup includes hostel and food facilities, and the institute highlights air-conditioned classrooms with multimedia, a spacious auditorium, gym and sports facilities including a FIFA-certified football ground.

Residential Campus

Hostel accommodation and food are available on campus, making IPE a residential PGDM option rather than a city-centre day-school format.

Learning & Activities

Students get seminars, special lectures, student activities, internships and management-focused events alongside classroom learning.

Who Should Choose IPE?

Good fit if you...

  • Want a Hyderabad-based PGDM.
  • Need multiple entrance-test routes.
  • Prefer a residential campus.
  • Want a moderate academic fee.
  • Are targeting general management, marketing, BFSI, HR, IB or analytics.

Reconsider if you...

  • Need a very high average placement package.
  • Want a central-city Hyderabad campus.
  • Expect a single high CAT cutoff to define admission.
  • Are choosing primarily on highest-package headlines.
ROI lens: With a ₹9.15 lakh academic fee and recent completed-batch averages around ₹7 lakh, IPE should be evaluated for role fit, placement rate and total living cost rather than highest package alone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IPE Hyderabad good for an MBA?
IPE offers PGDM rather than an MBA degree. It is an AICTE-approved management institute with multiple PGDM routes, a residential campus and established management education since 1995. Whether it is a good choice depends on your target specialisation, entrance score, budget and placement expectations.
What is the IPE Hyderabad fee for 2026–28?
The current published academic fee is ₹9.15 lakh for two years: ₹5.15 lakh in Year 1 and ₹4 lakh in Year 2. Hostel, food and transportation are additional.
Which entrance exams are accepted by IPE?
IPE accepts CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, CMAT and ATMA for its PGDM admissions. IPE's homepage also says TG-ICET candidates can apply for the currently advertised spot-admission seats.
What is the average package at IPE Hyderabad?
IPE's official placement page reports an average salary of ₹7.02 lakh for 2025 and ₹7.41 lakh for the 2026 cycle while placements are still in progress. Compare the completed 2025 figure rather than treating the 2026 number as final.
Does IPE Hyderabad offer scholarships?
Yes. IPE states that scholarships range from ₹60,000 to ₹4 lakh, including merit-based awards linked to entrance-test performance and scholarships for eligible students from specific categories, states and sports/arts backgrounds.
Does IPE Hyderabad have hostel facilities?
Yes. IPE lists residential hostel accommodation at its Shamirpet campus. For 2026–27, double occupancy with food and accommodation is listed at ₹1.20 lakh per academic year, subject to revision.
Is IPE Hyderabad's PGDM Business Analytics available?
Yes. IPE launched its two-year AICTE-approved PGDM Business Analytics programme in 2024–25. It is designed to develop analytics skills for business applications.
Are IPE Hyderabad placements 100%?
No blanket 100% claim should be made from the current official data. IPE reports 81.4% placed for 2025, while the 2026 cycle is still in progress. Placement percentages should therefore be read batch-wise and alongside the number of eligible students.
