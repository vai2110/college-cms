SIBM Nagpur Eligibility

For the MBA and MBA-FABM programmes, candidates must hold a graduation degree from a recognised university or Institution of National Importance. The institute specifies a minimum of 50% marks or equivalent grade for general candidates and 45% for candidates belonging to SC/ST categories.

Final-year students can apply, subject to meeting the required graduation marks by the time admission conditions are fulfilled. Candidates with foreign university qualifications need an equivalence certificate from the Association of Indian Universities, as specified by the institute.

Important: Meeting eligibility does not itself guarantee a GE/PI call or final admission. Selection is based on the institute's prescribed SNAP, GE and PI process.

SIBM Nagpur Admission Process

  1. Register for SNAP 2026 and appear for the SNAP test.
  2. Complete the separate SIBM Nagpur institute registration within the stated deadline.
  3. Wait for the institute's shortlist based on its selection process.
  4. Shortlisted candidates complete Group Exercise (GE) and Personal Interaction (PI).
  5. The final merit list is prepared using SNAP, GE and PI weightage.
  6. Pay the applicable programme fees within the deadline after selection.

How to Prepare for SIBM Nagpur Admission

For SNAP

Build speed and accuracy across the SNAP sections, then use mock tests to identify weak areas. Since the SNAP component carries 50% of the final merit weightage, a strong test performance is strategically important.

For GE

Practise structured discussion, listening, concise communication and evidence-based viewpoints. Avoid dominating the discussion; focus on adding useful points.

For PI

Be ready to explain your academic background, work experience if any, career goals, reasons for MBA, reasons for SIBM Nagpur and your preferred specialisation. Questions should be answered consistently with your application.

SIBM Nagpur Admission FAQs

Which exam is required for SIBM Nagpur MBA?

SNAP is the entrance test used in the SIBM Nagpur MBA admission process.

What is the final selection weightage?

The published weightage is 50 marks for SNAP, 10 for GE and 40 for PI.

When does the 2027-29 programme start?

The official schedule lists 1 June 2027 as the programme commencement date.

Is SIBM Nagpur registration separate from SNAP registration?

Yes. Candidates need to complete the SIBM Nagpur institute registration/payment within the institute's stated deadline after the SNAP process.
