SIBM Nagpur MBA-FABM: Quick Facts

Duration

2 years, full-time residential

Intake

60 students

Selection

SNAP + GE + PI

MBA-FABM is designed for management careers across food, agriculture and agribusiness. It combines general management learning with sector-specific exposure, field work and industry engagement.

Eligibility

A candidate must be a graduate from a recognised university or Institution of National Importance with 50% marks or equivalent grade; SC/ST candidates require 45%. Final-year candidates may apply subject to the prescribed conditions. Foreign university graduates require AIU equivalence.

Is an agriculture degree mandatory? The published eligibility criteria do not state that an agriculture degree is mandatory. However, students from non-agriculture backgrounds should be prepared to learn agriculture, food systems and agribusiness concepts.

SIBM Nagpur MBA-FABM SNAP Cutoff

The institute's official MBA-FABM page confirms SNAP as part of selection but does not publish a guaranteed future cutoff. Historical third-party reporting around SIBM Nagpur has cited approximately 55 percentile as a past benchmark. Because cutoff estimates vary, use this only for planning.

MBA-FABM Curriculum

The curriculum combines financial management, operations, marketing, HR, agricultural economics, agriculture fundamentals, food product development, consumer studies, accounting, business statistics and applied projects.

Electives listed by the institute include Technology and Innovation Management, Data Analysis Using Python and Management of Operations, making the programme relevant for students who want sector knowledge plus analytical skills.

SIBM Nagpur MBA-FABM FAQs

What SNAP percentile is required for SIBM Nagpur MBA-FABM?

SIBM Nagpur does not publish a guaranteed future cutoff on the current official MBA-FABM page. Historical reporting around SIBM Nagpur has cited about 55 percentile as a past benchmark. Treat this as a reference only; a higher percentile is preferable because SNAP carries 50 of the 100 final-merit marks.

Is an agriculture background compulsory for MBA-FABM?

No such compulsory agriculture-degree requirement appears in the published eligibility criteria. The programme is sector-focused, so students from commerce, science, engineering or other backgrounds should be ready to build foundational knowledge of agriculture and food businesses.

What is the MBA-FABM intake at SIBM Nagpur?

The official programme page lists an intake of 60 students. This smaller cohort is one reason the programme's placement and career outcomes should be compared separately from the flagship MBA.

What is the MBA-FABM fee for non-Nagpur domicile students?

For 2026-28, the official structure lists ₹3,02,000 academic fee per installment for other-than-Nagpur domicile students. The first listed installment is ₹6,32,100 after adding refundable deposits and the listed hostel and mess charges.

What careers can I pursue after MBA-FABM?

Potential areas include agri-inputs, food and FMCG, food processing, rural enterprise, agribusiness consulting, supply chain, procurement and development-sector programmes. The best fit depends on your preferred sector and whether you want commercial, analytical, operations or development-oriented work.

Is MBA-FABM residential?

Yes. It is a two-year full-time residential programme, so students should include hostel and mess charges when comparing total programme cost.
