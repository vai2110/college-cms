What is SIBM Nagpur MBA-FABM?

The MBA in Food and Agri Business Management is a two-year residential programme designed for careers across food, agriculture and agribusiness. It combines management fundamentals with sector-specific learning in agri-value chains, food processing, sustainable agriculture and rural enterprise management.

The programme uses field immersions, industry projects and stakeholder engagement to connect classroom learning with real-world food and agriculture businesses.

Best suited for: Students who want management careers in agribusiness, food processing, FMCG/agri-input businesses, rural enterprises, development organisations, cooperatives or related consulting roles.

Eligibility

A candidate must be a graduate from a recognised university/institution of national importance with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade. SC/ST candidates require at least 45%. Final-year students can apply subject to meeting the required marks. Foreign university graduates require an AIU equivalence certificate.

Admission & Selection

SIBM Nagpur uses SNAP followed by the institute's Group Exercise and Personal Interaction process. The current selection framework allocates 50 marks to SNAP, 10 to GE and 40 to PI.

MBA-FABM Curriculum

The 2026-28 structure includes financial management, operations, marketing, HR, agricultural economics, introduction to agriculture, food product development and consumer studies, spreadsheet modelling, accounting, business statistics and an APMC operations project.

Electives listed by the institute include Technology and Innovation Management, Data Analysis Using Python and Management of Operations. This mix is useful for students who want both sector knowledge and analytical management skills.

MBA-FABM Placements

The official placement report for the 2022-24 MBA-FABM batch reported a highest CTC of ₹18 LPA, average CTC of ₹6.78 LPA and median CTC of ₹6.5 LPA. The institute's current website separately displays a 2026 overview with ₹40.12 LPA highest, ₹12.70 LPA average and ₹10.40 LPA median; students should check the latest detailed report before using these figures for decision-making.

Important: Do not compare the MBA and MBA-FABM packages directly without considering batch, sector mix, role mix and placement report year.
