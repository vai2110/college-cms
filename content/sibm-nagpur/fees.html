What Is the Real Cost of SIBM Nagpur?

Do not treat the first installment as the total programme cost. Academic fees are payable across four installments, while hostel and mess charges are billed according to the institute's schedule.

Budgeting point: SIBM Nagpur states that hostel and mess fees can increase by up to 15% each year and government taxes may be applicable. Subsequent-year hostel and mess charges are communicated before the next academic year.

Students should therefore compare SIBM Nagpur with other MBA colleges using a realistic two-year cost estimate rather than only the first payment.

Refundable Deposits & Fee Changes

  • Institute deposit is listed as refundable.
  • Hostel deposit is listed as refundable.
  • Hostel and mess fees can change for subsequent academic years.
  • Applicable government taxes may be added.
  • Always use the latest official fee notice before making payment.

SIBM Nagpur Fees FAQs

What is the MBA first installment for a non-Nagpur domicile student?

For the 2026-28 batch, the listed first installment for MBA is ₹7,79,600 for candidates other than Nagpur domicile.

Is there a domicile-based fee difference?

Yes. The 2026-28 MBA and MBA-FABM fee structures list different academic fees for Nagpur domicile and other-than-Nagpur domicile students.

Are hostel and mess fees included?

They are separately listed components of the fee structure and should be included when estimating the overall cost.
