Miscellaneous & Additional Fees: What You Should Budget

Students often search for a single “miscellaneous fee” figure, but SIBM Nagpur's current published 2026-28 fee tables do not provide one separate universal miscellaneous-fee line item. What Is the Real Cost?

Do not use only the first installment to compare SIBM Nagpur with other MBA colleges. Academic fees are spread across four installments, while hostel and mess charges are recurring residential costs.

Fee escalation: SIBM Nagpur states that hostel and mess fees can increase by up to 15% each year, government taxes can be additional, and subsequent-year hostel/mess fees are communicated before the next academic year.

For a realistic comparison, add academic fees, hostel, mess, deposits, taxes where applicable and your own living/incidental budget.

SIBM Nagpur Fees FAQs

What is the SIBM Nagpur MBA fee for non-Nagpur domicile students?

For 2026-28, academic fee is ₹4,49,500 per installment. The first listed installment is ₹7,79,600 after adding the institute deposit, hostel deposit, hostel fee and mess fee shown in the official table.

Is there a separate miscellaneous fee at SIBM Nagpur?

The current published 2026-28 fee tables do not show one universal line called “miscellaneous fee”. They separately list refundable deposits, hostel, mess and the possibility of government taxes. Students should not invent or assume a fixed miscellaneous amount that the institute has not published.

Are hostel and mess included in the SIBM Nagpur fee?

They are separately listed components of the official fee structure. For MBA 2026-28, hostel is ₹2,13,600 per annum and mess is ₹76,500 per annum. These charges should be included when estimating total cost.

Can SIBM Nagpur fees increase after the first year?

Yes. The institute states that hostel and mess fees can increase by up to 15% each year. Subsequent-year hostel and mess fees are communicated before the next academic year, and government taxes may also be additional.

Are the institute and hostel deposits refundable?

Yes. Both the institute deposit and hostel deposit are explicitly marked refundable in the published 2026-28 fee structure. Refund timing and conditions should be checked against the institute's refund rules.
