SIBM Nagpur Placements FAQs

What is the average package at SIBM Nagpur?

The official 2026 MBA placement overview currently displays an average package of ₹12.84 LPA. This figure should be read with the corresponding batch and placement-report context.

What is the median package at SIBM Nagpur?

The official 2026 MBA placement overview displays a median package of ₹12.10 LPA. Median is useful because it is less affected by a small number of very high offers.

What is the highest package at SIBM Nagpur?

The current official 2026 overview displays ₹28.38 LPA for MBA and ₹40.12 LPA for MBA-FABM. These are programme-specific figures and should not be treated as typical student outcomes.

Who are the recruiters at SIBM Nagpur?

The official MBA overview names Flipkart, EY, Schneider Electric, Nykaa, SAP and General Mills among recruiters. Recruiter participation can change across batches, so students should check the latest detailed placement report for their target role.

Is the highest package a good way to compare MBA colleges?

No. Compare median and average salary, role quality, placement coverage, sector mix, batch size, recruiter profile and total programme cost. A high outlier package can make a college look stronger than the typical outcome if viewed alone.

Should MBA and MBA-FABM placements be compared directly?

Not without context. MBA-FABM serves a more specialised food, agriculture and agribusiness market and has a smaller intake. Compare roles, sectors and the relevant placement report rather than using package alone.
