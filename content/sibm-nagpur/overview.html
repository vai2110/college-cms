SIBM Nagpur: Overview

Symbiosis Institute of Business Management (SIBM) Nagpur is a constituent of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). The institute offers management programmes with an emphasis on industry exposure, experiential learning, technology-enabled learning and holistic development.

For students comparing MBA colleges, the most important SIBM Nagpur differentiators are its SNAP-based admission route, residential MBA format, early specialisation, and a dedicated MBA in Food and Agri Business Management (MBA-FABM).

Student takeaway: SIBM Nagpur is most relevant if you want a residential MBA within the Symbiosis ecosystem and are comfortable with a SNAP → GE/PI selection process. Do not compare it only on brand name; compare total cost, specialisation fit, placement outcomes and your target career.

Campus & Student Life

The Nagpur campus is located at Gat. No. 167, 168, 169, Village Mauje-Wathoda, Nagpur 440008. The institute describes its learning model around classroom learning, projects, internships, corporate interactions, simulations, research orientation and social outreach.

For students, the residential format matters because accommodation and mess are part of the overall cost calculation. SIBM also maintains an internationalisation ecosystem and academic/corporate engagement activities.

SIBM Nagpur FAQs

What is SIBM Nagpur known for?

SIBM Nagpur is known for its residential MBA programmes under Symbiosis International (Deemed University), with specialisations including Marketing, HR, Finance, Operations & Supply Chain and Business Analytics.

What entrance exam is accepted by SIBM Nagpur?

SNAP is the entrance test used for the flagship MBA and MBA-FABM selection process, followed by the institute's GE/PI process.

What is the SIBM Nagpur MBA intake?

The official MBA page lists an intake of 180 students.

What is the SIBM Nagpur MBA-FABM intake?

The official MBA-FABM page lists an intake of 60 students.

Is SIBM Nagpur residential?

Yes. The flagship MBA and MBA-FABM are described by SIBM Nagpur as two-year full-time residential programmes.

What is the SIBM Nagpur MBA average package?

The official 2026 MBA placement overview lists an average package of ₹12.84 LPA and median package of ₹12.10 LPA.

Does SIBM Nagpur have Business Analytics?

Yes. Business Analytics is listed as one of the MBA specialisations.

What is the SIBM Nagpur fee for non-Nagpur domicile students?

For MBA batch 2026-28, the official fee page lists ₹4,49,500 academic fee per installment for other-than-Nagpur domicile candidates, plus deposits, hostel and mess charges.
