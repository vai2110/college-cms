SIBM Nagpur: Overview

Symbiosis Institute of Business Management (SIBM) Nagpur is a constituent of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). Its flagship MBA and MBA-FABM programmes are full-time residential programmes with selection through SNAP followed by institute-level GE and PI stages.

Student takeaway: SIBM Nagpur is worth comparing if you want the Symbiosis ecosystem, a residential MBA and early specialisation. The decision should be based on your SNAP competitiveness, total two-year cost, target specialisation and placement outcomes rather than the highest package alone.

SIBM Nagpur SNAP Cutoff

SIBM Nagpur does not publish a guaranteed future SNAP cutoff on its current official MBA page. Third-party reporting has historically cited around 55 percentile as a past qualifying benchmark, while 2026 estimates vary considerably by source. Treat these numbers as planning references, not an official promise.

Cutoff referenceReported figureHow students should use it

2026 expected range reported by some sources55–60 percentileOnly a broad planning estimate; actual shortlisting can differ.
Safer targetHigher than the historical benchmarkA higher SNAP percentile improves competitiveness because final admission also depends on GE/PI.
Important: Do not confuse a qualifying cutoff with the percentile needed to be comfortably competitive. SIBM Nagpur's final merit for the 2027-29 batch gives 50/100 marks to SNAP, 10 to GE and 40 to PI.

SIBM Nagpur Admission 2027-29

The published 2027-29 process is SNAP 2026 → SIBM Nagpur registration → shortlisting → GE/PI → final merit list. The official schedule lists SNAP result on 12 January 2027, SIBM registration closing on 20 January, shortlisting on 22 January, GE/PI in late January and early February, and first merit list on 18 February.

SIBM Nagpur FAQs

What SNAP percentile is required for SIBM Nagpur?

There is no guaranteed future percentile published by SIBM Nagpur on its current MBA page. Historical third-party reports commonly cite around 55 percentile as a past benchmark, while 2026 estimates vary. Students should therefore treat 55%ile as a historical reference rather than a safe target. A higher SNAP percentile is advisable because SNAP contributes 50 marks to the 100-mark final merit calculation, with GE and PI contributing the remaining 50.

Does SIBM Nagpur accept CAT for MBA admission?

The SIBM Nagpur MBA admission route is built around SNAP. The institute's published 2027-29 selection process assigns 50 marks to the SNAP score, followed by GE and PI. CAT should not be treated as a substitute for SNAP for this programme.

What is the SIBM Nagpur MBA fee for non-Nagpur domicile students?

For the 2026-28 MBA batch, the official structure lists ₹4,49,500 academic fee per installment for other-than-Nagpur domicile candidates. The first listed installment is ₹7,79,600 after adding the ₹20,000 institute deposit, ₹20,000 hostel deposit, ₹2,13,600 hostel fee and ₹76,500 mess fee. Subsequent installments and later-year hostel/mess charges must be checked against the official fee notice.

Is SIBM Nagpur MBA residential?

Yes. The MBA is a two-year full-time residential programme. This is important when comparing total cost because hostel and mess charges are part of the official fee structure.

What is the MBA intake at SIBM Nagpur?

The official MBA page lists an intake of 180 students. The institute also states that 25% of seats are reserved for Nagpur-domiciled candidates, subject to its specific domicile definition.

Is MBA-FABM suitable without an agriculture degree?

The published eligibility criteria are graduate-level criteria and do not state that an agriculture degree is mandatory. MBA-FABM is sector-focused, so students from other backgrounds should be prepared to learn agriculture, food systems and agribusiness concepts.
