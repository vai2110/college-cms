SIBM Nagpur MBA: Quick Facts

Duration

2 years, full-time residential

Intake

180 students

Selection

SNAP + GE + PI

The MBA is a four-semester residential programme. SIBM Nagpur states that specialisation courses begin from Semester I, allowing students to build domain expertise early.

SIBM Nagpur MBA Eligibility

Candidates need a graduation degree from a recognised university or Institution of National Importance. The minimum is 50% marks or equivalent grade for general candidates and 45% for SC/ST candidates. Final-year students can apply subject to fulfilling the required marks. Foreign university graduates need AIU equivalence.

SIBM Nagpur MBA Cutoff

SIBM Nagpur's current official MBA page explains the SNAP-based selection process but does not publish a guaranteed future cutoff. Historical third-party reports commonly cite about 55 percentile as a past benchmark; newer expected estimates vary. Use these only as reference points.

SIBM Nagpur MBA Admission 2027-29

Admission follows SNAP 2026, separate SIBM Nagpur registration, shortlisting and GE/PI. For 2027-29, the published final merit weightage is 50 marks SNAP, 10 GE and 40 PI.

It is a better fit for students who want a residential MBA, can compete through SNAP and want an early specialisation. Business Analytics suits candidates targeting data-led business roles; Finance suits corporate finance, banking and investment paths; Operations & SCM suits supply-chain and process careers.

SIBM Nagpur MBA FAQs

What is the SIBM Nagpur MBA cutoff?

SIBM Nagpur does not currently publish a guaranteed future cutoff on its official MBA page. Historical third-party reports commonly cite around 55 percentile. Because estimates vary and final selection includes GE and PI, students should not treat 55 percentile as a safe admission target.

What is the SIBM Nagpur MBA intake?

The official MBA page lists 180 students. The institute also reserves 25% of sanctioned intake for Nagpur-domiciled candidates, subject to its published domicile definition.

Is SIBM Nagpur MBA residential?

Yes. The programme is two years, full-time and residential. Hostel and mess charges are therefore important when calculating the actual cost of attendance.

What specialisations are offered in SIBM Nagpur MBA?

The official programme information lists Marketing, Human Resource Management, Finance, Operations and Supply Chain Management, and Business Analytics. Specialisation choice should be linked to the roles and sectors you want after MBA.

Does SIBM Nagpur accept CAT?

The published SIBM Nagpur MBA selection process is SNAP-based. For the 2027-29 batch, SNAP contributes 50 marks to final merit, followed by GE and PI. Candidates should therefore plan for SNAP rather than assuming CAT can replace it.

What is the MBA fee for other-than-Nagpur domicile students?

For 2026-28, the official structure lists ₹4,49,500 academic fee per installment for other-than-Nagpur domicile students. The first listed installment is ₹7,79,600 after adding refundable deposits and first-year hostel and mess charges shown by the institute.
