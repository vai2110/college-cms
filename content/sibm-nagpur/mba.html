SIBM Nagpur MBA Programme

The SIBM Nagpur MBA is a two-year full-time residential programme structured across four semesters and 100 academic credits. The institute states that specialisation courses begin from Semester I, allowing students to build domain expertise early.

Specialisations

Marketing

Market research, digital marketing, brand management and customer-centric strategy.

Human Resource Management

Talent acquisition, industrial relations, HR analytics and performance management.

Finance

Corporate finance, investment analysis, banking, risk management and financial modelling.

Operations & Supply Chain

Process optimisation, logistics, lean systems and sustainable supply chains.

Business Analytics

Predictive modelling, machine learning, data visualisation and business intelligence.

SIBM Nagpur MBA Eligibility

Candidates must be graduates from a recognised university/institution of national importance with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade. For SC/ST candidates, the minimum is 45%. Final-year candidates may apply subject to meeting the required marks. Foreign university graduates require an AIU equivalence certificate.

SIBM Nagpur MBA Curriculum

The 2026-28 curriculum includes core areas such as economy and markets, financial management, operations, HR, business statistics, marketing, accounting and spreadsheet modelling, alongside electives and specialisation courses. The institute also lists data-oriented learning within the programme.

Who Should Choose SIBM Nagpur MBA?

SIBM Nagpur MBA is a stronger fit for students who want a residential management programme, are comfortable with SNAP and want to specialise early. Business Analytics is particularly relevant for students targeting data-driven business roles; Finance suits candidates focused on corporate finance, banking and investment roles.
