Asia Pacific Institute of Management Delhi Overview

Asia Pacific Institute of Management (AIM), New Delhi was established in 1996 and is located at 3 & 4, G.D. Birla Marg, Institutional Area, Jasola Vihar. Its current management portfolio includes AICTE-approved two-year full-time PGDM programmes in General Management, Marketing, Banking & Financial Services and Big Data Analytics. The PGDM General programme offers dual-specialisation options.

What should a student know first? AIM's current PGDM admission accepts CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT and ATMA scores for relevant PGDM admissions, followed by institute-level selection. The published 2026–28 PGDM academic fee is ₹11.50 lakh.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee of Asia Pacific Institute of Management Delhi for PGDM?
For the 2026–28 PGDM batch, AIM's official fee structure lists ₹11.50 lakh as the total academic fee for the self-sponsored category. It is split into registration, seat confirmation and four ₹2.50 lakh academic instalments.
Which entrance exams does AIM Delhi accept?
AIM's current admission information lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT and ATMA scores for relevant PGDM admissions. Applicants should verify the accepted exam for their exact programme and intake.
Can I get admission to AIM Delhi without CAT?
Yes. CAT is not the only accepted test. AIM lists XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT and ATMA as accepted management-entrance scores for relevant PGDM admissions.
What is the eligibility for PGDM at AIM Delhi?
The current PGDM information requires a bachelor's degree from a recognised university with at least 50% marks. Final-year students can apply subject to applicable conditions, and a valid accepted management-entrance score is part of the current admission pathway.
What is the highest and average package at AIM Delhi?
AIM's current admissions pages highlight ₹35 LPA as the highest package and ₹8.75 LPA as the average. Its detailed placement page reports ₹22 LPA as the highest and ₹8.75 LPA average. Because the official pages differ on the highest figure, students should verify the underlying placement cycle.
Is Asia Pacific Institute of Management AICTE approved?
Yes. AIM's current PGDM programme pages identify its PGDM programmes as AICTE-approved. The current PGDM General page also states MBA equivalence through AIU; students should verify the relevant approval/equivalence documents for their intake.
What PGDM specialisations are available at AIM Delhi?
The current PGDM portfolio includes General Management, Marketing, Banking & Financial Services and Big Data Analytics. PGDM General provides dual-specialisation exposure across areas such as Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and Business Analytics.
