Asia Pacific Institute of Management Delhi Overview

Asia Pacific Institute of Management (AIM), New Delhi was established in 1996 and is located at 3 & 4, G.D. Birla Marg, Institutional Area, Jasola Vihar. Its current management portfolio includes AICTE-approved two-year full-time PGDM programmes in General Management, Marketing, Banking & Financial Services and Big Data Analytics. The PGDM General programme offers dual-specialisation options. citeturn0search6turn1search1

What should a student know first? AIM's current PGDM admission accepts CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT and ATMA scores for the relevant PGDM admissions, followed by institute-level selection. The published 2026–28 PGDM academic fee is ₹11.50 lakh. citeturn0search1turn1search0

Admission Process: Detailed but Crisp

Check eligibility.
AIM's current PGDM information requires a bachelor's degree from a recognised university with at least 50% marks. Final-year students can apply subject to the applicable conditions. citeturn1search1
Hold a valid accepted management-test score.
AIM lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT and ATMA for relevant PGDM admissions. The accepted test should be verified for the specific programme and intake before applying. citeturn0search1
Submit the AIM application.
Enter academic, personal and entrance-test details, select the intended PGDM pathway and submit the application through AIM's admission process.
Attend the institute-level selection stage.
Shortlisted candidates go through Group Discussion and Personal Interview. AIM says these stages assess communication, analytical thinking, leadership qualities and managerial aptitude. citeturn1search1
Receive the admission decision and pay according to the offer.
Selected candidates confirm their seat and follow the published payment schedule. The 2026–28 fee page specifies registration, seat confirmation and four subsequent academic instalments. citeturn1search0
Other admission modes: AIM's current PGDM admission information is built around recognised management-entrance scores plus institute-level selection. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee of Asia Pacific Institute of Management Delhi for PGDM?
For the 2026–28 PGDM batch, AIM's official fee structure lists ₹11.50 lakh as the total academic fee for the self-sponsored category. The amount is split into registration, seat-confirmation and four ₹2.50 lakh academic instalments. The published fee includes tuition and specified academic resources and activities; accommodation and personal expenses should be checked separately. citeturn1search0
Which entrance exams does Asia Pacific Institute of Management accept?
AIM's current admission information lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT and ATMA scores for relevant PGDM admissions. Applicants should verify the accepted exam for their exact programme and intake because admission policies can change. citeturn0search1turn0search2
Can I get admission to AIM Delhi without CAT?
Yes, CAT is not the only accepted test. AIM lists XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT and ATMA as accepted management-entrance scores for relevant PGDM admissions. This means an applicant with a valid accepted alternative score does not have to rely on CAT alone. I have not treated unverified management quota/direct guaranteed admission as an official route. citeturn0search1
What is the eligibility for PGDM at Asia Pacific Institute of Management?
The current PGDM General information requires a bachelor's degree from a recognised university with at least 50% marks. Final-year students can apply subject to the applicable conditions. A valid accepted management-entrance score is also part of the current admission pathway. citeturn1search1
What is the highest and average package at Asia Pacific Institute of Management?
AIM's current admissions pages highlight ₹35 LPA as the highest package and ₹8.75 LPA as the average. Its detailed placement page, however, reports ₹22 LPA as the highest and ₹8.75 LPA average. Because these official pages are not internally consistent on the highest figure, students should not treat ₹35 LPA as a typical outcome or as a clean year-on-year figure without checking the underlying placement cycle. citeturn1search5turn1search8
Is Asia Pacific Institute of Management AICTE approved?
Yes. AIM's current PGDM programme pages identify its PGDM programmes as AICTE-approved. The current PGDM General page also states that the programme is considered equivalent to an MBA by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). Students should verify the approval/equivalence documents for the specific intake when making a formal academic decision. citeturn1search1
What PGDM specialisations are available at AIM Delhi?
The current PGDM portfolio includes General Management, Marketing, Banking & Financial Services and Big Data Analytics. PGDM General provides dual-specialisation exposure across areas such as Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and Business Analytics. Students should select based on the career function they want to build rather than only the title of the programme. citeturn1search1turn1search2
Does AIM Delhi provide hostel facilities?
AIM provides hostel/accommodation information for students, including separate facilities for boys and girls near the campus on its current admissions material. Because availability, room type, fees and meal arrangements can change, outstation students should confirm the exact accommodation offer for their batch before treating hostel costs as part of the academic fee. citeturn1search8
